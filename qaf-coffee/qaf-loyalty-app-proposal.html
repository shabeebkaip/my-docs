Overview
One App to Build, Odoo Runs the Rest
Qaf wants customers to open a Qaf app, collect points, claim rewards and buy — the same relationship Bonat gives its merchants, but branded as Qaf and owned in-house, without paying Bonat a monthly fee per branch or renting a tablet at every till. Because Qaf already runs Odoo, only one thing needs building: the customer app. Staff award and redeem points at the Odoo POS, and head office manages rules, offers and reports from the Odoo back office — so no cashier app and no admin dashboard are needed.
About Qaf & the Starting Point

Qaf sells specialty coffee through its online store (qafcoffee.com) — roasted beans, envelopes, capsules and brewing tools — alongside café, store-supply, franchise and careers pages. Customers already have accounts and order history in the store. The loyalty system matches those customers by phone number, so their online and in-store activity earn points in one place, inside Odoo.

Odoo Back Office — Admin
Head office sets rules, sends offers and reads reports in Odoo. No separate admin dashboard to build.
Why In-House Beats a Subscription

Bonat charges a monthly subscription per branch plus a one-time fee for each till tablet — a cost that grows with every branch Qaf opens. Building the app on Odoo is a one-time build Qaf owns: the customer data, the loyalty rules and the brand stay with Qaf in a system it already runs, and adding a branch costs nothing per month. The shared marketplace Bonat runs (one app serving many businesses) is not needed for a single brand — see Assumptions.

Approach
A Real App, Not a Web Wrapper
A loyalty programme lives in an app customers open — on the home screen, with a card in their wallet and a push notification when a reward lands. A PWA (a mobile version of the website) cannot do that reliably. We recommend a native Flutter app for the customer side.
Reliable push notifications
"Reward earned", offers and order status land on the phone. On iOS, web push is limited and easily missed; native push is dependable.
Apple & Google Wallet passes
The loyalty card sits in the phone's wallet, scannable at the till — a web page cannot.
Instant QR at the till
The member's QR displays and scans in a second so the Odoo POS awards points without slowing the queue — native camera access a browser can't guarantee.
Store presence & a home-screen icon
A listing on the App Store and Google Play, discoverable and branded — not a bookmark customers forget.
The Online Store Still Has Its Place

qafcoffee.com stays live for browsing and buying products. The app links to it (and, later, sells inside the app), while the loyalty relationship — points, rewards, the wallet card and notifications — lives in the native app where it belongs.

How the Pieces Connect
The app reads and writes one Odoo back end — loyalty rules, points and rewards live in Odoo. Odoo already runs the till and the eCommerce link, so in-store and online sales both earn points natively, with no separate loyalty database to reconcile.

Scope of Work — The App We Build
Customer App
Every feature below is part of the v1 build. The app is the customer's home for the Qaf loyalty programme — earning, rewards, offers and pickup, in Arabic and English.
AreaFeatures
AccountPhone sign-up — mobile number plus SMS code, with existing store customers matched by phone; Arabic & English with full right-to-left layout and a language switch; profile for name, birthday, preferred branch and notification choices; and account deletion to erase data from the app.
LoyaltyPersonal QR code shown at the till (phone number as a back-up); points balance & history listing every earn, spend and expiry; visit & stamp progress (for example, 9 drinks and the 10th free); reward catalogue showing what is claimable now and what the next reward needs; my rewards wallet for unused rewards and coupons, each with an expiry date; tiers such as Silver and Gold with a progress bar; and expiry reminders before points or rewards lapse.
OffersOffers & coupons — current promotions with a code or QR to redeem; surprise gifts that Qaf sends, with an alert and the gift shown in the wallet; and referral, inviting a friend so both earn points after the friend's first purchase.
GiftingGift cards — buy a card, send it to a friend with a message, and redeem it in store.
VisitBranch finder with list, map, opening hours and directions; pickup pre-order to pick items, pay in the app and collect at the branch; and order history & reorder for past pickup orders with one-tap reorder.
ShopLink to the online store, opening qafcoffee.com for beans, capsules and tools; and in-app shopping to browse and buy store products inside the app.
EngagePush & inbox for reward-earned, offers and order status; ratings & feedback to rate a visit or order and leave a comment; contact Qaf by WhatsApp, call or message; and wallet card to add the loyalty card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.
PaymentPrepaid balance — top up and pay with app balance.
