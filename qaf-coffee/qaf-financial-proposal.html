How the Two Options Work

Option 1 — Keep Salla, connect it to Odoo. Two-way sync between Salla and Odoo, plus the checkout fixes (pickup address, pricelist pricing, category rules) built directly into Salla. Salla subscription stays; bounded by what Salla's platform allows.

Option 2 — Move the storefront onto Odoo. Full website rebuild on Odoo eCommerce, migrated off Salla. No integration layer, no sync — one database for storefront, stock and accounting.

#Task & Scope Description
01Salla ↔ Odoo two-way syncProducts, prices and stock synced from Odoo to Salla; orders and customers synced from Salla to Odoo.

08Warehouse pickup workflowPick/pack confirmation, stock deduction and pickup-ready notification from the central warehouse.
09Pricelist configurationPickup vs. delivery pricelists built and mapped so checkout price always reflects the correct fulfillment method.
10Testing & go-live supportEnd-to-end order testing across both fulfillment paths and all four couriers, plus go-live support and admin walkthrough.
Why This Approach

Items 01–03 are the connector — the same pattern we use for any Salla-to-Odoo integration. Items 04–06 are your specific checkout customizations, requiring custom development through Salla's App/Partner tools. The exact behavior was only discussed by voice call so far — we'll confirm it in writing during discovery week 1.
