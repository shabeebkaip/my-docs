Terms & Conditions
General Terms
  • Scope of Work: Covers the modules and tasks listed on page 3. Features outside that scope require a separate written agreement and may incur additional charges.
  • Client Responsibilities: Qaf names one project owner who approves each phase within 5 working days, and provides product lists, recipes, yields, supplier lists, opening balances, and Salla/ZATCA credentials in our templates.
  • Third-Party Costs: Odoo per-user licence, domain, SSL, payment gateway, courier and SMS/notification costs are not included and are Qaf's responsibility.
  • Intellectual Property: Upon full payment, all custom code and configuration become Qaf's exclusive property. Odoo platform code remains subject to its own licence.
  • Revisions & Warranty: Up to two (2) revision rounds per phase. 30 days of complimentary bug-fix warranty post go-live; extended retainers available on request.
  • Confidentiality & Law: Both parties keep this proposal confidential. Governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Valid for 30 days from issuance.

Assumptions & Exclusions
Key Project Assumptions
  • Migrating off the current Odoo.sh trial database (auto-deletes 17 October 2026) to a permanent production instance is included and is a hard dependency for go-live.
  • Qaf holds active accounts with SPL, SMSA, DHL and RedBox with API access; account setup itself is not included. Domain and hosting are client-provided.
  • Bonat loyalty integration and thebasebev.com are not included in this price — scoped and quoted separately if wanted.
  • This scope was built from the live demo and a backend review, and will be re-verified once fresh Odoo backend credentials are confirmed.
Alternative Path — Not Priced Here

Qaf's team also asked about a lighter alternative: keeping Salla as the storefront and connecting it to Odoo (two-way sync, checkout customization, courier integration) instead of the full implementation above. That path's pricing is still under internal review and is not included in the SAR 70,000 total — we can send a separate figure on request.
