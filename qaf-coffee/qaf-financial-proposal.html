Alternative Path
Keep Salla, Connect It to Odoo
Our recommendation, and what Qaf is targeting, is the full Odoo implementation priced above. Your team also asked about a lighter alternative — keeping Salla as the storefront and connecting it to Odoo. Alternative

Customize Salla + Integrate with Odoo

Pricing pendingUnder internal review — not final
#Task & Scope Description
01Salla ↔ Odoo two-way syncProducts, prices and stock synced from Odoo to Salla; orders and customers synced from Salla to Odoo.
02Automatic order, delivery & invoice creationSalla orders create Odoo sales orders, deliveries and ZATCA invoices automatically.
03Payment reconciliationSalla payouts reconciled in Odoo Accounting.
04Store-pickup checkout customizationRemove the delivery-address requirement for "Pickup in Store" orders and apply pricelist-based pricing instead of standard delivery pricing.
05Category & volume fulfillment rulesRestrict selected categories (e.g. drinks) to pickup-only; route orders to pickup vs. drop-off by order volume.
06Courier integration — SPL, SMSA, DHL, RedBoxDelivery carrier setup in Odoo Inventory/Delivery. SPL reuses an integration Code-ox already has built; SMSA, DHL and RedBox are net-new.
07Warehouse pickup workflow & pricelistsPick/pack confirmation, stock deduction, pickup-ready notification; pickup vs. delivery pricelists mapped to checkout.
08Testing & go-live supportEnd-to-end order testing across both fulfillment paths and all four couriers, plus go-live support.
Why This Stays Secondary

This path keeps Salla's subscription and platform limits — bounded by what Salla's App/Partner API allows for checkout customization. The full Odoo path removes that ceiling and the ongoing integration to maintain, which is why it's our lead recommendation. If Qaf's team wants this alternative priced formally, we'll finalize and send a separate figure.
