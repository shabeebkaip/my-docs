Option 1
Scope of Work — Customize Salla + Integrate with Odoo
Core — Keep Salla, Connect It to Odoo
  • Two-way sync: products, prices and stock from Odoo to Salla; orders and customers from Salla to Odoo
  • Salla orders create Odoo sales orders, deliveries and ZATCA invoices automatically
  • Payment reconciliation of Salla payouts in Odoo Accounting
  • Requires Odoo.sh hosting for the custom connector; Salla subscription stays and is billed separately by Salla
Salla Checkout Customization — Your Requirements
  • Store-pickup checkout: remove the delivery-address requirement for "Pickup in Store" orders and apply pricelist-based pricing instead of standard delivery pricing
  • Category-level fulfillment rules: restrict selected categories (e.g. drinks) to pickup-only, hidden from home delivery
  • Volume-based fulfillment logic: configurable rule routing orders to pickup vs. drop-off by order volume (threshold confirmed with your team during discovery)
  • Courier integration — SPL, SMSA, DHL, RedBox — in Odoo Inventory/Delivery, mapped to the correct fulfillment and pricelist rules. SPL reuses an integration Code-ox already has built; SMSA, DHL and RedBox are net-new. All four need API credentials from your carrier accounts
  • Warehouse pickup workflow from the central warehouse: pick/pack confirmation, stock deduction, pickup-ready notification
  • Pricelist configuration so checkout price always reflects the correct fulfillment method automatically
  • End-to-end testing across both fulfillment paths and all four couriers, plus go-live support and admin walkthrough
Depends On Salla's Platform

The checkout customization items above require custom development through Salla's App/Partner tools. The specifics were only discussed by voice call so far — we'll confirm the exact behavior in writing during discovery week 1. If Salla's platform does not expose an API or app hook for something, we'll flag it and propose the closest achievable alternative before development starts — see assumptions on page 9.

Option 2
Scope of Work — Fully Native Odoo Website

We rebuild qafcoffee.com on Odoo eCommerce so online orders, stock and invoices live in one place, building on the demo storefront already in place (page 3).

1. Design and build
  • Custom Arabic (right-to-left) and English theme in Qaf branding, mobile first
  • Home page: hero banner, new releases, best sellers, harvest collections, capsules, reviews, branch finder
  • Header, mega menu and footer matching the current Salla menu
  • Replace all placeholder content (contact details, footer, empty sections)
2. Pages (about 12)
  • About us, Branch locations with Google Maps, Wholesale, Franchise, Careers, Contact
  • FAQs, Terms, Refund and exchange policy, Privacy, Shipping and delivery
3. Shop
  • Categories: Coffee Packages, Saudi Harvests, Espresso Harvest, Filter Harvest, 1 kg crops, Drip Bags, Capsules, Coffee Tools
  • Product pages with origin, process, roast level, tasting notes, altitude, brew guide (Arabic and English)
  • Variants for bag size (250 g / 500 g / 1 kg) and grind type — set optional, not required, at checkout
  • Filters by origin, process, roast level, price
  • Wishlist, compare, related products, "New" and "Best seller" badges
  • Remove café drinks (Flat White, V60) from the online shop unless Qaf wants pickup ordering
4. Checkout and payments
  • Payment gateway: Mada, Visa/Mastercard, Apple Pay (via HyperPay, Moyasar, Tap or PayTabs — Qaf's choice); not yet configured on the demo
  • Buy-now-pay-later: Tabby and/or Tamara
  • Cash on delivery (keep, with optional fee)
  • Guest checkout, saved addresses, Saudi national address fields
  • Pickup-in-store fixed to skip the address step and apply the branch pricelist instead of standard pricing
5. Shipping
  • Delivery rates by city/zone, free-shipping threshold
  • Click and collect from any branch
  • Courier integration — SPL, SMSA, DHL, RedBox — as native Odoo delivery carriers. SPL reuses an integration Code-ox already has built; SMSA, DHL and RedBox are net-new. All four need API credentials from your carrier accounts
  • Category-level rule so selected categories (e.g. drinks) are pickup-only; volume-based routing to pickup vs. delivery (threshold confirmed with your team)
6. Customer features
  • Account portal: orders, invoices, reorder, tracking
  • Discount codes, promotions, gift cards
  • Loyalty points shared with the cafés (Bonat integration or Odoo Loyalty — decision needed, not priced in below)
  • Optional: coffee subscriptions (monthly bean delivery) with Odoo Subscriptions
7. Migration from Salla
  • Products, images, descriptions (both languages), categories
  • Customers (with consent) and open orders
  • 301 redirects from all Salla URLs so Google rankings are kept
8. SEO, tracking and launch
  • Meta titles and descriptions, sitemap, Google Search Console
  • Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, Snap and TikTok pixels
  • Migrate off the current Odoo.sh trial database (auto-deletes 17 October 2026, not backed up) onto a permanent, backed-up production instance
  • Domain switch, SSL, go-live checklist
9. Housekeeping
  • Remove the default "My Website" and the unused demo site from the database
  • thebasebev.com (a separate site found in the same database): out of scope unless Qaf confirms it is a group brand
Why This Is Different

Because the storefront and Odoo ERP share one database, everything above ships without an integration layer to build, maintain or debug. Full comparison and the annual-cost breakdown are on page 8.

The Honest Answer

There is no separate "Website app fee." Odoo's own pricing page states both plans "include unlimited support, hosting and maintenance" with "no hidden costs" — the Website and eCommerce apps are included in whatever per-user plan you're already paying for your ERP, at your existing 36 users. Because Qaf already runs Odoo, going live with the Odoo Website adds zero new licence cost above the figures shown — unless you add new user seats (e.g. a dedicated web/content admin), billed at the same per-user rate. Source: odoo.com/pricing-plan, fetched 24 September 2026; SAR converted at a fixed 3.75 peg — verify current rates and your actual plan tier before signing, as Odoo revises pricing periodically.
