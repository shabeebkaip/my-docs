1Project overview

Code-Ox will take QAF Roasteries from a partly configured Odoo 19 demo to one live system that runs the roastery, the cafés, the online store, wholesale and the books. The work is split into seven phases so QAF can approve and pay by phase.

LineWhat it is todayWhere it will run
RoasteryGreen beans roasted and packed in Al-KhobarOdoo Manufacturing + Inventory
Cafés7 branches listed online (Khobar 3, Riyadh 3, Dammam 1), about 10 in total incl. Jubail and hospital sitesOdoo Point of Sale (Restaurant)
Online storeqafcoffee.com on Salla, Arabic/EnglishOdoo eCommerce (or Salla connected to Odoo, see option B)
Wholesale (B2B)Orders by email and WhatsAppOdoo Sales + B2B customer portal
Franchise5-year programme, 9% royaltyOdoo multi-company + royalty billing
FinanceNeeds one P&L with margin per branch and channelOdoo Accounting + ZATCA Phase 2

Goal: from bean to books in one system — true cost per cup and per pack, live stock, profit per branch, and ZATCA-compliant invoices.

3Website and eCommerce scope

We rebuild qafcoffee.com on Odoo eCommerce so online orders, stock and invoices live in one place. If QAF prefers to keep Salla, option B replaces this section with a two-way connector.

Option ARecommended — move qafcoffee.com from Salla to Odoo

1. Design and build

  • Custom Arabic (right-to-left) and English theme in QAF branding, mobile first
  • Home page: hero banner, new releases, best sellers, harvest collections, capsules, reviews, branch finder
  • Header, mega menu and footer matching the current Salla menu
  • Replace all placeholder content (contact details, footer, empty sections)

2. Pages (about 12)

  • About us, Branch locations with Google Maps, Wholesale, Franchise, Careers, Contact
  • FAQs, Terms, Refund and exchange policy, Privacy, Shipping and delivery

3. Shop

  • Categories: Coffee Packages, Saudi Harvests, Espresso Harvest, Filter Harvest, 1 kg crops, Drip Bags, Capsules, Coffee Tools
  • Product pages with origin, process, roast level, tasting notes, altitude, brew guide (Arabic and English)
  • Variants for bag size (250 g / 500 g / 1 kg) and grind type
  • Filters by origin, process, roast level, price
  • Wishlist, compare, related products, "New" and "Best seller" badges
  • Remove café drinks (Flat White, V60) from the online shop unless QAF wants pickup ordering

4. Checkout and payments

  • Payment gateway: Mada, Visa/Mastercard, Apple Pay (via HyperPay, Moyasar, Tap or PayTabs, QAF's choice)
  • Buy-now-pay-later: Tabby and/or Tamara
  • Cash on delivery (keep, with optional fee)
  • Guest checkout, saved addresses, Saudi national address fields

5. Shipping

  • Delivery rates by city/zone, free-shipping threshold
  • Click and collect from any branch
  • Courier integration (Aramex or SMSA; one included, others as change request)

6. Customer features

  • Account portal: orders, invoices, reorder, tracking
  • Discount codes, promotions, gift cards
  • Loyalty points shared with the cafés (see Bonat decision in section 5)
  • Optional: coffee subscriptions (monthly bean delivery) with Odoo Subscriptions

7. Migration from Salla

  • Products, images, descriptions (both languages), categories
  • Customers (with consent) and open orders
  • 301 redirects from all Salla URLs so Google rankings are kept

8. SEO, tracking and launch

  • Meta titles and descriptions, sitemap, Google Search Console
  • Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager (existing GTM-TGFC6FV), Meta Pixel, Snap and TikTok pixels
  • Domain switch, SSL, go-live checklist

9. Housekeeping

  • Remove the default "My Website" and the unused demo site
  • thebasebev.com (28 pages found in the database): out of scope unless QAF confirms it is a group brand
Option BKeep Salla, connect it to Odoo
  • Two-way sync: products, prices and stock from Odoo to Salla; orders and customers from Salla to Odoo
  • Salla orders create Odoo sales orders, deliveries and ZATCA invoices automatically
  • Payment reconciliation of Salla payouts in Odoo Accounting
  • Needs Odoo.sh hosting (custom connector); Salla subscription stays

4.1 Company, users and security

  • Company profile: legal name, VAT number, CR, national address, logo (all empty today)
  • Multi-company setup for franchise entities (if franchisees use Odoo)
  • User roles: owner, finance, roastery, warehouse, branch manager, barista, online store, HR
  • Branch-level access so each manager sees only their branch

4.2 Purchase (new)

  • Green bean suppliers (international) and local suppliers (cups, milk, packaging)
  • Purchase orders in USD/EUR with landed costs (freight, customs, clearance)
  • Reordering rules for packaging and café consumables
  • Vendor bills matched to receipts

4.3 Manufacturing — roastery (new)

  • Bill of materials per roast profile: green kg → roasted kg with 15–20% weight loss built in
  • Packing bill of materials: roasted kg → 250 g / 500 g / 1 kg bags, drip bags, capsules (incl. bags, valves, labels)
  • Lot number per roast batch, roast date and best-before date on each bag
  • Work centres: roaster(s), packing line; roaster time and labour cost in product cost
  • Actual vs expected yield report per batch
  • Optional: Quality checks (cupping score, colour) per batch

4.4 Inventory and warehouses

  • Locations: green bean store, roastery, finished goods, central warehouse, one location per branch
  • Unit chain: kg green → kg roasted → bags → grams per drink
  • Branch replenishment: branch requests → central warehouse transfer → receipt at branch
  • Lot tracking and expiry (FIFO/FEFO)
  • Barcode app for receiving, transfers and stock counts
  • Monthly stock count per branch with variance report
  • Stock valuation: automated, average cost

4.5 Point of Sale — cafés

  • Set up about 10 branches (Nada and ARAMCO exist; add Khobar Olaya, Shark Khobar, Agdan Walk, Riyadh Olaya, Shark Riyadh, Boulevard World, Jubail 24h, hospital site; final list from QAF)
  • Rebuild the drink menu with combos and modifiers (espresso bean, extra shot, milk type, cup type, syrups) instead of 168 variants per drink
  • Drink recipes: each drink deducts beans (e.g. 18 g), milk, cup and lid from branch stock
  • Branch-specific prices and menus; happy hour and staff discounts
  • Payment terminals: Mada/card integration (e.g. Geidea, Nearpay or Surepay — to confirm per branch)
  • Kitchen/bar display or order printer; Arabic/English receipts with ZATCA QR
  • QR self-ordering at tables (already enabled on demo) and online pickup orders
  • Delivery apps (HungerStation, Jahez, Keeta) — via aggregator, as option
  • Clean up 9 unused floor plans; one floor plan per dine-in branch
  • Offline mode and shift close with cash control

4.6 Sales and wholesale B2B

  • Wholesale pricelists by tier (café, restaurant, e-store) and volume
  • Quotation → order → delivery → invoice flow
  • B2B customer portal: wholesale customers log in, see their prices, reorder, download invoices
  • Credit limits and payment terms
  • Private-label orders (if offered)

4.7 Franchise management

  • Franchisee records, contract dates (5 years), territories
  • Franchisee ordering via portal at franchise prices
  • Monthly 9% royalty invoice based on franchisee sales (custom)
  • Intercompany rules if franchise entities run inside the same Odoo

4.8 Accounting and finance

  • Review KSA chart of accounts; map to QAF's current accounts
  • Analytic accounts per branch and per channel (café, online, wholesale, franchise)
  • Bank accounts and bank statement sync or import
  • VAT return, Zakat and withholding tax reports
  • Fixed assets (roasters, espresso machines, fit-outs) with depreciation
  • IFRS 16 leases for branch rent (journal exists in demo)
  • Budgets per branch; month-end close checklist
  • Dashboards: sales, margin per cup and per bag, branch P&L

4.9 HR and payroll (new)

  • Employee records, contracts, Iqama/passport expiry reminders
  • Shift planning for baristas across branches, attendance
  • Saudi payroll: GOSI, WPS/Mudad file, end-of-service benefit, leave
  • Recruitment and Online Jobs (installed) linked to the Careers page
  • Employees self-service: leave requests, payslips

4.10 Loyalty and marketing

  • Loyalty points and rewards shared between cafés and online store (Odoo Loyalty) or Bonat integration (see section 5)
  • Gift cards and e-wallet
  • Email and SMS campaigns to customers

5ZATCA, integrations and data migration

ZATCA Phase 2 is the one item that must be live on day one; it is not installed in the demo today.

5.1 ZATCA Phase 2 (e-invoicing)

  • Install and configure Odoo's Saudi e-invoicing module
  • Onboard each device/branch with ZATCA (CSID via Fatoora portal OTP) — sandbox first, then production
  • Simplified invoices (B2C) from every POS and the online store, reported within 24 hours
  • Standard tax invoices (B2B) for wholesale and franchise, cleared before sending
  • Credit notes and returns
  • Bilingual Arabic/English invoice and receipt layouts with QR code
  • Error monitoring and resubmission process
