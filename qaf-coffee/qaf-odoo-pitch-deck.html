02
The honest starting point

A coffee business runs on flows Odoo doesn't model on day one.

You're already an Odoo customer — that's the right foundation, and a real head start. But out of the box, Odoo doesn't know that a kilo of green coffee becomes 800 grams of roast, or that an 8-gram dose leaves the counter with every espresso. Those rules have to be built in. Until they are, the numbers drift.

None of what follows is a criticism of anyone's earlier work. It's the normal set of maturing steps every roastery-plus-café group goes through as it scales on Odoo. We'd rather name them openly than pretend they don't exist.

03
Worth checking together

Where value quietly leaks in a coffee Odoo.

Nine places a roastery-and-café group typically outgrows a first Odoo setup. We'll confirm which apply to you in discovery — some may already be solved.

Roasting not modelled

Green → roasted loses 15–20% to moisture. Without a bill of materials with yield and scrap, and a lot per roast batch, stock value and cost-of-goods are wrong at the root.

Biggest technical gap

The unit-of-measure chain

kg green → kg roasted → 250g / 500g / 1kg packs → an 8g dose at the counter. If units are kept flat, everything downstream miscounts.

POS ↔ back office

Ten counters selling all day. Sales, stock and cost should flow into Odoo live — not as a manual daily journal entry with stock reconciled by hand.

Salla ↔ Odoo integration

Your store runs on Salla — keep it. A proper two-way connector syncs products, prices, orders, stock and customers between Salla and Odoo, so online orders drop straight into the books and stock stays true. Without it, orders get re-keyed and the two never agree.

Multi-location discipline

Roastery + central warehouse + ~10 branches. Without clean internal transfers you get negative stock; without a branch analytic you get no per-branch profit.

ZATCA Phase 2

Simplified QR invoices from the counter, cleared B2B invoices for wholesale. A common place for a setup to stall or run outside Odoo.

Arabic receipts & invoices

Bilingual, right-to-left receipts and tax invoices that render correctly every time — usually needs custom report templates.

KSA payroll

Shift-based baristas across branches, with GOSI, WPS / Mudad and end-of-service. Not something Odoo handles out of the box for Saudi.

Franchise & intercompany

Royalty billing, franchisee ordering and separate legal entities that still roll up into one group view.
