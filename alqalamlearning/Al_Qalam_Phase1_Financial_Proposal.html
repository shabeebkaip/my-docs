Terms & Conditions
General Terms
  • Scope of Work: This proposal covers the 16 Phase 1 feature modules listed in Section 1, across mobile app, tablet, and desktop web. Any feature not listed there is out of scope for this price and belongs to Phase 2.
  • Scope Changes: Small additions during the project are welcome and will be batched into a fortnightly review. Anything that takes under 1 day is absorbed at no cost. Anything larger is quickly estimated and either scheduled without slipping the 12-week deadline, or queued into Phase 2.
  • Client Responsibilities: Al Qalam Learning agrees to provide branding assets, content (EN & Malayalam), and timely feedback within agreed deadlines. Delays in providing these may impact the delivery timeline.
  • Third-Party Costs: App Store & Google Play developer accounts, Supabase hosting plan, push notification service fees, and domain/hosting for the web portals are not included and are the client's responsibility.
  • Intellectual Property: Upon receipt of full payment, all custom code and design deliverables become the exclusive property of Al Qalam Learning. Third-party libraries remain subject to their respective licences.
  • Revisions: The design phase (Weeks 1–4) includes up to two (2) rounds of revisions. Additional revision rounds will be charged separately at an agreed rate.
  • Warranty: Code-ox Technologies provides 30 days of complimentary bug-fix warranty post-launch, covering all delivered Phase 1 modules.
  • Confidentiality: Both parties agree to keep the contents of this proposal confidential and not disclose them to any third party without prior written consent.
