1Cutting licence cost — and yes, it's legal

You raised the big one: today you pay a user licence per person, which gets expensive fast. And you asked the right question — is the employee / portal approach actually legal? The clear answer: yes, it is perfectly legal. Not licensing every head is Odoo's own licensing model — portal and external users are free by design, and an employee record isn't a user at all. This is exactly how Odoo intends it to work.

✓

Straight answer for your team

The employee-and-portal concept is a standard, fully-licensed Odoo model — not a loophole. Odoo bills only for people who log into the backend to create and edit documents. Everyone else can be an employee record or a free portal user. Nothing to worry about on the legal side.

✓ Where you save — all 100% legitimate

✓
Employees ≠ Users. An employee record has no login and costs nothing. Your entire workforce can sit as employee records with zero licences.
✓
Shop-floor kiosk mode. Attendance and production steps run through a shared kiosk — no per-worker login, no licence.
✓
Free portal for customers, suppliers and staff self-service views — unlimited, no cost.
✓
Licence only real operators — the few who actually create and edit documents in the backend (planning, purchasing, inventory, accounts, HR).

◑ One practical thing to know

i
A portal user is free but limited — it's for viewing your own documents, not for running backend apps. That's a functional point, not a legal one.
i
So anyone who needs to actively work inside Inventory, Manufacturing, Payroll or Appraisals gets a normal licensed user. We simply count those people accurately.
i
Result: you pay for a handful of real operators instead of everyone — legal, clean, and a big drop from where you are today.
◎

Our recommendation — the licence split

We map your headcount into three buckets: employee records (free) for most of the shop floor, kiosk for attendance/production, and paid licences only for the handful who truly transact. You get the cost drop you're after, fully within Odoo's terms.

Final per-user pricing is confirmed against the live Odoo KSA price list at quotation time — list prices move and carry promo/annual discounts, so we won't quote a hard SAR figure here. As your Silver Partner, we secure partner pricing on your behalf.

4SIRI & the Future Factories grant

SIRI (Smart Industry Readiness Index) is the official framework the Ministry of Industry & Mineral Resources (MIM) uses to measure how digitally mature a factory is — the only such index endorsed by the World Economic Forum, adopted through INCIT and NIDLP. It gates the Future Factories Program (launched 20 July 2022), whose goal is to move 4,000 Saudi factories to automated, ERP-integrated manufacturing. Every licensed factory qualifies; as a 2019 plant, ARP sits in the existing-factory track.

The target: SIRI Band 2 — and Odoo delivers it

Band 2 is where a factory moves from paper to integrated digital visibility. It needs: an ERP running procurement, inventory, production orders and finance in one place; digital work instructions on the floor; quality records linked to production batches; basic sensors/network with cybersecurity; and documented training + a written transformation plan. Odoo (MRP, Inventory, Purchase, Quality, ZATCA Phase 2) is exactly that ERP layer.

🏗

Why it matters for ARP specifically

ARP already supplies NEOM, Red Sea Global, ROSHN, SABIC, King Salman International Airport and Saudi Water Authority — mega-projects whose primes increasingly require digitally-capable, traceable suppliers. A SIRI rating becomes a credibility signal alongside your SASO, ARAMCO, CE, ASTM and CSA compliance — and the grant is a genuinely underused funding source most SMEs miss.
