Context
Understanding of ARP

Alternative Rebar Plant, founded 2019 in Dammam 3rd Industrial City, manufactures GFRP (glass-fibre reinforced polymer) rebar using FRT Canada technology. ARP is a registered/approved vendor to Aramco, NEOM, Red Sea Global, ROSHN, King Salman International Airport, Saudi Water Authority and SABIC, with SASO, ARAMCO, CE, ASTM and CSA compliance — a supplier operating at mega-project standard.

An Odoo subscription is already in place, but from our discussions the system is under-used and not fully integrated — a normal, maturing stage for a plant that scaled quickly. The opportunity now is not to start over, but to re-implement on solid foundations: model manufacturing properly, make inventory trustworthy, connect the modules, and layer on the HR, compliance and integration pieces the business has grown into.

▸What ARP makes
GFRP rebar from resin and glass fibre — a real manufacturing flow (raw material → work orders → finished goods) Odoo must model with BOMs, routings, yield and batch traceability.
▸What ARP needs from Odoo
Trusted stock and cost, quality tied to batches, KSA-compliant invoicing and payroll, department visibility, and a licence model that fits a large workforce without a seat per person.

Cost Model
Licensing Model & Cost

You pay a user licence per person today, which scales badly. The saving is real, and it is fully legal — it is Odoo's own model: Odoo bills only for people who log into the backend to create and edit documents. Everyone else is free.

Straight answer

The employee-and-portal concept is a standard, fully-licensed Odoo model — not a loophole. Odoo only bills people who log into the backend; a portal user is free but limited (viewing, not running apps), so anyone who actively works inside Inventory, Manufacturing, Payroll or Appraisals gets a normal licensed user. Nothing to worry about on the legal side.

◎
Our recommendation
The licence split

We map your headcount into three buckets — most of the plant carries no licence at all, and you pay only for the handful who genuinely work inside the backend:

BucketWho it covers at ARPCost
Employee recordNo login — held as a record, managed by a supervisorProduction-line workers, packers, loaders, drivers, general labour — the bulk of the workforce.Free
Kiosk (shared)One shared terminal, no per-worker loginShop-floor attendance clock-in and work-order steps done at a shared screen on the line.Free
PortalExternal, view-only self-serviceCustomers & suppliers viewing their own orders, invoices and delivery status.Free
Internal licenceCreates / edits backend documentsThe few who truly transact: production planner, inventory controller, buyer, QA/QC engineer, accountant, HR/payroll officer, and managers.Paid

Result: instead of a licence per head, ARP pays for a small core of operators — often a large drop from the current bill. The exact licensed-user count is confirmed by headcount in the audit.
