Cost Model

4Licensing model & cost — and yes, it's legal

You pay a user licence per person today, which scales badly. The saving is real, and it is fully legal — it is Odoo's own model: Odoo bills only for people who log into the backend to create and edit documents. Everyone else is free.

✓

Where you save — 100% legitimate

Employees ≠ Users: an employee record has no login and costs nothing. Kiosk mode for attendance/production needs no per-worker login. Free portal for customers & suppliers. Licence only the handful who truly transact.

◑

One practical point

A portal user is free but limited — for viewing, not running backend apps. So anyone actively working inside Inventory, Manufacturing, Payroll or Appraisals gets a normal licensed user. We simply count those people accurately — legal and clean.

◎

Our recommendation — the licence split

We map your headcount into three buckets: employee records (free) for most of the shop floor, kiosk for attendance/production, and paid licences only for real operators. You get the cost drop, fully within Odoo's terms — the exact licensed-user count is delivered in the audit.

Final per-user pricing is confirmed against the live Odoo KSA price list at quotation time. As your Silver Partner, we secure partner pricing on your behalf.

Government Support · Vision 2030

8SIRI & Future Factories funding

SIRI (Smart Industry Readiness Index) is the framework the Ministry of Industry & Mineral Resources uses to rate factory digital maturity — the only such index endorsed by the World Economic Forum. It gates the Future Factories Program, whose grants co-fund exactly the kind of ERP transformation in this proposal. As a 2019 plant, ARP is in the existing-factory track; the practical target is Band 2, which an integrated Odoo directly delivers.
