One connected ecosystem around your CRM.

Telephony — how calls reach the voice agent

Your phone line. Your number. The voice agent on the other end.

The voice agent plugs into the phone setup Madeline already has. One number on your SIP trunk — from whichever telecom provider you use, directly or through your PBX — is pointed at the voice agent. Every call in or out runs on it, under Madeline’s caller ID.

01 · The number

A dedicated number on your SIP trunk — with any provider — is assigned to the voice agent. Customers see Madeline’s caller ID, and anyone who calls that number back reaches the voice agent — 24/7.

02 · Two ways to connect

Direct: your telecom provider routes the trunk number to the voice agent.
Through your PBX: your IT adds one SIP route from the PBX to the voice agent. Existing lines stay untouched.

03 · Back to a human

When a person is needed, the voice agent transfers the live call through your PBX to a collector’s extension — with the customer file already on screen.

From Madeline: SIP trunk or PBX access, the number(s), simultaneous-call capacity and caller-ID approval. Code-Ox handles the configuration and testing.

Language & voice

Speaks the way your customers do.

The voice agent detects the customer's language and dialect from the first reply and adapts. A customer can switch mid-call — "English please" — and the agent follows without missing a beat.

Why it sounds human
  • Low latency. Streaming speech-to-text, a fast reasoning model and streaming text-to-speech so replies land in under a second.
  • Turn-taking. Handles interruptions and back-channels ("aywa", "mm-hm") instead of talking over the customer.
  • Trained on your material. Your scripts, FAQ, call recordings and negotiation rules shape the persona — not a generic bot.
  • Numbers done right. Amounts, dates and IDs read naturally in Arabic, and repeated back for confirmation.

Human hand-off

When a person is needed, they arrive already briefed.

The voice agent transfers the live call to an available collector and pushes a screen-pop with everything that happened so far. The customer never repeats themselves; the agent never starts cold.

  • Triggers: customer asks for a human · disputes information · complex case · escalation rule fires · AI confidence drops.

  • Context travels: verified identity, amount discussed, customer's stated reason, sentiment, and the transcript so far.
