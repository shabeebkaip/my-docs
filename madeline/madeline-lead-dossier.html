1 · Snapshot — what Madeline does

Madeline positions itself as "your strategic partner in recovering your financial rights." In plain terms: it is a third-party collection agency. Creditors (mostly banks and insurers) hand over portfolios of overdue debts, and Madeline's team chases repayment by phone, field visits and — where needed — legal action, then reports results back to the client.

SERVICE LINE 01

Companies & Individuals

Recovery of outstanding debts and overdue payments for companies, institutions and individual creditors.

SERVICE LINE 02

Banks

Collection of overdue loans, credit-card balances, financing defaults and bounced cheques on behalf of banks; follow-up with individual and corporate debtors to settle.

SERVICE LINE 03

International Collection

Cross-border recovery for clients with foreign receivables or debtors outside the Kingdom.

How the work is delivered (from their FAQ)

  • Debt analysis & customised recovery plans per portfolio/client.
  • Debt follow-up via phone and field visits — i.e. a call-centre team plus field collectors.
  • Legal representation in debt-recovery cases (they describe an in-house legal team).
  • Regular performance and progress reports to clients.

Who they serve

Banks — loans, credit cards, financing Insurance companies (per X job posts) Corporations & institutions Individual clients

How they describe themselves

  • Highly experienced team in the Saudi market; customised solutions per client.
  • "Strong commitment to data security and confidentiality."
  • "Fast execution with measurable results"; "professional and ethical approach."
  • Claims to be fully licensed, registered and compliant with all Saudi regulatory authorities verify.

AI layer — AIKA & AIWA differentiator

  • AIKA (voice): automated first-touch and reminder calls across large portfolios (due-date reminders, PTP confirmation, payment-link SMS after call), freeing human collectors for negotiation. Arabic dialect handling matters here.
  • AIWA (WhatsApp): two-way reminders, settlement offers, instalment-plan acceptance, document collection, payment links; full log back into the case record.
  • Debtor segmentation / propensity-to-pay scoring to prioritise the floor's calling list.
  • Compliance note: debt-collection communications to individuals in KSA are governed by SAMA conduct rules (calling hours, frequency, tone, consent). Automation must be built with these guardrails — position this as a strength, not a risk. verify current SAMA rules

Growth — digital marketing later

  • B2B positioning towards banks, insurers, BNPL/fintech lenders, telecoms and real-estate developers; LinkedIn presence (none found), Arabic content, Google Business Profile.

Regulatory context to confirm before pitching: SAMA has been tightening rules on debt-collection activity for financial institutions and third-party agencies (conduct rules, and licensing/approval requirements for agencies acting for SAMA-regulated lenders). Madeline's "fully licensed" claim and which regulator covers them should be confirmed in discovery — it also tells us how much compliance tooling (call recording, audit trails, consent logs) the system must include.

5 · Discovery questions for the first call

Business

  • How many active client portfolios, and from which segments (banks / insurance / corporate)?
  • Number of accounts under management and monthly recovery volume?
  • Fee model — commission on recovery, fixed, or hybrid?
  • Headcount split: phone collectors / field / legal / admin?
  • Which regulator licenses them, and what reporting does it require?

Systems & process

  • What do they use today to track cases — spreadsheets, a collections tool, a CRM?
  • How do client files arrive (Excel, SFTP, API) and how are reports sent back?
  • Telephony: which dialler/PBX, is call recording in place?
  • How are field visits assigned and verified?
  • How are collector commissions calculated and paid?
  • Accounting / payroll system in use? (Odoo swap-in opportunity)

Digital

  • Who built the current website, and who owns it internally?
  • Do clients ever ask for a portal or live status? Do debtors ask to pay online?
  • Any WhatsApp/SMS use today? Official WhatsApp Business account?

Decision

  • Who owns the decision — GM/owner, Collection Manager, or Marketing Director (Shahad Anaz, per ContactOut)?
  • Budget cycle and any current RFP/vendor conversations?
  • Pain they would rank #1 today: winning new bank clients, recovery rates, staff turnover, or reporting load?
