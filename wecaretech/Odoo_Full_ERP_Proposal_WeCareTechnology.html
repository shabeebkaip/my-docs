Three rules from Odoo's Enterprise agreement that shape these options
  • The portal is for outside people only. Odoo's agreement exempts only "accounts used by external people … through the portal". Staff cannot be moved onto free portal accounts to avoid licences.
  • Every employee record counts. The agreement treats "any active employee record … not linked to an active non-portal user account" as a Light User. So once 40 staff are in Odoo's Employees app, Odoo bills at least 40 users.
  • Odoo checks automatically. The software reports user counts to Odoo. Extra users are billed at list price for the rest of the subscription, and tampering with the count carries a 300% fee.
What this means for WeCare

Leave, expenses and attendance fit within the standard Light User licence (Option 1). For Options 2 and 3, Code-ox extends Light User access with a custom module, so staff users can work in Project, Timesheets and Sales without buying full licences. Code-ox pays the Light User licences for all staff, so in all three Enterprise options WeCare pays only for the 5 full users. To be open with you: this extended access goes beyond Odoo's standard Light User, and Odoo counts anyone who logs timesheets as a paying user. If Odoo reviews the subscription, it may ask for these users to be upgraded to full licences. The cost of that is shown on the Cost Comparison page. Option 4 avoids the question entirely, because Community has no user licences.
