Overview
Executive Summary
Code-ox Technologies is pleased to submit this commercial quotation for a fresh Odoo ERP implementation for WeCare Technology, covering CRM, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Project, and Accounting. This is a new Odoo instance built from the ground up — no existing installation to work around, no legacy configuration to untangle.

As a systems integrator running ICT, ELV, MEP, and CCTV projects, WeCare's real bottleneck today is presales and procurement: vendor prices scattered across emails and spreadsheets, BOQ revisions that are hard to track version to version, and submissions rebuilt from memory instead of pulled from one source of truth. This proposal closes that gap — one connected system where a tender becomes an opportunity, an opportunity becomes a quotation, a quotation drives purchasing and stock, and every project's real cost and margin is visible as it happens.

Per our discussions, the initial rollout is 1–5 users starting with Sales and Presales, with the full module set configured from day one so Purchase, Inventory, Project, and Accounting are ready the moment more users come on board.

General Terms
  • Scope of Work: This proposal covers fresh installation and configuration of CRM, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Project, and Accounting as described. Any features outside this scope will require a separate written agreement and may incur additional charges.
  • Client Responsibilities: WeCare Technology agrees to provide master data (chart of accounts, client/consultant/vendor contacts, product-service catalog, opening balances) and timely feedback within agreed deadlines. Delays caused by the client may impact the project timeline.
  • Odoo Licence Costs: Odoo user licence/subscription fees for 1–5 users (exact count confirmed at kickoff, starting with Sales/Presales) are billed directly by Odoo to WeCare Technology and are not included in this quotation.
  • Data Migration: This quotation covers a fresh install with master data setup. Migration of historical transactional data from any prior system is not included and can be scoped separately if required.
  • Revisions: Configuration includes up to two (2) rounds of adjustment post-training. Additional rounds will be charged separately at an agreed rate.
  • Warranty: Code-ox Technologies provides 6 months of complimentary support post go-live, covering all configured modules — see scope and terms below. Extended support retainer packages are available upon request after this period.
  • Data Ownership: All data entered into WeCare Technology's Odoo instance — records, documents, and configuration — remains WeCare Technology's property at all times and is exportable on request, including after project completion.
  • Delivery Timeline: Code-ox Technologies will propose a project timeline at kickoff and keep WeCare Technology informed of progress against it. Delays caused by Code-ox outside of agreed client-dependency delays will not be billed to WeCare Technology.
  • Limitation of Liability: Each party's liability to the other under this agreement is capped at the total value of this quotation, and neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses. This limit does not apply to breaches of confidentiality or misuse of client data.
  • Termination: Either party may terminate this engagement with 14 days' written notice. WeCare Technology pays only for work completed up to the termination date; Code-ox Technologies delivers all configuration and documentation completed to that point.
  • Confidentiality: Both parties agree to keep the contents of this proposal confidential and not disclose them to any third party without prior written consent.
  • Governing Law: This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, with both parties entitled to equal recourse under it.
Highlight — 6 Months Complimentary Support

Covered for 6 months from go-live, at no extra cost: bug fixes and configuration corrections on the CRM, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Project, and Accounting modules as built, plus minor adjustments to fields, roles, and templates — target response 2 business days. Not covered: new features, changes from Odoo platform updates, or issues from WeCare Technology's own post-launch configuration changes — these are scoped separately, keeping the period fair to both sides. An optional retainer covers support after 6 months.
