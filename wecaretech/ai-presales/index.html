The KSA contractor who adopts a presales AI workspace in 2026 has a 12–24 month head start over peers.

03 · Problem Definition

The seven failure modes of presales today.

Distilled from WeCare's own Odoo BRD and Sales SRD, from the v2 Command Center deck, and from interviews patterns we see across every KSA contractor we have worked with.

FAILURE 01
Fragmented tooling

Engineers jump between Excel, Word, ERP, vendor email threads, and SharePoint to build one quotation. Context-switching cost is real and unmeasured.

FAILURE 02
Revision chaos

A typical tender goes through R01–R05 with no audit trail. Which revision was actually sent? What changed between R03 and R04? Nobody can answer reliably.

FAILURE 03
Pricing is tribal knowledge

Vendor pricelists live in PDFs in inboxes. The senior engineer who knows last quarter's Datwyler price quits, and the team is exposed.

FAILURE 04
No compliance check

Tenders demand clause-by-clause compliance matrices. These are hand-built in Word — slow, error-prone, and the leading cause of disqualification.

FAILURE 05
Naming & numbering by salesperson

Each engineer invents their own quote numbering. Cross-team search and reporting becomes impossible. (WeCare BRD section 4.2.1.)

FAILURE 06
Margin is a post-mortem metric

Profitability is measured after the project closes — not during quote construction. The opportunity to course-correct is already gone.

FAILURE 07
Mobile / offline gap

Site engineers cannot quote on-site without internet. A surprisingly common KSA scenario — industrial sites, NEOM, remote substations.

RESULT
Quotation lead time = days, not hours.

Win rate is constrained by speed and accuracy. The contractor who responds first with the most defensible BOQ wins.

04 · Product Vision

One workspace. From tender PDF to client-ready submission, in under an hour.

The Presales AI Command Center is the single application a presales engineer opens in the morning. They drop in the tender package; the system extracts the BOQ, normalises items against the company's catalog, fetches latest vendor pricing, scores margin in real time, and on one click generates the complete technical and commercial submission pack — pricing sheet, technical proposal, compliance matrix, method statement, SLD, and project timeline — branded, bilingual, and ready to send.

Single Pane of Glass

All inputs (RFQ, drawings), all working data (BOQ grid), and all intelligence (margin, alternatives, compliance) live on one screen. No tab-switching.

AI as a teammate, not a chatbot

An embedded copilot that reads context, asks the right clarifying questions, and proactively suggests scope gaps, alternate vendors, and risk flags.

Bilingual & local by default

Arabic-first UI option, ZATCA-ready outputs, Hijri/Gregorian dual dates, KSA data residency, and PDPL-compliant tenancy from day zero.

Integration philosophy

Every connector is built on the same internal "Integration Framework" — a single mapping/transform/sync engine. We do not write integrations from scratch per ERP. Adding the next ERP = writing one adapter file and a field-mapping config. This is how we scale to 5+ ERPs without a 5x engineering team.

09 · KSA Localization & Compliance

Built for Saudi Arabia first, not retrofitted.

Every globalised SaaS that enters the Kingdom learns the hard way that "translate to Arabic" is not localisation. The product is engineered with KSA as the primary, default context.

Bilingual UI + content

RTL/LTR switch per user. Bilingual product master (Arabic + English descriptions per item). All generated documents available in either language or dual-column layout.

Hijri + Gregorian dates

Stored as both. User-pref selectable. Critical for government tenders that mandate Hijri.

VAT 15% & ZATCA Phase 2

Built-in VAT computation, ZATCA-compliant invoice XML, QR code generation, and integration with accredited e-invoicing solution providers.

KSA data residency

Primary deployment in Saudi region (Vercel me-south / AWS me-south-1). Documented data-flow for PDPL audits.

PDPL compliant

Data subject rights workflows, consent records, breach notification automation, DPA template for customers.

Saudi business identifiers

VAT number, Commercial Registration (CR), Wathiq integration for KYB, IBAN/SAR-only currency defaults.
