NADEC — Company Profile

Source pages: /en/about, /en, /en/ceo_speech, /en/Chairman_speech, /en/faqs, /en/statistics, /en/quality, /en/certificates

Identity

  • Full name: National Agricultural Development Company (NADEC) — نادك
  • Founded: 1981 (1401 AH), by Royal Decree, converting Haradh Agricultural and Animal Production Company into NADEC. First agricultural joint-stock company in the Saudi capital market.
  • Initial capital: SAR 400 million
  • Listed: Saudi Stock Exchange (Tadawul), ticker 6010 (NADEC:AB), since 1993 per FAQ page
  • HQ: Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
  • Major shareholder: SALIC (Saudi Agricultural and Livestock Investment Company) owns 38.648% of shares (source: /en/faqs)
  • Financial year end: 31 December (statements issued annually per /en/faqs)

Mission

"Providing products with beneficial nutritional value and exceptional quality that meet the needs and aspirations of customers and beneficiaries." (/en/about)

Vision

Deliver products with high nutritional value to achieve "regional and global leadership in the food industry sector," contributing to food security aligned with Saudi Vision 2030. (/en/about)

Tagline

"Nourish your life every day" — appears in product-page footer copy (e.g. /en/products/284); consistent with the RFP-referenced brand tagline. Note: per website-audit-findings.html Finding 3.5, this tagline was not found as real text on the homepage itself in the prior audit pass — it does appear as text on deeper templates (product pages), suggesting inconsistent application across the site, a finding worth carrying into the redesign brief.

Core Values

Care · Responsibility · Motivation · Excellence · Cooperation

Strategic Framework: 2023–2027 Strategy

Three pillars: Sustainability, Growth, Health. Goals: sustainable agricultural projects, investment in new food sectors, market expansion, and Vision 2030 food-security alignment. Related program name found in content: "New Dawn" strategy — positions NADEC as transforming into an integrated food producer with sustainable food security as the throughline (/en/food-safety).
