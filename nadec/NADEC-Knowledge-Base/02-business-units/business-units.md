# NADEC — Business Units / Divisions

## 1. Dairy (Milk and Cheese) — `/en/milk`
"Your Ideal Destination for Discovering the World of Fresh Dairy and Delicious Products." Largest product line by SKU count (11 pages of products site-side; 72 products cataloged in this crawl, likely 130+ total across all 11 pages). Sub-lines: Milk, Cheese (Feta, Cheddar, Mozzarella, Halloumi, Cream Cheese, Sandwich/Burger slices), Cream, Laban (fresh + flavored), Yoghurt (Greek + Fresh), Butter, Desserts (pudding-style), and the "Al Hilal" sub-brand (flavored long-life milk line launched 2025).

## 2. Juices — `/en/juice`
"High nutritional value, refreshing flavor, exquisite taste." 24 products across two pages: single-fruit juices (Orange, Apple) and "Mix Fruit Nectar" blends (Pineapple, Guava, Kiwi Lime Mint, Mango, Pomegranate, Strawberry, Berry, Lemon Mint, Orange Carrot), plus a fortified "8 Vitamins" nectar line and a "Strawberry Mojito" flavor.

## 3. Red Meat (Protein) — `/en/meat`
"Three core principles: fresh products from trusted livestock farms, quality/food safety standards, diverse options (beef, lamb, mutton)." 22 products across two pages, filterable by "Fresh" and "Chilled and Frozen." Product range spans whole cuts (Front Quarter, Thigh, Brisket, Rib Eye, Striploin, Tenderloin) and named sheep breeds (Local Naimi, Pakistani, Australian, Swakni) — indicating sourcing diversity across domestic and imported livestock. News article `/en/news/583` confirms a new Red Meat Slaughterhouse in Haradh completed final operational trials — active capacity expansion in this vertical.

## 4. Food and Vegetables — `/en/feed`
URL slug is "feed" but the page title and menu label are "Food and Vegetables" — a naming inconsistency inherited from an earlier IA (possibly originally an animal-feed section, repurposed). Current content: fresh tomato varieties (Candy Crew, Candy, Cherry on the Vine, Plum, Strabena, Tomato on the Vine) and the Olive Oil line (250ml–4L, positioned as "Innovative"). Only 11 products, the thinnest catalog of the four core categories — a content-completeness gap if NADEC intends this as a genuine growth vertical (produce diversification is referenced in the 2023-2027 strategy).

## 5. Nadec Solution — `/en/Solution`
B2B/foodservice division. "Tailored for restaurants, cafes, hotels, large corporations, and government entities." Single-vendor sourcing across dairy, juice, meat, and produce categories, with 24/7 ordering and delivery claimed. This is NADEC's only explicit B2B go-to-market channel found on the site — positioned as a differentiator (one vendor, all categories) versus category-specialist foodservice suppliers.

## 6. Innovative Products — `/en/innovation`
Not a separate manufacturing vertical but a cross-category tag applied to newer/premium SKUs: flavored high-protein milk drinks (Salted Caramel, Cookies & Cream, Vanilla, Chocolate, Strawberry), Ayran, Greek Yoghurt variants, Dates Milk, and the Olive Oil range. 15 products in this crawl were flagged "innovative" via cross-reference with this page's product list (see `products.json` `innovative_flag` field).

## Cross-Cutting: Agriculture & Livestock Projects
Four named agricultural projects underpin the product verticals above (`/en/projects`):
- **Hail Project** — agricultural/livestock
- **Wadi Al-Dawasir Project** — large-scale agriculture
- **Haradh Project** — "one of the most important and largest" NADEC projects, agriculture/livestock (site of the new red meat slaughterhouse per news/583, and the "NADEC Sustainability Park"/half-million-tree green cover initiative per news/618)
- **Al-Jouf Project** — olives, feeding the Food & Vegetables olive oil line
