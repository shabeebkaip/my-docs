National Agricultural Development Company — Knowledge Base & Business Analysis
Prepared for: Code-OX RFP response — NADEC website redesign, 2-year support, and SEO
Compiled: 22 July 2026
Source: Direct crawl of www.nadec.com (English site, ~90 pages/templates visited), cross-referenced with prior UX/SEO/competitor audit deliverables in /nadec/

1.Executive Summary

NADEC (National Agricultural Development Company) is a 45-year-old, Tadawul-listed, vertically integrated Saudi dairy/agri/FMCG company (ticker 6010) spanning agriculture, dairy processing, juices, red meat, and produce, with a B2B foodservice arm ("Nadec Solution"). The company is majority-anchored by SALIC (38.648% ownership) and reports actively to investors on a quarterly cadence, with a comprehensive governance-document refresh completed in early-to-mid 2026. Its public website is content-rich but structurally uneven: substantial, well-maintained investor and governance content sits alongside thin commercial product content (no nutrition facts, ingredients, or pricing anywhere in the catalog) and at least three page templates (/en/recipes, /en/departments, /en/initiatives) that render entirely empty despite having working UI scaffolding. The company's digital ambition — evidenced by a dedicated VP of Digital & Technology and a 2025 ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI-governance certification — outpaces what the current website itself demonstrates, which is failing Core Web Vitals on both mobile and desktop (see Section 21/22). This creates a clear, evidence-backed narrative for a redesign proposal: NADEC's operational and digital maturity is real and growing, but the public-facing website has not kept pace, and closing that gap is squarely the opportunity this RFP addresses.

2.Company Overview

NADEC was established by Royal Decree in 1981 (1401 AH) with SAR 400 million in initial capital, converting the former Haradh Agricultural and Animal Production Company into the National Agricultural Development Company — the first agricultural joint-stock company in the Saudi capital market. It listed on Tadawul in 1993. Headquartered in Riyadh, NADEC operates under the mission of "providing products with beneficial nutritional value and exceptional quality" and a vision of achieving "regional and global leadership in the food industry sector," explicitly tied to Saudi Vision 2030 food-security goals. Five core values — Care, Responsibility, Motivation, Excellence, Cooperation — anchor its stated culture. The company's 2023-2027 strategy rests on three pillars: Sustainability, Growth, and Health, operating in parallel with an internally referenced "New Dawn" transformation program aimed at integrated food-security leadership. (Source: /en/about, /en/ceo_speech, /en/food-safety; full detail in 01-company-profile)

3.Business Model

NADEC operates a vertically integrated model: it owns and operates agricultural projects (Hail, Wadi Al-Dawasir, Haradh, Al-Jouf) that feed raw materials into its own dairy, juice, meat, and produce processing operations, which are then distributed through a claimed 38,000+ retail/distribution outlets nationally. A separate B2B channel, Nadec Solution, serves restaurants, cafes, hotels, corporations, and government entities directly with 24/7 ordering across all four product categories from a single vendor relationship — a differentiated value proposition versus category-specialist foodservice suppliers. No e-commerce/direct-to-consumer online sales exist anywhere on the site, consistent with the category-wide pattern confirmed across all benchmarked competitors (Almarai, SADAFCO, Baladna, Al Safi Danone — none sell D2C either, per competitor-benchmark.html). Revenue is not concentrated in a single vertical based on catalog breadth (Dairy is by far the largest SKU count at ~130+ products across 11 listing pages), but exact segment-level revenue was not extractable from on-page content (it resides in unparsed annual report PDFs). (Source: /en/Solution, /en/projects, 02-business-units)

4.Organization

Governance runs through a 9-member Board of Directors chaired by Abdulaziz bin Saleh Al-Rebdi (Vice-Chairman: Amr bin Abdulaziz Al-Jalal), with the most recent appointment being Eng. Abdulrahman Abdulmohsen AlFadley (17 Nov 2025). Executive management is led by CEO Dr. Solaiman bin Abdulaziz Al-Twaijri, supported by eight Vice Presidents/C-level roles spanning Governance/Risk/Compliance/Legal, Digital and Technology, Sourcing and Supply Chain, HR, Finance, Agriculture, Sales and Distribution, and Marketing & Development — a structure that maps cleanly to the full vertically-integrated value chain. No individual biographies, tenure histories (except AlFadley), or committee memberships are published for any board or executive member — a governance-disclosure gap worth flagging. Named board committees (via governance-document titles, not membership rosters) include Audit, Nomination & Remuneration, and Executive Committees. (Full roster: leadership.json, 14-organizational-structure)

5.Products

The crawled catalog spans 141 products across four core categories: Dairy (72 captured of an estimated 130+), Juices (36, complete), Red Meat (22 of an estimated ~40), and Food and Vegetables (11, complete). Dairy is the deepest and most diverse line (milk, laban, cheese in 6+ formats, cream, Greek and fresh yoghurt, butter, desserts, plus a newer "Al Hilal" flavored milk sub-brand launched around May 2025 that is not referenced anywhere in corporate "about" copy). Juices offer single-fruit and "Mix Fruit Nectar" blends including a fortified "8 Vitamins" line. Red Meat spans both domestic (Local Naimi, Local Angus) and imported (Pakistani, Australian) livestock cuts. Food and Vegetables is the thinnest category (11 SKUs: tomato varieties plus an Olive Oil line badged "Innovative"). Across every product detail page sampled, no nutrition facts, ingredients, allergen data, SKU codes, or pricing were found — a systemic content gap confirmed at full-catalog scale, not an isolated page issue. (Full data: products.json, products.csv; 03-products)

6.Operations

NADEC states 2,500+ refrigerated trucks/cars in its distribution fleet, 910+ million square meters of farm and food-industry project land, and 38,000+ distribution points. Two figures for daily quality testing appear inconsistently on the site: 21,000+ (homepage) versus 13,000+ (Quality page) — both recorded verbatim without reconciliation since neither page explains the discrepancy. Six dairy farms and ~60,000 cows, and two dairy processing plants producing 1.5 million+ litres/day, are figures carried forward from prior verified research (seo-audit-findings.html) and were not independently re-confirmed with the exact same numbers on the pages crawled in this pass, though the "1.5 million+ litres daily" aggregate figure for dairy and juice production does appear on-site. (Source: /en/statistics, /en/quality, /en)

7.Manufacturing

Manufacturing capacity investment is actively underway in the red meat vertical: news coverage confirms "final operational trials" for a new red meat slaughterhouse in Haradh (news/583), consistent with the company timeline's 2024 entry "expanded red meat production." Separately, news coverage references "advanced automation system at NADEC production lines" boosting efficiency and food safety (news/610), and automated milking technology operated by female staff in dairy farms (news/614) — indicating manufacturing-line automation investment is happening in parallel across both the meat and dairy verticals, not just at the corporate/digital-strategy level. No manufacturing plant count, capacity, or location detail beyond "two dairy plants" and the four named agricultural project sites was found in crawled content. (Source: /en/news/583, /en/news/610, /en/news/614, /en/projects)

8.Agriculture

Four named agricultural projects anchor NADEC's upstream operations: Hail (agriculture/livestock), Wadi Al-Dawasir (large-scale agriculture, established 1982, NADEC's earliest project), Haradh ("one of the most important and largest" NADEC projects — site of both the new red meat slaughterhouse and the "NADEC Sustainability Park," a green-cover initiative with over half a million trees per news/618), and Al-Jouf (olives, established 1987, feeding the olive oil product line and the subject of a 2025 excellence/innovation award). International agricultural presence extends to a Sudan Agricultural Project since 2014. A verified KAUST (King Abdullah University of Science and Technology) research partnership on sustainable food systems exists (news/595) but is not reflected in the static /en/research page copy — a content-integration gap. (Source: /en/projects, /en/research, news articles 583, 595, 618)

9.Food

NADEC's finished-food product range spans Dairy, Juices, Red Meat, and Food/Vegetables (fresh produce plus olive oil), unified under the "Food Culture" section, which frames the company's quality commitments around three pillars: raw material control, process control, and inspection/testing. No recipes, meal-usage content, or nutrition education exists anywhere on the site — the /en/recipes page exists as a template but returns "No results found," confirmed as one of three empty-but-scaffolded page templates found in this crawl (alongside Departments and Initiatives). This is the single most consistently identified content gap across all three research documents referenced in this RFP effort (this crawl, competitor-benchmark.html, and seo-audit-findings.html). (Source: /en/food_culture, /en/recipes)

10.Feed

The /en/feed URL — despite its "feed" slug (likely a legacy holdover from an earlier animal-feed-focused IA) — now serves as the "Food and Vegetables" product category, containing fresh tomato varieties and the Olive Oil range. This is the smallest catalog (11 products) of the four core verticals and shows the least evidence of active content investment relative to Dairy, Juice, and the now-expanding Meat vertical. (Source: /en/feed)

11.Innovation

NADEC maintains a dedicated /en/innovation page curating 15+ products as "innovative" — largely high-protein flavored milk drinks (Salted Caramel, Cookies & Cream, Vanilla, Chocolate, Strawberry variants), Ayran, Greek Yoghurt variants, Dates Milk, and the Olive Oil range. This functions as editorial curation rather than a formal R&D/innovation-lab program; no dedicated innovation lab, incubator, or structured NPD (new product development) pipeline is described anywhere on the site. The company's most substantive documented innovation signal in this crawl is technological rather than product-focused: the ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI Management System certification achieved by the Digital & Technology division (news/643), positioning NADEC as an early Saudi adopter of formal AI governance — ahead of where its own public website's technical execution currently sits. (Source: /en/innovation, /en/news/643)

12.Sustainability

Sustainability is the single most heavily covered editorial theme in NADEC's news output: 11 of 36 sampled recent articles (30%) are sustainability/CSR-focused, spanning environmental workshops (with Riyadh Municipality's "Madinati" program), the Haradh "Sustainability Park" (500,000+ trees), Ramadan food-basket distribution, and health-awareness partnerships (e.g., with Bupa Arabia). Formal ESG governance materially advanced in 2026: three new ESG Position Statements (Product Quality & Safety, Environmental, Animal Welfare) were all published in April 2026, alongside Sustainability & ESG Reports for FY2023 and FY2024. A 2019 solar power purchase agreement with ENGIE and a 2020 Pure Harvest Smart Farms memorandum are the two most concrete sustainability-infrastructure commitments found in the company timeline. (Source: /en/Social_responsibility, /en/documents-library, news articles 618, 619, 626, 637; documents.json)

13.Financial Overview

NADEC discloses on a full quarterly cadence: 10 earnings presentations were cataloged spanning Q2 2021 through FY2025 (dated February 2026), showing genuinely continuous investor disclosure. Three years of Annual Reports (2023, 2024, 2025) are available as direct PDF downloads. No revenue, profit, EPS, or margin figures were extracted from on-page HTML content in this pass — these exist only inside the PDF documents, which were catalogued as metadata (title/date/URL) but not parsed page-by-page, consistent with this crawl's HTML-focused scope. The one hard financial-structure fact confirmed on-page: SALIC (Saudi Agricultural and Livestock Investment Company) owns 38.648% of NADEC's shares (/en/faqs), with financial statements issued annually as of 31 December. A follow-up pass opening the FY2025 Annual Report PDF directly is recommended to populate exact revenue/profit figures for a fully quantitative version of this section. (Source: /en/investors, /en/faqs, /en/documents-library)

14.Investor Insights

NADEC's investor relations content is integrated into the main site navigation (not a separate portal), matching the best-practice pattern identified in competitor-benchmark.html (the "Baladna model") rather than the siloed-subdomain failure mode SADAFCO exhibits. A comprehensive governance-documentation refresh occurred in a tight window (12 of 16 governance documents dated February-May 2026), strongly suggesting an active CMA/Tadawul compliance cycle — a redesign should be sequenced to visually match this substantive backend modernization. One structural inefficiency persists: the "Annual Reports" nav item under Investors links externally to an SAP HANA Cloud-hosted property (nadec-landing.cfapps.eu10-004.hana.ondemand.com) rather than the on-domain documents library, splitting brand/crawl equity across domains — a finding independently confirmed in both this crawl and seo-audit-findings.html Section 1. 15.Partnerships

The /en/partnerships page describes partnership categories generically (contractors, consultants, suppliers, manufacturers) and links to a functioning Ariba-based supplier self-registration portal, rather than naming specific named partners — a missed opportunity, since the news archive independently documents multiple concrete, named partnerships: KAUST (sustainable food systems research, news/595), the Social Development Bank (cooperation agreement, news/576), and diplomatic engagement with Georgia, Japan, and Tunisia around agricultural/trade collaboration (news/639, 640, 642). A separate "Partnership Initiatives" CSR page names LEK Consulting (food safety training funding) and the Association of Children with Disabilities as concrete community partners. Surfacing these named, verifiable partnerships as actual content on /en/partnerships and /en/research — rather than leaving them buried only in the news archive — is a clear, low-cost content win. (Source: /en/partnerships, /en/partnerships_Initiatives, news articles 576, 595, 639, 640, 642)

16.Research

The /en/research page describes R&D in general terms: modern technology integration, continuous process improvement, and collaboration "with various companies, institutions, and universities" — without naming any of them. This is materially understated relative to the actual, citable KAUST partnership found in news coverage (news/595, "KAUST & NADEC Bring Science & Agriculture Together to Develop Sustainable Food Systems in Saudi Arabia"). No dedicated R&D facility, lab, patent activity, or named research output was found elsewhere on the site. 18.SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Genuinely vertically integrated model (own farms → own processing → own distribution) is a hard-to-replicate structural advantage and a strong storytelling asset (echoing the "À l'heure du lait" real-farm-storytelling reference in design-direction.html).
  • Active, continuous investor disclosure (quarterly earnings since 2021) and a 2026 governance-document modernization already underway — investor trust infrastructure is solid, just visually dated.
  • Broad, credible certification portfolio (ISO 22000, ISO 9001, FSSC 22000, Global GAP, IFOAM, plus the forward-looking ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI certification).
  • Simpler brand architecture than its main rival Almarai (single master brand vs. Almarai's 12 sub-brands) — a genuine structural advantage for site IA simplicity, as already noted in competitor-benchmark.html.

Weaknesses

  • Product content is uniformly thin (no nutrition, ingredients, or pricing anywhere in a 141-product sample) — undermines both SEO and consumer trust-building.
  • At least three page templates render entirely empty (Recipes, Departments, Initiatives) — visible evidence of content-operations gaps, not just isolated bugs.
  • Two internally inconsistent statistics (quality-tests-per-day figure) sit on different pages of the same site.
  • Orphaned content exists (Chairman's Speech has no navigation path) alongside missing content (no named research/innovation partnerships surfaced despite them existing in news).
  • Website itself fails Core Web Vitals on mobile and desktop (see Section 21/22) — undermines the credibility of a company otherwise investing in real digital/AI maturity.

Opportunities

  • A recipe/nutrition content hub is white space versus every dairy peer in the benchmark set except NADEC itself (competitor-benchmark.html Section 4) — NADEC's broader category range (dairy + juice + meat + produce) makes this a bigger opportunity than for single-category peers.
  • The KAUST partnership, Haradh Sustainability Park, and other concrete, citable stories currently buried in the news archive can be elevated into permanent Research/Sustainability page content with minimal new research required — the material already exists.
  • SALIC's anchor ownership and the 2026 governance refresh provide a credible moment to relaunch a more visually mature, Baladna-grade Investors section.
  • Red meat vertical capacity is expanding (Haradh slaughterhouse) ahead of a likely marketing/content push — the website redesign can be timed to support that commercial ramp-up.

Threats

  • Almarai's greater scale (4M litres/day vs. NADEC's 1.5M+, per prior verified research) and larger point-of-sale network remain the structural competitive gap NADEC cannot close through website design alone.
  • SADAFCO and Baladna are actively investing in their own digital/content experiences (Baladna's blog and IR integration in particular) — the "no Saudi competitor has a nutrition blog" white space window will not stay open indefinitely.
  • Mismatched-certificate and off-domain-microsite issues (nadec.com.sa cert mismatch, SAP HANA/Azure properties) pose an ongoing, cumulative trust and SEO-equity cost the longer they remain unresolved.

19.Competitor Analysis

20.Digital Presence

NADEC maintains seven social platforms (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, YouTube, TikTok, Snapchat) — a broad footprint appropriate to a large consumer FMCG brand, with a live X/Twitter feed embed on the social-media hub page suggesting that channel is treated as a primary real-time presence. Follower counts, posting cadence, and engagement data are not published on-site and were not independently pulled from each platform in this pass (out of scope for a website-content crawl) — flagged as a discovery-phase task if social strategy is in the RFP's scope. Brand handle naming is inconsistent across platforms (nadecfoods vs. nadec.com.sa vs. NadecKSA vs. nadecfood), a minor but fixable brand-consistency issue. Beyond social, NADEC's digital footprint is fragmented across at least four separate hostnames: the primary nadec.com, a jobs2web.com careers ATS, an SAP HANA Cloud-hosted Annual Reports property, and (per prior research) an Azure-hosted property and a nadec.com.sa domain serving a mismatched SSL certificate — all splitting crawl/brand/trust equity that a consolidated digital strategy should address. (Source: /en/social-media, 07-social-media)

23.Website Architecture

Confirmed technology stack (per website-audit-findings.html Finding 1.1, re-confirmed by this crawl's consistent Drupal-pattern URLs throughout): Drupal 9/10 with a custom theme (nadec_theme), fronted by Cloudflare. URL structure mixes clean paths (/en/about) and legacy /index.php/en/x paths interchangeably — often for the exact same link within a single page's markup, confirmed at much higher volume in this crawl (dozens of instances across products, investors, and legal-page links) than the original 2-example finding in seo-audit-findings.html Finding 6.4. Site-wide primary navigation follows a 7-department flat structure (About, Social Responsibility, Products, Investors, Sustainability, Media Center, Contact), each with 3-6 second-level items — comprehensive but not audience-segmented (see design-direction.html Section 3 for the recommended audience-first restructure). Three additional, functionally significant pages exist outside the main navigation, discoverable only via sitemap.xml: Chairman's Speech (populated), and Recipes/Departments/Initiatives (all empty templates). Content types identified: static informational pages, a paginated product catalog (category → detail, numeric ID-based URLs), a paginated news archive (11 pages), a filterable documents library (4 pages, tag-filterable by ESG/Governance/Financial categories), and a JS-rendered interactive branch-locator map (the one component in this crawl not accessible via static HTML fetch). (Source: full crawl; navigation.json)

24.Recommendations

Content/knowledge-base-specific recommendations only — see website-audit-findings.html, seo-audit-findings.html, and design-direction.html for the full UX/SEO/design recommendation sets, which are not duplicated here.

  1. Populate or retire the three empty page templates (Recipes, Departments, Initiatives) as a first-wave content-operations task, ahead of or alongside the visual redesign.
  2. Link the Chairman's Speech into primary navigation.
  3. Commission full nutrition/ingredient/SKU content authoring across the product catalog — the single largest content-scope item this crawl identified, likely requiring NADEC's quality/regulatory team's input, not just marketing copy.
  4. Reconcile the two conflicting daily-quality-test figures before either is reused in new site copy.
  5. Replace the careers Safelinks URL (which exposes an internal staff email) with a clean branded link.
  6. Actively surface named partnerships (KAUST, Social Development Bank, LEK Consulting, diplomatic engagements) as real content on the Research and Partnerships pages rather than leaving them buried in the news archive only.
  7. Publish a downloadable press/media kit (logo assets, brand guidelines) — none currently exists.
  8. Resolve the "feed" URL slug versus "Food and Vegetables" page-title mismatch as part of any URL-restructuring work.
  9. Standardize social-media handle naming across all seven platforms.
  10. Budget a headless-browser or direct-data-export pass to fully enumerate branch/distribution-point locations, since the current map widget is not crawlable via static HTTP fetch.

Full detail: 15-recommendations.

25.Opportunities for Digital Transformation

NADEC's internal digital maturity — a dedicated VP of Digital and Technology, the ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI Management System certification, production-line automation in both dairy and meat operations, and a comprehensive 2026 governance-documentation modernization — is genuinely ahead of what its public website currently reflects. This is the central strategic opportunity for the redesign RFP to address: the website redesign is not starting from zero digital maturity, it is catching the public-facing layer up to operational and governance maturity that already exists internally. Concretely, this creates five aligned transformation opportunities: (1) a nutrition/recipe content hub, filling the single clearest white-space gap versus every benchmarked competitor; (2) a modernized, Baladna-grade Investors section that visually matches the substantive 2026 governance refresh already completed; (3) full structured-data/schema implementation (Organization, Product, LocalBusiness, FAQPage) to convert NADEC's real scale and certifications into search-visible rich results; (4) consolidation of the fragmented multi-domain footprint (careers ATS, SAP HANA annual-reports property, and any Azure/mismatched-cert properties) under the primary domain to concentrate crawl and brand equity; and (5) a genuine "farm to carton" storytelling section — leveraging NADEC's real, differentiated physical assets (named farms, the Haradh Sustainability Park, KAUST research partnership) rather than synthetic or borrowed visual concepts, consistent with the "Grounded Premium" design direction already recommended in design-direction.html. Together, these position the redesign not as a cosmetic refresh but as the digital layer finally catching up to a company that is already investing seriously in AI governance, automation, and sustainability — a genuinely strong, evidence-backed narrative for the RFP response.
