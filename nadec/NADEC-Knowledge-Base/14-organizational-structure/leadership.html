Organizational Read

The executive bench spans the full vertically-integrated value chain: Agriculture (farm operations) → Sourcing/Supply Chain → Digital & Technology (note: this VP owns the ISO/IEC 42001 AI certification per news/643) → Sales & Distribution → Marketing & Development → Finance → Governance/Risk/Compliance/Legal → HR. This is a conventional integrated-FMCG executive structure; the explicit "Digital and Technology" VP seat (distinct from a general operations/IT role) is notable and signals digital transformation has a dedicated executive owner, not just a project team — directly relevant to how a website redesign RFP should be pitched (this VP or their delegate is the likely internal sponsor/stakeholder).
