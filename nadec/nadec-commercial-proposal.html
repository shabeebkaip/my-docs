03 · Payment Milestones
Payment Milestones

Payment is milestone-based against formal acceptance evidence. Deliverable 2 milestones apply only if the optional deliverable is activated.

Milestone% of D1 PriceAmount (SAR)
D1-1Kickoff, discovery, approved IA & user journeys 30% 6,600
D1-2Approved wireframes & design direction 30% 6,600
D1-3Deliverable 1 formal written acceptance (final designs, prototypes, static templates, source handover) 40% 8,800
Total — Deliverable 1 (Excl. VAT) 100% 22,000

Deliverable 2 payment milestones (RFP §5.1 gates D2-1 through D2-4) are proposed at the point of activation, tied to its own confirmed price.

Delivery Governance Behind Each Milestone

Each payment gate maps to a stage-gate in Code-OX's delivery lifecycle (kickoff → discovery → UX/UI design → front-end development → testing & QA → client review & approval → deployment/go-live → hypercare), with weekly progress reporting, a shared risk register, and documented change control throughout — not a single lump-sum release at the end.

Commercial Conditions

  • Prices are held firm for the stated proposal validity of 90 days from submission.
