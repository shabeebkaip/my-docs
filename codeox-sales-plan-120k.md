## 4. WeCare Technology — Pricing Risk Flag

### The Problem

WeCare was being positioned as a "lighthouse partner" — potentially receiving a heavily discounted AIKA/AIWA deployment. At 58,000 SAR/year in vendor cost, any WeCare deal priced below 80,000 SAR/year produces less than 30% gross margin — and at below 70,000 SAR/year, CodeOx is covering vendor cost with near-zero or negative net profit.

### Recommended WeCare Deal Structure

**Option A — Phased engagement (recommended):**
Phase 1: Sell WeCare in-house consulting services only — workflow design, requirements documentation, vendor integration mapping, and a 60-day pilot using AIWA in manual mode (no platform subscription yet). Price: 20,000–28,000 SAR for Phase 1. Margin: high (pure in-house delivery). Outcome: WeCare is embedded in the CodeOx ecosystem, the use case is validated, and CodeOx has a reference story without destroying margin.

Phase 2: Once Phase 1 validates ROI, introduce the full AIKA/AIWA platform at new standard pricing (97,000–105,000 SAR/year). WeCare's Phase 1 investment is credited toward Phase 2 if they commit within 30 days of Phase 1 completion.

**Option B — Discounted consulting, full-price platform:**
Offer WeCare a 20% discount on CodeOx in-house add-on services (custom workflows, reporting, Arabic tuning) but hold the platform price at standard. Total deal: 97,000 SAR/year platform + 12,000 SAR in-house (discounted from 15,000 SAR) = 109,000 SAR/year.

**Option to avoid:** Discounting the AIKA/AIWA platform below the 40% gross margin floor.

## 12. Objection Handling Scripts

**"LinkedIn Marketing is too expensive"**
"Our minimum engagement is 3,500 SAR/month — that is one day of a marketing coordinator's salary in KSA. We handle 30 days of content, in Arabic and English, across your company and personal LinkedIn pages. The question is not whether 3,500 SAR is expensive. The question is whether your competitors are showing up on LinkedIn every week while you are not."

**"We can hire someone internally for LinkedIn"**
"A capable in-house LinkedIn content person with Arabic + English writing skill costs 6,000–9,000 SAR/month in KSA, plus benefits, onboarding, and time to ramp. We are operational from day one at 5,500 SAR/month with a team that has already built content libraries for your sector."

**"AIKA/AIWA is too expensive at 97,000 SAR/year"**
"A junior procurement coordinator in KSA costs 4,000–5,500 SAR/month — that is 48,000–66,000 SAR/year. That person takes Eid leave, works 8 hours a day, cannot handle 200 vendor WhatsApp messages simultaneously, and produces no audit trail. AIKA and AIWA work 24/7, scale to any volume, and have never missed an RFQ deadline. The ROI is positive from month one if your team is spending more than 2 hours a day on manual vendor communication."

**"We need to think about it"**
Do not send a follow-up email. Ask: "What specifically would you want to know before moving forward? Let's address it in a 15-minute call on [specific day]." If they will not book a call, the deal is cold — move to monthly nurture.

**"We have an internal team for this"**
"What does your internal team currently not have time for? Our clients typically come to us when their internal team is stretched across operations and cannot maintain consistent output. We are not replacing your team — we are the execution arm that runs so your team can focus on strategy."
