SECTION 03

Foundations: Entity, Intent and Audience

Before any page is written, the strategy fixes three things. In semantic SEO these are the anchors that keep every page pointing in the same direction.

Central entity

Al Saber Cars — a 75-year Saudi Arabian vehicle import and dealership house. Every page connects back to this entity, and to the brands, models, financing and import services around it.

Central search intent

A Saudi buyer, individual or corporate, researching a specific make and model's price, financing route and dealer trustworthiness before buying. This mixes wanting-to-know with wanting-to-buy, which is why content must both inform and lead to inventory.

Target audience

  • Primary: Riyadh-based individual buyers, from mass-market brands (Toyota, Kia) through to ultra-luxury (Ferrari, Rolls-Royce).
  • Secondary: corporate and fleet buyers, who already have a dedicated purchase path on the site.

Why this matters

Al Saber is a dealer and importer, not an advertising-funded media site. That single fact shapes every decision: content isn't written to sell ad space, it's written to move a real buyer toward a real car or a finance application. Every page ends with a clear step — view matching stock, or apply for financing.

Evergreen pages vs. stock listings: today, each car page is tied to a single unit and disappears when the car sells, taking any ranking with it. SECTION 06

The Content Production System

Every important page is written the same way, so quality stays high whoever writes it — the answer-first method, built so both a person and an AI can use the page.

The page blueprint

  • Open with the direct answer in the first two or three lines — for a price guide, the actual starting price in riyals.
  • Follow with the detail the buyer needs: versions, specifications, financing, how it compares.
  • Under each question-style heading, a short answer block of about 35–45 words that makes sense on its own.
  • Add a proof layer: the 75-year history, real showrooms, real outcomes or reviews where available.
  • End with a clear next step: view matching stock, or apply for financing.

Writing rules that make content rank and get quoted

  • Use specific model names, real prices, and real numbers — AI quotes specifics, not adjectives.
  • Write for the buyer's real question, not for a keyword. Headings should read like a question a person asks.
  • Keep sentences short and direct — one idea per sentence.
  • Never invent facts, prices, or client stories. Where a real proof point is missing, gather it before publishing.

The proof layer — Al Saber's advantage

Most competitors write generic content. Al Saber can do something they can't: back claims with 75 years of real trading history, real physical showrooms, named leadership, and real import cases. This proof layer earns trust from both buyers and AI search, and it's built into every page rather than added as an afterthought.

SECTION 08

Technical & On-Page Requirements

These fixes make the content readable by Google and AI, and remove faults confirmed in our own live review of alsabercars.com. Several are quick wins.

Meta descriptions & titles

Blank sitewide on every page checked. Write a real, keyword-aware title and description for every page.

Otherwise Google writes its own snippet, and the site loses control of how it appears in results.

Missing H1s

Zero H1 elements confirmed on the pages audited. Every page needs one clear, descriptive H1 that reads like the buyer's question or the model name.

Without it, Google can't tell what the page is about.

Sitemap & robots.txt

/sitemap.xml returns 404. robots.txt returns 200 (allow-all) but has no Sitemap directive.

Google gets no map of the site — generate and submit a sitemap, add the directive.

Zero structured data

No JSON-LD anywhere. Add Vehicle/Product/Offer schema on model pages, FAQPage on Q&A content, AutoDealer/LocalBusiness on branch pages.

68% of pages cited in Google AI Overviews use structured data — this is table stakes for AI search, not just classic SEO.

Zero Open Graph tags

No OG/Twitter Card tags confirmed. Every shared car link on WhatsApp — the primary way cars circulate socially in the Kingdom — arrives as a bare URL with no preview.

The cheapest organic marketing channel currently arrives stripped of its selling power.

Invisible English version

EN exists only via a session-based switch — the same URL serves both languages, with zero hreflang and zero canonical tags.

Search engines can't discover, crawl, or rank the English content. Fix: language-prefixed URLs with proper hreflang pairs.

Duplicate hosts

www and apex both serve identical live content with no 301 redirect and no canonical tag.

Ranking signals split across two "sites" instead of consolidating on one.

Images invisible to search

125 of 126 images confirmed with no alt text; galleries are CSS background-image divs, not real <img> tags.

The entire inventory is excluded from Google Images — a major car-shopping discovery channel.

Core Web Vitals

Measured: mobile PageSpeed 55, LCP 57.5s, 34MB page weight.

Performance is itself a ranking factor — a slow site suppresses everything else this strategy builds.
SECTION 09

Local SEO for the Riyadh Showrooms

Al Saber has real, physical showrooms in Riyadh — a genuine advantage over online-only rivals, currently unused in search. None of CarDekho, CarWale or YallaMotor publish true branch-level local content — this is an open lane.

To confirm before publishing: exact branch names, addresses and categories need sign-off from Al Saber directly during Phase 1 — we don't want to publish location data we haven't verified with the client.
  • Create and fully complete a Google Business Profile for each showroom — photos, hours, services, and specific categories (a brand-specific category like "Mercedes-Benz Dealer" outperforms a generic "Car Dealer").
  • Publish a dedicated page per showroom — map, address, opening hours, stock available there, contact details.
  • Keep business name, address and phone number identical across the website, both profiles, and every directory listing (NAP consistency).
  • Build 15–20 local and automotive citations to confirm Al Saber is a real, established Saudi business.
  • Run a steady review-generation process (post-purchase SMS/WhatsApp ask) — review velocity matters more than total count for map-pack ranking.
  • Add a general "buy a car in Riyadh" guide linking into the brand hubs and both showroom pages.

76% of local searchers visit a business within 24 hours of searching — the map pack is, in effect, the digital forecourt.

SECTION 10

AI Search, Schema & Answer Engine Optimization

Buyers increasingly get answers from AI tools — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews — not just a list of links. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) means making Al Saber's content so clear and specific that these tools choose it when building an answer.

  • Answer blocks: a self-contained 40–60 word answer under each question heading, written so an AI can lift it directly.
  • Labelled FAQ schema: code that tells AI "this is a question and this is its answer," on the FAQ hub and key pages.
  • Price and comparison tables: structured, specific data AI prefers to quote over vague prose.
  • Real specifics everywhere: prices in riyals, model names, showroom names, and the 75-year history — AI quotes facts, not adjectives.
An honest correction to common 2026 advice: no major LLM provider (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta, Mistral) reads an llms.txt file in production as of 2026, and Google's own May 2026 guidance explicitly advises against creating AI-specific text files. We won't spend budget on that tactic. Schema markup plus answer-first content is what actually earns AI citations — the same build that helps classic Google ranking helps AI search. One build, two payoffs.

SECTION 13

Assumptions & Risks

Being honest about what this plan depends on, and where the uncertainty sits.

Assumptions to confirm early

  • A full technical and keyword audit runs in Phase 1 to set real baselines, since none exists yet.
  • Saudi-specific keyword volumes, in Arabic and English, are confirmed before writing, so effort goes where demand is.
  • Al Saber's real best-selling models per brand are confirmed, so tiered page counts reflect actual stock priorities.
  • Exact showroom names and addresses are confirmed with Al Saber directly before any location page or Google Business Profile is published.
  • Access is provided to the website, Google Analytics, and Google Search Console, and to real proof points for the content.

Honest notes on risk

No exact ranking can be guaranteed
Google's and AI systems' algorithms aren't ours to control. The commitment is to the method, the work, and honest reporting.
Templates give structure, not filler
Every page still needs genuinely useful, specific content. Thin pages built on a template will not rank.
Publishing must be paced
Many pages published too fast reads as spam to search engines — growth is checked against Search Console so the site grows in a way search engines trust.
Financing content isn't uncontested
CarSwitch and ArabWheels already publish murabaha guides — Al Saber wins on in-house, dealer-backed credibility, not the keyword alone.
