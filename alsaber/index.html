02 · The Plan

Three phases, one growth engine.

01
Premium Bilingual Website
A fast, indexable Arabic + English site that carries the brand the cars deserve: split luxury/value experiences, full-screen galleries, per-car WhatsApp and call actions, and a Request-a-Quote flow that asks three things — name, phone, car — and hands the rest to the salesman.
Fixes the conversion leak
02
SEO Growth Engine
Sitemap, structured data, Open Graph, hreflang, one canonical host, and per-car rich results — turning an inventory Google cannot see into one it recommends. Every new arrival indexed the day it lands.
Fixes the visibility gap
03
VIP Mobile App
For the buyers worth keeping: stock alerts, saved cars that persist, private viewing bookings, and concierge chat. Retention for a clientele that buys more than once.
The website acquires · the app retains
