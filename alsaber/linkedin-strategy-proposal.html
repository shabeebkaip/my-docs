Overview
Executive Summary
Al Saber Cars carries 75+ years of family heritage in vehicle import and export, and a live inventory spanning roughly 70 brands — from Ferrari, Lamborghini and Rolls-Royce to BYD, Maxus and Toyota, plus collector metal such as a 1990 Testarossa and a 2009 SLR 722. None of it is visible on LinkedIn, where the Kingdom's fleet managers, procurement leads and business owners concentrate their professional attention. Today, that page does not exist.

This proposal covers a fully managed LinkedIn engagement: a company page built from scratch, a bilingual four-pillar content engine, a founder profile carrying the family story, and a lead engine aimed at six named buyer profiles — not an abstract "luxury audience." Every claim in this strategy survives the test a sceptical fleet manager would apply: one web search.

Positioning
Not an Official Dealer. The Kingdom's Sourcing House.
Official agents are constrained: one brand, factory allocations, list prices, corporate tone. Al Saber's independence is the product — it can source any marque, any year, any spec, buy back, re-export across the GCC, and move faster than any franchise. Seventy-five years of family trade is a credential no franchise agreement can grant. Every post, profile line and DM in this campaign speaks from that position — differentiating against SAMACO and Fast Auto Technic rather than imitating them.
The Core Line

"The official dealer sells you what the factory sends. Al Saber finds you the car you actually asked for."

Hook: fleet cost-per-kilometre breakdowns, cash-vs-financing math, EV fleet economics under Vision 2030 — lands on the existing شركات purchase track.
02 SME Owners & Entrepreneurs — PrimaryBusiness owners who buy twice — a statement car for themselves and vehicles for the company. Live on LinkedIn professionally; vet a supplier's credibility before ever calling. Hook: "buy for yourself and your business" content, import savings vs showroom list prices, heritage story.
03 HNWIs & Collectors — PrimaryQuiet researchers, decisive buyers wanting what the official channel cannot allocate: rare specs, discontinued models, provenance cars. Al Saber's live inventory — Testarossa, SLR 722, Purosangue — is the proof. Hook: sourcing stories, provenance threads, market-value commentary on collector metal.
04 Regional Dealers & Exporters — PrimaryThe B2B trade room nobody else addresses: GCC and African dealers sourcing Saudi stock, exporters using Riyadh as a hub. Import/export is Al Saber's founding business. Hook: re-export regulation explainers, GCC price-arbitrage insight, wholesale availability notes.
05 Finance, Leasing & Insurance Partners — AmplifierBanks, leasing firms and insurers seeking dealer partnerships. Vet partners on LinkedIn first; a credible page opens co-branded financing offers that make ICPs 1–2 easier to close. Hook: partnership announcements, financing-product commentary.
06 Automotive Press & Vision 2030 Ecosystem — AmplifierJournalists and mobility-programme executives who quote sources, not sellers. Market-insight posts earn citations; citations compound back into every room. Hook: quotable data points on the Saudi import market, quarterly trend notes.

Content Architecture
Four Pillars, Four Posts a Week
#Pillar & Outcome
01 Heritage & House — SundaySeventy-five years of the Al Saber family in the car trade: standards, sourcing discipline, the name — unmatchable, no franchise can grant or copy it. Outcome: inbound trust; passes the silent audit every buyer runs before the first message.
02 Market Insight — TuesdayThe Saudi import market from the inside: price movements, Chinese-EV fleet adoption, Vision 2030 mobility shifts, GCC re-export flows. Outcome: source status — press quotes it, partners share it.
03 The Sourcing Story — ThursdayHow a specific car was found, verified, imported, delivered — from a fleet of Maxus vans to a 1990 Testarossa. Outcome: familiarity and differentiation — content no official agent is allowed to make.
04 Fleet & Client Proof — SaturdayCorporate deliveries, partnership announcements, collector acquisitions — social proof mapped to each ICP, always ending at the corporate purchase track or a DM. Outcome: conversion — warm attention becomes a showroom enquiry.

2–3 personal posts a month from a named family leader. LinkedIn's distribution rewards people over logos, and a 75-year family story belongs to a face.
