Overview
Executive Summary
Code-ox Technologies is pleased to submit this proposal for the complete redesign of alsabercars.com. Our independent audit — 47 verified findings, cross-checked against Google PageSpeed data — found a showroom carrying Rolls-Royce, Ferrari and Lamborghini presented on a generic template that takes up to 57.5 seconds to show a car on mobile, is nearly invisible to Google, and mixes a 465,000 SAR G63 AMG with a 180,000 SAR family SUV in one undifferentiated grid.

This proposal covers a full rebuild: a brand-true, bilingual (Arabic/English) website that separates the luxury and everyday-value buying journeys, gives every car a proper detail page with WhatsApp and viewing-request actions, and ships with the technical SEO foundation the current site never had.
