Beyond Google

Getting Quoted by ChatGPT and AI Overviews

Buyers increasingly ask AI tools for answers, not just a list of links. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) means writing so an AI can lift Al Saber's content directly into its answer.

💬

Answer Blocks

A self-contained 40–60 word answer under every question heading.

🏷️

FAQ & Product Schema

Labelled markup that tells AI exactly what's a question, an answer, a price.

📊

Price & Comparison Tables

Structured, specific data — AI quotes numbers and facts, not adjectives.

An honest correction to common 2026 advice: no major LLM (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta, Mistral) reads an llms.txt file in production, and Google's own May 2026 guidance says not to bother creating one. We won't spend budget there. Schema + answer-first content is what actually earns AI citations — the same build that helps classic Google ranking also wins AI search. One build, two payoffs.
