Overview
Executive Summary
Al Saber Cars carries 75 years of import heritage, in-house Murabaha financing, and a range from Toyota to Ferrari — a genuine advantage no online-only marketplace can copy. None of it shows up in search today: our own audit found 8 of 9 core technical checks failing, zero structured data anywhere on the site, and an English version that's invisible to Google entirely.

This proposal is a 12-month, six-track growth engine: fix the technical foundation, build a 205-page evergreen content map (growing to ~735 pages as it matures), win local search for both Riyadh showrooms, and write content the way AI Overviews and ChatGPT actually quote — schema and answer-first, not keyword-stuffed. 76% of local searchers visit a business within 24 hours of searching — the map pack is the digital forecourt. Exact branch names/addresses to be confirmed with Al Saber directly in Phase 1.

Beyond Google
Getting Quoted by ChatGPT and AI Overviews
Buyers increasingly ask AI tools for answers, not just a list of links. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) means writing so an AI can lift Al Saber's content directly into its answer.
01Answer BlocksA self-contained 40–60 word answer under every question heading.
02FAQ & Product SchemaLabelled markup that tells AI exactly what's a question, an answer, a price.
03Price & Comparison TablesStructured, specific data — AI quotes numbers and facts, not adjectives.
An Honest Correction to Common 2026 Advice

No major LLM (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta, Mistral) reads an llms.txt file in production, and Google's own May 2026 guidance says not to bother creating one — we won't spend budget there. Schema and answer-first content is what actually earns AI citations; the same build that helps classic Google ranking wins AI search too. One build, two payoffs.
