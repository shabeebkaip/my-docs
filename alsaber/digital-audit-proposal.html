The Core Problem

What You Sell vs. What Your Website Says

The current site was a reasonable starting point for getting online. The business has simply outgrown it — the inventory moved upmarket faster than the website did.

The showroom

Rolls‑Royce Ferrari Lamborghini Bentley Range Rover Porsche
Audi MG Elantra + 50 more brands

A curated collection any buyer in the Kingdom would take seriously — from a first family car to a seven-figure supercar.

The website, as that buyer sees it

  • Hero banner: a cut‑out car image on a grey box, headline text cropped off the screen
  • Homepage dominated by 60+ identical grey logo tiles — reads like a parts catalog
  • A Mercedes G63 AMG (465,000 SAR) listed in the same card grid as a 180,000 SAR family SUV
  • Car page: one watermarked warehouse photo, a two-word description, a lone "Buy" button

A buyer who can afford a Purosangue pattern‑matches in under 10 seconds. If the digital showroom doesn't feel like their world, they leave before ever seeing the inventory — that is the missing conversion.
What We Observed — First-Impression Audit

Four Moments Where the Experience Breaks

We walked the full journey on desktop and mobile, exactly as a customer would.

🎬

The first screen undersells the cars

The hero slider shows the Rolls‑Royce Spectre and Ferrari Purosangue as flat cut‑outs on grey shapes, with the headline text partially cut off. These are cars whose makers spend millions on presentation — the gap is instantly felt.

🗂️

Luxury and economy share one bucket

The G63 sits beside the Skoda in identical cards. The premium buyer reads "trader, not curator"; the value buyer gets no benefit either. One inventory, two audiences, zero segmentation.

📄

The decision page is empty

The car detail page — where desire becomes an enquiry — offers a single photo, a two-word description, and no gallery, video, financing calculator, WhatsApp button, or "book a private viewing". The most important page has the least content.

🌐

Half your market can't read it

The site exists only in Arabic. Riyadh and Jeddah's international and expat luxury buyers — a segment that specifically searches in English — can't use the site at all.

The Fix — Part One

A Website Worthy of the Cars

Not a re-skin — a rebuild around one principle: the digital showroom must create the same feeling as walking into the physical one.

Premium & Exotic

Cinematic full-screen photography, dark gold-accented identity drawn from your logo, full galleries and video per car, "book a private viewing" and direct-WhatsApp concierge — built to make a buyer crave the car.

The Fix — Part Two

An SEO Program Built From Zero — the Right Way

Because almost nothing exists today, every step produces visible movement. The program, in the order it will run:

1 · Foundation

  • Proper titles & descriptions for every car and page, Arabic and English
  • Sitemap, robots, canonical structure — give Google the map
  • Name every image — put the inventory into Google Images

2 · Rich results

  • Vehicle & dealer structured data — prices and cars directly in search results
  • Social-share previews — every car link shared on WhatsApp shows a beautiful card
  • Google Business Profile tuned for "car showroom near me"

3 · Ongoing growth

  • Bilingual content targeting brand + model + city searches
  • Monthly ranking & enquiry reports — SEO measured in leads, not jargon
  • Continuous technical health monitoring
Your Question, Answered Honestly

"What Would a Mobile App Actually Do for Us?"

Here is the straight answer: nobody discovers a dealer through an app — but an app is how a dealer keeps a wealthy customer for life. The two tools have different jobs:

The Website + SEO

Wins the customer

It's where new buyers find you — through Google, Instagram, a shared WhatsApp link. Its job is the first impression and the first enquiry. This must come first, because every ad dirham and every search click lands here.

The Mobile App

Keeps the customer

A luxury car buyer is a repeat buyer. The app lives on their phone as a private club: new arrivals before the public, their ownership documents, service bookings, trade-in offers. It turns one sale into the next three.

🔔
Early-access push

"A Purosangue just arrived — you're seeing it 24 hours before anyone." Direct to their pocket, no Instagram algorithm in between.

🗝️
VIP concierge

Book viewings and test drives, service appointments, documents, and a private line to your team — the ownership experience, not just a catalog.

🏎️
The craving machine

Full-screen cinematic inventory, 360° interiors, engine sound — the showroom-in-the-pocket experience that makes a buyer come see the car.

Our honest sequencing advice: website and SEO first, app as phase three. An exceptional app behind a weak website wastes its potential — fix the front door, then build the private lounge. When the website starts converting, the app multiplies it.
