Why Now

Three Facts That Point the Same Direction

These aren't opinions — the first is measured from your own site, the second is how the Kingdom already buys, the third is a KSA competitor proving the model works.

57.5s
Measured · your mobile site

Google PageSpeed measured 57.5 seconds to load your main content on mobile — the device almost every buyer uses first. A website that slow can't hold attention long enough to sell a car. An app installed once opens instantly, every time.

60%
How KSA actually buys — sourced

60% of Saudi car buyers now prefer online channels, and online car-buying in the Kingdom is valued at $3.2B, heading to $7.7B by 2033. Separately, in-chat WhatsApp checkout converts ~35% higher than a web redirect — the region's default commerce channel, not a side channel.

$82M
Syarah — proof, not theory

Syarah, the Kingdom's largest car marketplace, has raised $82M+ (incl. a $60M Series C, Sep 2024) running an app-first model — 12,000+ cars/year, 400+ dealers. Saudi buyers already trust a phone to buy a car; the only question is whose app they trust it with.

The Business Case

What This Fixes That the Website Alone Can't

The Leak We Already Measured

The purchase page drops the car

Today, clicking "Buy" on a specific car discards it — the buyer re-finds it in a 782-option dropdown, then faces a salary/bank interrogation before any human contact. In-app, the selected car, its price and a financing estimate travel with the buyer all the way to a named advisor. No re-selecting, no cold form.

The Channel That Doesn't Exist Yet

No way to reach past buyers

Right now, once a visitor leaves alsabercars.com, they're gone until they think to come back. The app is a permanent, owned channel — push notifications for new arrivals, price drops and trade-in offers — that costs nothing extra to use, unlike ads to win the same customer back.

What You Might Be Thinking

The Honest Answers

"We already have a website."
The audit measured it at 46/100 with a 57.5s mobile load and a purchase flow that drops the car the buyer selected. Apps convert 2–5x higher than mobile web and abandon at ~20% vs. ~85.65% (industry benchmarks). The website should be fixed and the app built — they serve different buyers.
"An app sounds expensive."
A realistic MVP runs SAR 90K–200K (industry-benchmarked range). Against a conservative recovered-revenue model of SAR 63K–468K/month, mid-case payback is under one month — and a single recovered luxury sale can cover the entire build.
"Our customers just use WhatsApp anyway."
Agreed — that's the design, not a contradiction. Today there's no per-car WhatsApp CTA (audit finding); the app puts a deep-linked WhatsApp button on every car with the car's details already attached, so the sales team gets context instead of a cold "hi."
"We need to think about it."
Fair. Rather than a blind commitment, we propose a small, fixed-scope paid discovery + clickable prototype first — a real browse→car→WhatsApp flow in a buyer's hands before committing to the full build.
