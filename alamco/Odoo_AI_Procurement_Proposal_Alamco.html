Overview
Executive Summary
Code-ox Technologies is pleased to submit this commercial quotation for a custom AI Procurement Agent and a fresh Odoo Enterprise ERP implementation for Alamco Spaceworks, covering CRM, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Project, Employees, and Accounting.

As a fit-out and bespoke joinery contractor delivering complex, multi-division BOQs across commercial, hospitality, and retail projects, Alamco's real bottleneck is procurement: BOQ files re-uploaded to ChatGPT session after session, prompts rewritten by hand, output cleaned up manually, and every purchase order re-typed into a system that has no knowledge of your catalogue or vendors. This proposal closes that gap on two fronts — an AI agent that turns a raw BOQ into live purchase requisitions in Odoo in minutes instead of days, and a single Odoo Enterprise instance that centralises the operations you run today across Zoho and a separate project app. Per our discussions, the initial rollout is 1–5 users, with the full module set configured from day one.

Scope of Work — AI Procurement Agent
The AI Procurement Agent is the custom-built layer that turns a raw BOQ into live purchase requisitions in Odoo. You do Step 1 — upload the file. The agent handles the rest.
# Module / Feature & Scope Description
Module 1 — BOQ Intelligence
01 Multi-Format BOQ Parsing (Excel & PDF) Reads standard, non-standard, and legacy BOQ formats — including merged cells and multi-sheet workbooks — with no manual prompting per file.
02 Division → Section → Item Extraction Full BOQ hierarchy captured; every line item extracted with its description, quantity, and unit — nothing missed.
03 Quantity & Unit Validation Extracted quantities verified against BOQ subtotals so errors are caught before anything reaches Odoo.
04 Project & Site Tagging Project name, site, and delivery date tagged to every extracted BOQ automatically for full context.
Module 2 — Odoo 19 Integration
05 Product Catalogue Matching Each material matched to your Odoo product catalogue and linked to its internal SKU; low-confidence matches flagged for a quick human check.
06 Approved-Vendor Matching Line items matched to vendors from your approved supplier list before any requisition is raised.
07 Auto Purchase Requisition Creation Validated procurement lists pushed into Odoo 19 as live Purchase Requisition records — zero manual re-entry.
08 Full Audit Trail in Odoo Every agent action recorded against the requisition inside Odoo — who, what, and when — for complete traceability.
Module 3 — Procurement Tracker
09 Per-Item Real-Time Status Live visibility on every requisition — ordered, pending, or overdue — across all active projects.
10 Automated Delay Alerts & Escalation Delays surfaced automatically before materials fail to arrive on site, with escalation to the responsible user.
11 Cross-Project Procurement Dashboard One dashboard view across every project, with historical reporting and analytics on procurement performance.
12 Secure File Handling Uploaded BOQs validated, scanned, and de-duplicated before processing.
Why This Approach — and the Hand-off to Odoo

The agent replaces the fragile manual ChatGPT workflow — re-uploading files, rewriting prompts, cleaning output, and re-typing every PO — with one repeatable pipeline. A 200-line BOQ that takes half a day today is processed in minutes, and every requisition it raises is written directly into the Odoo Purchase module covered in Section 2 — from there it flows through native Odoo purchasing, inventory, and accounting as one connected system. How It All Connects
Every module above is configured as one connected system — no re-keying between them, no separate apps to reconcile. A tender enters CRM and becomes a Sales quotation; when a BOQ arrives, the AI Agent pushes it through Purchase; POs receive into Inventory; materials and Employee labour land on the Project; and every cost and invoice rolls into Accounting. One instance, one source of truth — end to end.

One Source of Truth

Because Purchase, Inventory, and Project share the same records, a cost entered once is visible everywhere it matters — the vendor bill, the stock valuation, the project P&L, and the company ledger all reconcile automatically. Replacing Zoho and your separate project app removes the double entry and the gaps between them.
