02Your Current Challenges — Solved by Odoo 19
Without a CRM (Today) With Odoo 19 CRM
Free consultation requests arrive by WhatsApp, email, or website — no single place to see them all All inquiries land in one CRM inbox. Odoo auto-creates a lead from each email or web form submission
Assigning leads to one of the 5 reps is done manually by WhatsApp message — easy to forget Auto round-robin or manual assignment in one click. Each rep sees only their own pipeline
WhatsApp conversations with brands are on personal phones — history is lost if an employee leaves Send WhatsApp directly from the CRM lead record. Full conversation history saved against the brand forever
No visibility on how many leads are stuck, how long at each stage, or which rep is overloaded Live dashboard shows pipeline by stage, rep workload, conversion rate, and average deal time
Follow-up reminders are sticky notes or WhatsApp messages to yourself — things fall through Schedule a call, meeting, or email follow-up on any lead. Odoo notifies the rep when it's due
When a client asks "where are we on the proposal?" — the rep has to search through chats Every email, WhatsApp, note, and document is on the brand's CRM record — one click to answer

04WhatsApp & Email — The Saudi Business Stack
💬  WhatsApp Business Integration
  • Message brands directly from their CRM lead record
  • Full WhatsApp thread saved on the record permanently
  • Pre-approved message templates for consultation confirmations
  • Incoming replies trigger a notification to the assigned rep
  • One shared WhatsApp Business number — no personal phones needed
✉️  Email Integration
  • Connect your company email (Gmail, Outlook, or custom)
  • Set up info@franchiseme.net as a lead auto-creation alias
  • Every email to/from a brand is logged on their record
  • Send branded proposal emails with templates from the CRM
  • Mass email campaigns to past & future leads
KSA Market Context: WhatsApp is the primary B2B communication channel in Saudi Arabia. Odoo 19's native WhatsApp Business API integration means your team never leaves the CRM to message a client — and no conversation history is ever lost on a personal device.
