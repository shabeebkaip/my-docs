01 · The Brief

Project overview & our understanding

Nabdha AI has asked us to build the MVP of its AI business intelligence platform — a tool that reads an SME's own business data and tells the owner what to do next, not just what happened. The business owner uploads their data, Nabdha AI analyzes it, and the owner receives a Business Intelligence Report.

This is a complete design-and-build engagement: data upload and cleaning, an AI analysis engine, an AI-generated report, a web dashboard in Arabic and English, secure login, and go-live support. Every number in the report is calculated by code running on the owner's own data — the AI layer only explains, ranks and writes the recommended actions, keeping the output accurate and trustworthy from day one.

02 · Objectives

What the MVP sets out to achieve

Trustworthy numbers
Every figure in the report is calculated by code, not guessed by the AI — building confidence from the first report.
Decisions, not dashboards
Top risks, top opportunities and a prioritized action plan — not a wall of charts to interpret.
Fast, friction-free data entry
Excel, CSV or PDF upload — no system integrations required to get a first report.
Forward-looking
A 30-day sales forecast alongside the diagnosis, so owners see what's coming next.
Arabic-first, bilingual
Full Arabic and English report and dashboard for the Saudi SME market.
Architecture that scales
Built so direct system integrations and advanced features can be added later without a rebuild.

This MVP is data-upload only. Direct integrations with e-commerce, POS and accounting systems (Salla, Zid, Foodics, Odoo and similar) are a future phase, architecture-ready but not part of this build.

03 · Scope

What we'll design & build

Eight modules make up the MVP, from data upload through to a live, working report.

ModuleScope description
1 · Data Upload & IngestionExcel (.xlsx), CSV and PDF upload with downloadable templates for sales, inventory, customer and expense data.
2 · Data Cleaning & Quality LayerCommon data model mapping, missing/duplicate detection, format standardization, and a data-quality score.
3 · AI Analysis EngineRule-based and statistical signal detection across sales, inventory, customer and cash-flow indicators.
4 · Business Intelligence ReportAI-generated Top Risks, Top Opportunities and a prioritized Action Plan, in Arabic and English.
5 · 30-Day Sales ForecastExpected sales trend and revenue range for the next 30 days, based on uploaded historical data.
6 · Web Dashboard AR/ENDecision-first report view presenting risks, opportunities, forecast and action plan.
7 · Secure Login & Organization SetupAccount authentication and an isolated data space per organization.
8 · QA, UAT & Go-LiveFunctional and data-validation testing, UAT support, and production deployment.
Not included in this MVP

Direct system integrations (Salla, Zid, Shopify, WooCommerce, Foodics, Odoo, Rewaa, Qoyod, Daftra, Zoho Books, Salesforce/Dynamics 365), the conversational AI Business Advisor, and automated alerts are future-phase items — architecture-ready, quoted separately when scoped.
