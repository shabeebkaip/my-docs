01 · The Brief

Project overview & our understanding

Nabdha AI has asked us to build Version 1 of its AI business intelligence platform — a tool that reads an SME's own business data across sales, inventory, customers, orders, payments and delivery, and tells the owner what to do next, not just what happened. The business owner uploads their data, Nabdha AI analyzes it end to end, and the owner receives a full Business Intelligence Report.

This is a complete design-and-build engagement: data upload and cleaning, an AI analysis engine, an AI-generated report, a web dashboard in Arabic and English, secure login, and go-live support. Every number in the report is calculated by code running on the owner's own data — the AI layer only explains, ranks and writes the recommended actions, keeping the output accurate and trustworthy from day one.

02 · Objectives

What Version 1 sets out to achieve

Trustworthy numbers
Every figure in the report is calculated by code, not guessed by the AI — building confidence from the first report.
Decisions, not dashboards
Top risks, top opportunities and a prioritized action plan — not a wall of charts to interpret.
Fast, friction-free data entry
Excel, CSV or PDF upload — no system integrations required to get a first report.
Forward-looking
A 30-day sales forecast alongside the diagnosis, so owners see what's coming next.
Arabic-first, bilingual
Full Arabic and English report and dashboard for the Saudi SME market.
Architecture that scales
Built so direct system integrations and advanced features can be added later without a rebuild.

Version 1 is data-upload only. Direct integrations with e-commerce, POS and accounting systems (Salla, Zid, Foodics, Odoo and similar) are a future phase, architecture-ready but not part of this build.

03 · Scope

What we'll design & build

A complete data-to-decision platform, built in three layers: how data comes in, how the AI reads it, and what it hands back to the owner.

Data Ingestion & Processing
ComponentScope description
Multi-Source Data UploadExcel (.xlsx), CSV and PDF upload with downloadable templates covering Sales, Inventory, Customer, Order, Payment/Financial and Delivery/Logistics data.
Data Cleaning & StandardizationCommon data model mapping, format standardization (dates, currency, AR/EN), missing and incomplete record detection.
Deduplication & ValidationDuplicate-record detection, unusual-value flags, and consistency checks across every uploaded file.
Data Quality ScoreA visible quality score shown before the report generates — a weak report is explained by weak data, not the platform.
AI Analysis Engine
ComponentScope description
Sales Signal DetectionWeek-on-week and month-on-month decline, sudden spikes/drops, slowing growth, single-product revenue concentration.
Inventory Signal DetectionStock-out risk (days of stock vs. supplier lead time), slow-moving stock and overstock exposure.
Customer Signal DetectionRevenue concentration in top customers, churn detection, falling repeat-purchase rate.
Profit & Cash Signal DetectionMargin trend per product, expenses growing faster than revenue, cash-position indicators.
Opportunity Signal DetectionGrowing-demand products, cross-sell/bundle pairs, seasonal and regional demand patterns.
Business Intelligence & Forecasting
ComponentScope description
Business Health OverviewA single high-level diagnosis across sales, inventory, customer, cash and operational indicators.
Top 3 RisksTitle, explanation, supporting evidence and potential business impact for each risk.
Top 3 OpportunitiesOpportunity, why it was identified, supporting data and a recommended action.
30-Day Sales ForecastExpected sales trend and revenue range for the next 30 days, based on uploaded historical data.
Prioritized Action PlanHigh / Medium / Low priority actions tied directly to each detected risk and opportunity.
