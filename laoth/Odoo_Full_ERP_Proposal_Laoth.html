Executive Summary
Code-ox Technologies is pleased to submit this commercial quotation for a fresh Odoo ERP implementation for Laoth Advanced Trading Est., covering Accounting, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Manufacturing, Payroll, and Expenses. General Terms
  • Scope of Work: This proposal covers fresh installation and configuration of Accounting, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Manufacturing, Payroll, and Expenses as described. Any features outside this scope will require a separate written agreement and may incur additional charges.
  • Client Responsibilities: Laoth Advanced Trading Est. agrees to provide master data (chart of accounts, customers, vendors, products, employees, opening balances) and timely feedback within agreed deadlines. Delays caused by the client may impact the project timeline.
  • Odoo Licence Costs: Odoo user licence/subscription fees for the 2 users are billed directly by Odoo to Laoth Advanced Trading Est. and are not included in this quotation.
  • Data Migration: This quotation covers a fresh install with master data setup. Migration of historical transactional data from any prior system is not included and can be scoped separately if required.
  • Revisions: Configuration includes up to two (2) rounds of adjustment post-training. Additional rounds will be charged separately at an agreed rate.

  • Confidentiality: Both parties agree to keep the contents of this proposal confidential and not disclose them to any third party without prior written consent.
  • Governing Law: This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
