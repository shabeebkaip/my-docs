## Rules for every slide

- One clear idea per slide. If a slide needs more than ~25 words of body copy outside a mockup caption, cut it.
- No stock "AI-generated" clichés: no generic robot icons, no abstract gradient blobs, no meaningless dot-grid textures.
- Real content only — use the exact numbers, names, and terms given above. Do not invent new stats or features beyond what's specified here.
- Never show a raw clinical number (pain score, bare step count, bare sleep duration) directly to the patient — always narrate it as a relative, human sentence. This is a hard rule across the whole deck, not just Section 11.
- Keep every mockup phone the same frame style and proportions across slides.
- Gold fills a whole screen only in the celebration moment (Section 11) — everywhere else it's a single accent, never a background wash.
