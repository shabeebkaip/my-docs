Section 01
Executive Summary

The business problem, in one paragraph

Vitality Home Health Services has no functioning mobile booking experience today: every "Book Now" on the website exits to a plain WhatsApp chat or an external hospital-intake-style portal (patients.nixpend.com), with no live availability, no confirmation artifact, no payment flow, and no member accounts or history. Meanwhile every GCC digital-health competitor researched — Sehhaty, Sanar, Cura, Thaat, DarDoc, Justlife — competes on transactional breadth, not on being a trusted companion through a recovery journey. This BRD defines the mobile app that closes both gaps at once: a real native booking experience, plus a companion-first wellness layer (narrated scores, session-dot journeys, celebrations, family coordination) that no direct competitor currently offers.

Scope at a glance: 32 functional requirements across 5 layers, 25 of them MUST-have for v1.0, delivered across 3 release phases (v1.0 → v1.5 → v2.0), for iOS and Android, in Arabic (RTL-first) and English.

Section 02
Purpose & Scope

What this document defines

This BRD defines the complete functional scope of the Vitality member-facing mobile app (iOS + Android), replacing the current experience where every "Book Now" exits to a plain WhatsApp chat or an external hospital-style portal (patients.nixpend.com). It captures 32 requirements across 5 functional layers, each with a unique FR ID, priority (MoSCoW), owning dependency (App-native vs. Nixpend HMS vs. AIWA/AIKA), and target release phase.

In Scope

  • Native iOS and Android member-facing app covering booking, wellness tracking, emotional/motivational layer, in-app communication, and family coordination (FR-A1 through FR-E6, 32 requirements total).
  • Read-only wearable and health-platform data sync (FR-B9): Apple Health, Samsung Health (via Google Health Connect), and WHOOP.
  • Integration contracts with Nixpend HMS (availability, bookings, plans, visit summaries, red-flag routing), AIWA (WhatsApp/chat), and AIKA (voice) at the level of what data crosses each boundary — not their internal implementation.
  • Bilingual (Arabic RTL-first / English), prayer-time-aware, and Ramadan-aware behavior as system-wide non-functional requirements.

Out of Scope

  • Visual design specification and design tokens — see 08-design-direction.md.
  • Screen-by-screen UX flows and interaction detail — see 10-screens.html / 11-experience.html.
  • Commercial terms, pricing, and payment schedule — see the separate commercial quotation.
  • Nixpend HMS, AIWA, and AIKA internal architecture — owned by their respective system specs, referenced here only at the integration-contract level.
  • Computer-vision exercise feedback, telehealth video consults, pharmacy/marketplace breadth, wearable write-back, fall-detection escalation, and community features — explicitly deferred, see Section 11 (WON'T).

Section 04
Business Objectives

Why this app is being built

  • Close the digital gap. A full bilingual 4-step booking wizard already exists in the website's codebase but was never shipped as real UI. No live availability, no confirmation artifact, no payment flow, no accounts/history exist today.
  • Own the empty "companion" quadrant. Sehhaty, Sanar, Cura, Thaat, DarDoc, and Justlife all sit in the transactional half of the market. No GCC competitor combines clinical credibility with a wellness-grade, companion-first experience.
  • Make recovery visible. Session-dot journeys, narrated wellness scores, and outcome-evidence charts turn a service grid into a journey with a visible ending.
  • Support the family, not just the patient. The Family Care Circle lets one account (e.g. Noura) coordinate care for a dependent, with patient-controlled, per-datatype privacy.
  • Arabic-first, not Arabic-translated. RTL-first design, gender-guaranteed caregiver matching, prayer-time-aware scheduling, and Ramadan mode are infrastructure, not add-ons.

Acceptance Criteria — Key Requirements

How "done" is verified for the highest-risk requirements

Every MUST-have requirement needs a testable acceptance criterion before QA sign-off. The table below covers the requirements with the most ambiguity risk; remaining MUST items follow the same pattern at build time.

CriterionRequirementAcceptance Criteria
AC-01FR-A1 Booking WizardA member can complete Select Service → Date/Time → Details → Payment and receive a confirmation artifact (booking reference, caregiver card, calendar add) in under 4 taps beyond required data entry, using live Nixpend availability, with prayer-time windows excluded from bookable slots.
AC-02FR-A3 Therapist MatchingGender preference captured once is never asked again for that member across all future bookings; the Match Reveal screen displays a named caregiver with photo, credentials, and MOH license badge before any booking is finalized.
AC-03FR-B3 Daily Check-insA check-in (pain/mood/sleep sliders) completes in ≤30 seconds; any red-flag threshold breach triggers a Nixpend/AIWA routing event within 5 minutes without surfacing an alarming UI state to the member.
AC-04FR-B5 Narrated Wellness ScoreThe score is never displayed without its accompanying Arabic-first narrated sentence; no numeric value renders in red or any alarm-associated color under any data condition.
AC-05FR-B9 Wearable SyncConnecting Apple Health, Samsung Health, or WHOOP requires no data entry beyond the platform's native permission prompt; disconnecting or leaving a device unworn falls back silently to manual check-in with no reconnect nagging; no raw clinical number or wearable brand name appears on the home screen.
AC-06FR-D5 Prayer-Time NotificationsZero push notifications are delivered during any of the five daily prayer windows (Riyadh timings) across a 7-day test window, verified against the published prayer calendar.

Blocked pending confirmation of Nixpend's payment API contract — see risk below.
AC-10FR-B3 Functional Check-inThe daily check-in includes one plan-linked functional question in plain language (never a numeric ROM/clinical scale shown to the member); a declining trend over 3+ consecutive days routes to the care team the same way a pain red-flag does.
AC-11FR-B1 Plan Sync from Clinical NotesWhen a therapist updates the care plan in Nixpend after a visit, the member's Today's Care Plan reflects the change within 1 hour without requiring an app update or manual refresh by the member.

Section 13
Non-Functional Requirements
  • NFR-01 Bilingual, RTL-first. Every screen designed RTL-first with English as the faithful mirror; warm MSA copy localized to English, not the reverse.
  • NFR-02 Data residency & compliance. Health data is Sensitive/Tier-4 under Saudi PDPL (SDAIA). Cross-border transfer (relevant to WHOOP/Mi Health cloud APIs) needs contractual safeguards, not an assumption — formal legal review required before committing to any cloud-based wearable pipeline.
  • NFR-03 Prayer-time and cultural awareness as infrastructure. No pushes across the five daily prayer windows, Ramadan-shifted schedules, Friday-family-day awareness — on by default, not configurable per feature.
  • NFR-04 Privacy by design. Per-datatype consent model (FR-E4) applies system-wide, including any future wearable data — conservative defaults, plain-language sharing summaries.
  • NFR-05 Performance & pacing. Matching wait screen minimum 2400ms (theatrical, even if backend is instant) — deliberate pacing is a UX requirement, not a performance gap.
  • NFR-06 Integration boundary. Nixpend HMS is system of record for availability, bookings, and clinical facts; the app never becomes a second source of truth for clinical data.
  • NFR-07 Availability. Booking and check-in flows must degrade gracefully (cached last-known state, clear retry messaging) during any Nixpend API outage — never a blank or crashed screen.
  • NFR-08 Accessibility. Minimum WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and type-size floor across both language directions, consistent with the "premium, not just pretty" brand requirement.
  • NFR-09 Security. Biometric unlock for returning members after deferred-until-booking identity; step-up authentication required specifically for payment and medical-record actions.

Section 14
Assumptions & Risks

Assumption: Nixpend HMS exposes the read/write APIs this BRD assumes (availability read, booking write, plan read, visit summary, red-flag write). Confirm API contract with Nixpend before Layer A/B implementation begins.

Risk (open, needs verification): Payment processing for FR-A6 (Mada, Apple Pay, Tabby/Tamara, STC Pay, cash-on-visit) is owned by Nixpend, not built directly into the app. Nixpend's payment API contract — supported methods, tokenization/PCI scope, refund flow, webhook/callback handling, and settlement reporting — has not yet been confirmed. This must be checked with Nixpend before FR-A6 implementation begins; it directly affects checkout UX and go-live timeline.

Risk: Mi Health (Xiaomi) has no confirmed public third-party API. If unavailable, B9 ships with Apple Health, Samsung Health/Health Connect, and WHOOP only — do not commit Mi Health to the client until verified.

Risk: Garmin Connect and Samsung Health's own Partner Apps Program were reportedly not accepting new applicants as of last check (unconfirmed beyond forum signals) — verify directly with each vendor before relying on this for B9 beyond the Health Connect route.

Assumption: "Companion quadrant is empty in the GCC" remains true at launch. Sehhaty (HealthKit/CareKit steps sync) and the Sehhaty×WHOOP national-platform partnership exist but are narrower than Vitality's proposed depth — re-verify competitive claims before final go-to-market copy.
