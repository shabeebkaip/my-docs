01 · The Brief

Project overview & our understanding

Vitality has asked us to design and build its patient-centric mobile app — a single digital journey for home and virtual healthcare that takes a patient from Discover → Select → Book → Pay → Receive Care → Follow Up → Rebook, across five service categories: Medical Care, Women & Family, Wellness & Recovery, Home Aesthetics, and Memberships & Packages.

Code-Ox brings established product strategy and prior delivery experience with Vitality to this engagement, enabling the project to move directly from the client's BRD into design and build. This proposal sets out the complete scope, platform integrations, commercial terms and delivery timeline for the Vitality mobile application.

02 · Objectives

What this build sets out to achieve

A recovery companion, not a marketplace
A named, consistent caregiver and a visible recovery journey — the first in the region to pair booking convenience with genuine care continuity.
Everything in-app
Discovery, booking, in-app payment and visit-day tracking — no redirect out to a third-party portal.
Family-inclusive
One account manages care for multiple family members, with privacy controls that respect the patient.
Culturally native
Arabic-first, gender-guaranteed caregiver matching, notifications scheduled around prayer times.
Connected to what's live
Native Nixpend HIS, plus WhatsApp and voice/call channels reused rather than rebuilt — one conversation, one voice line.
Measurable
An admin dashboard reporting the client's own KPIs — bookings, conversion, retention, satisfaction.

04 · The Differentiator

A recovery companion, not a booking marketplace

Every home-healthcare app in the region — including the market leader Sehhaty and the closest competitor Sanar — is built as a transactional booking marketplace: pick a service, pick a slot, pay. None builds a relationship with the patient during recovery. That gap is Vitality's moat, and premium home care is what makes it possible.

PillarWhat it means, delivered in the home
Named, consistent caregiverA real person — matched on gender (hard guarantee), language and care-style — revealed by name, who returns across the whole care plan. A clinic cannot promise the same face at your door each visit; home care can.
A visible recovery journeySessions done vs. remaining, rehab goals in real-life terms ("walking without support"), and before/after outcome charts — motivation the patient sees, and the reason packages renew.
Care that reaches into the homeA daily recovery plan with guided exercises, Arabic voiceover and a "how did that feel" check-in — extending care into the days between visits that a clinic never sees.
Family that stays involvedA Family Care Circle lets a son or daughter book, pay and follow milestones for a parent from their own phone — while the patient's private notes stay private.
Culturally native by defaultArabic-first, gender-guaranteed matching, and care-only notifications scheduled around prayer times.
Why this positioning wins

Vitality becomes the first in the region to pair booking convenience with a genuine recovery-companion experience — a named caregiver, a visible journey, and a family kept in the loop. A real, currently-unoccupied market position, not a nicer interface on the same transactional idea.

Dependency & risk — confirm before build

Every screen that touches Nixpend — real-time availability, booking-write and in-app payment — is built against Nixpend's API, never a redirect out to its own interface. Confirm up front that Nixpend exposes an availability API, a booking-write API, and a payment API / gateway-token flow. Should Nixpend not expose a payment API, Code-Ox will deliver a baseline in-app gateway integration so the launch holds on schedule — the payment contract is the one hard dependency to confirm before build.

UX Patterns We're Adopting
  • Map-pin-first home booking — pin location as step one; saved addresses are the shortcut for repeat patients, not the default.
  • Insurance eligibility with an honest cash fallback — check the moment a payer is selected; never dead-end a patient without coverage.
  • One profile, two care modes — a single caregiver/clinician profile serves both in-person home visits and virtual consultations from one booking flow.
  • Digital patient card — a persistent record identifier without a full patient portal.

Recommended lead metric: care-plan completion rate — the strongest signal that the recovery-journey features are working, not just the booking flow.
