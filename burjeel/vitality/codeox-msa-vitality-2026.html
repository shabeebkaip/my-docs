1 Definitions and Interpretation

In this Agreement, capitalised terms shall have the meanings set out below, unless the context otherwise requires:

"AIWA" means the AI-driven WhatsApp / messaging agent platform provided, configured and managed by Code-ox Technologies, including the associated agent logic, prompts, orchestration workflows, conversation memory, classifiers and supporting configurations as deployed for the Client.
"AIKA" means the AI-driven outbound and inbound voice calling agent platform provided, configured and managed by Code-ox Technologies, including the associated agent logic, prompts, voice routing, transcription pipelines and post-call automation as deployed for the Client.
"Unified Dashboard" means the operational single-pane dashboard provided by Code-ox Technologies as part of the Services, covering live conversations and calls across AIWA and AIKA, booking pipeline, therapist availability, campaign performance, coordinator inbox for escalations, and export / reporting functions.
"Code-ox IP" means all IP rights in Code-ox Technologies' configurations, customisations, workflows, prompt engineering, integrations and deployment artefacts created in connection with the Services, excluding the underlying Technology Platforms.
"Technology Platforms" means the underlying AI, telephony and messaging platforms used to power AIWA and AIKA, made available via Code-ox Technologies' confidential technology partnerships.
"Client Data" means data provided by, or generated on behalf of, the Client in the course of the Services, including patient records shared by the Client, conversation transcripts arising from Client end-users, and operational logs limited to the Client's instance.
"Credits" means the included annual entitlement of (a) AIWA messages and (b) AIKA voice minutes set out in Annexure B.
"Deliverables" means the configured AIWA and AIKA instances, integrations and supporting artefacts described in Annexure A.
"Go-Live" means the date on which the Deliverables are made available to the Client's end-users in a production environment following successful completion of integration and configuration.
"Intellectual Property Rights" or "IPR" means all patents, copyrights, database rights, trademarks, service marks, trade secrets, know-how, moral rights, design rights, and all other rights of a similar nature, whether registered or unregistered, anywhere in the world, together with all applications, renewals and extensions thereof.
"Overage" means any usage of AIWA messages or AIKA voice minutes in excess of the included Credits during the Term, which is funded on a prepaid basis through the purchase of Top-Up Credits at the rates set out in Annexure B.
"Pass-Through Costs" means amounts billed by third parties and re-invoiced to (or borne directly by) the Client, including but not limited to: Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform per-conversation fees, STC and any other telecom SIP termination and per-call charges, SMS gateway subscription and per-message fees, and payment gateway transaction fees. For the avoidance of doubt, AI, LLM, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, voice agent orchestration platform costs, and Saudi-region cloud hosting under fair-use limits, are included in the Annual Licence Fee and borne by Code-ox Technologies as part of the SaaS delivery model.
"Services" means the services described in Annexure A and all related professional, configuration, integration and support services provided by Code-ox Technologies to the Client under this Agreement.
"SOW" or "Statement of Work" means the scope description set out in Annexure A, together with any further written statements of work executed under this Agreement.
"Term" has the meaning given in clause 7.
"Third-Party Services" means any service, platform, library, API or system operated by a party other than Code-ox Technologies that is required for, or used in connection with, the Services, including without limitation Twilio, Meta / WhatsApp Business API, Google Cloud, AWS, Vapi, OpenAI, Google (Gemini), ElevenLabs, Nixpend HMS, payment gateways, SMS gateways and telecom carriers (including STC).

4 Annual Credits, Top-Up and Prepaid Overage
4.1
The Annual Licence includes the Credits set out in Annexure B. The Credits represent the aggregate entitlement of AIWA messages and AIKA voice minutes available to the Client during the Term and are non-transferable, non-exchangeable between products (i.e., AIWA messages cannot be substituted for AIKA minutes or vice versa) and non-refundable.
4.2
Unused Credits at the end of the Term shall expire and shall not carry forward, unless the Client renews the Agreement under clause 7, in which case unused Credits (if any) may be rolled into the renewal period at Code-ox Technologies' discretion.
4.3
Prepaid overage credits. Any usage beyond the included Credits shall be funded by the Client through the prior purchase of additional credit packs ("Top-Up Credits") at the per-unit rates set out in Annexure B. The Services operate on a prepaid model: the Client purchases Top-Up Credits in advance, and the Services consume those Credits as usage occurs. No postpaid or month-end invoicing for overage shall apply.
4.4
When included Credits are depleted, Code-ox Technologies shall notify the Client and the Client shall promptly purchase Top-Up Credits to ensure uninterrupted operation. Code-ox Technologies may also offer auto top-up arrangements on terms separately agreed in writing.
4.5
Top-Up Credits are consumed only after included Credits for the Term are exhausted. Top-Up Credits are valid until the end of the Term and any unused Top-Up Credits at Term expiry are non-refundable.
4.6
Code-ox Technologies maintains usage logs which shall be made available to the Client on request. Such logs shall, save for manifest error, be conclusive evidence of usage for the purpose of computing Credit consumption.
4.7
Code-ox Technologies reserves the right to temporarily throttle, rate-limit or suspend the Services where: (a) included Credits are exhausted and no Top-Up Credits have been purchased; or (b) usage patterns indicate abuse, spam, prohibited content, or breach of Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform policy. Any such throttling or suspension shall not constitute a breach of this Agreement by Code-ox Technologies.

A·1 Solution Overview

Code-ox Technologies shall design, configure and deploy a unified, always-on patient coordination platform for Vitality, spanning voice and WhatsApp, integrated directly with Vitality's Nixpend HMS, and hosted on Saudi-region cloud infrastructure. The platform comprises four operational components:

AIWA
Arabic (Najdi) & English bilingual WhatsApp agent for 24/7 inbound conversations and outbound marketing campaigns.
AIKA
24/7 AI receptionist for inbound calls, therapist confirmation, patient reminders and outbound marketing dialling.
Unified Dashboard
Single pane covering conversations, bookings, therapist status, campaign performance and escalations across AIWA & AIKA.
Integration Layer
Bi-directional connectivity with Nixpend HMS, SMS Gateway, Payment Gateway, Google Maps and Email integrations.

  • ✓24/7 inbound patient conversations in Arabic (Najdi) and English within the same chat

  • ✓Cancellation and reschedule handling over voice with HMS write-back
  • ✓Post-service feedback calls with structured outcome capture
  • ✓Outbound marketing voice campaigns: call list management, scheduling, retry logic, outcome capture per call
(C) Unified Dashboard
  • ✓Single pane covering AIKA and AIWA across all numbers and campaigns
  • ✓Live view of active conversations and ongoing calls
