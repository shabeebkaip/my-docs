Access Requirements · WhatsApp Integration

Connecting the AI Assistant to WhatsApp

What we need, why we need it, and how access stays controlled - for Vitality Home Health Services.

Prepared by Code-ox Technologies · For Burjeel / Vitality review

This document explains the access required to connect an AI assistant to a Vitality WhatsApp number, enabling patients to book home-healthcare appointments over WhatsApp and enabling Vitality to run approved outbound campaigns and reminders. The WhatsApp account and number remain owned by Vitality at all times - we operate the integration on Vitality's behalf, and coordinators can take over any conversation.

What the integration does

  • Inbound: patients message the Vitality WhatsApp number to enquire about services and request appointments. The AI assistant handles the conversation and passes qualified booking requests to Vitality's coordinators.
  • Coordinators confirm the therapist and send the payment link, and can take over any conversation at any time.
  • Outbound: Vitality can run campaigns and reminders - appointment reminders, follow-ups, and marketing messages - using message templates.
  • The AI never operates unsupervised - your team stays in control.

How it connects

We manage the integration through our platform, using Twilio (a Meta-approved WhatsApp Business provider) as the transport to Meta. Message templates are created and submitted from our platform for Meta's approval.

Our Platform→ Twilio (BSP)→ Meta / WhatsApp

What we need from Vitality

  • Admin access to the WhatsApp Business Account for one of our team members - either farisar@code-ox.com or shabeeb.k@code-ox.com is sufficient. This can be scoped to the WhatsApp asset only.
  • The number to be used - we recommend a new number dedicated to the AI assistant; an existing number is also fine.
    Why a dedicated number: it keeps the AI booking and campaign line separate from Vitality's existing staff lines, gives a clean sender identity for patients, protects the existing number's quality rating from any campaign activity, and makes analytics and future changes simpler to manage.
  • A verified Meta Business Account - required by Meta before going live.

Why admin access is required

WhatsApp Business setup cannot be completed with view-only access. Admin rights are needed specifically to:

  • Register the phone number with our WhatsApp provider (Twilio).
  • Create and submit the message templates required for appointment confirmations, reminders, and approved campaigns.
  • Configure the number's profile and connection settings.

These are one-time setup actions. Once complete, the service runs on its own secure connection, and the admin access can be reviewed or reduced.

Exactly what this access allows - and what it does not

Our team CAN
  • Register the WhatsApp number for the integration
  • Create and manage WhatsApp message templates
  • Configure the WhatsApp number's settings and connection
Our team CANNOT
  • Access any patient records in Vitality's or Burjeel's systems
  • Access other Meta assets - Facebook pages, Instagram, or ad accounts
  • View or change billing, or add or remove other users (when scoped to the WhatsApp asset)

How access stays controlled

  • The account and number remain Vitality's - access can be revoked at any time.
  • Scope is limited - access is confined to the WhatsApp asset, not the wider business account.
  • Vitality controls the messaging - campaigns, reminders, and templates are all run on Vitality's behalf and under Vitality's direction.
  • Reversible - once setup is complete, admin access can be reduced without affecting the running service.

In summary: we need admin access to the WhatsApp Business Account for one of our team members (either Faris or Shabeeb is sufficient) - scoped to WhatsApp only - to register the number and set up the message templates for inbound booking and outbound reminders and campaigns. The account stays Vitality's, the access is limited and reversible, and no patient or clinical systems are involved.
