The PhysioTrio team runs WhatsApp campaigns to its patients across both branches. When a patient replies, an AI assistant answers in their language and passes anyone ready to book to the coordinators, with the full conversation. The platform and features are the same on both packages.

Terms of working together

Written to be fair to both teams: what each side provides, how pricing and billing work, and how either side can change course.

  1. Working together. Each team names one contact person. Code-ox runs the set-up, platform and support. PhysioTrio provides the WhatsApp number, contact lists, campaign briefs and template approvals, normally within 5 working days of a request.
  2. Go-live. The module goes live in week 8 from signing, following the timeline in this quotation, once the access listed under its dependencies is in place. If the eCare integration cannot be done as planned, go-live moves to week 9. If either team needs more time, new dates are agreed together in writing, without penalty to either side.
  3. Prices. Prices are in Saudi Riyal and exclude VAT (15%). The annual licence fee is fixed for its licence year, and bundle prices are fixed while Meta's Saudi WhatsApp rates stay the same.
  4. If Meta changes its rates. Code-ox gives at least 30 days' written notice of any resulting change to message or bundle prices. Before the change applies, PhysioTrio may keep its package, switch package or stop, without any charge for the decision.
  5. Invoices. Set-up and the annual licence are paid 50% on signing and 50% once live. Bundles are invoiced monthly in advance and extra messages with the next invoice. Invoices are due within 15 days.
  6. Messages you have paid for. On Package A, unused messages roll over to the next month. On Package B, the allowance can be used at any time in the licence year. Messages WhatsApp does not charge for are credited back. Paid messages stay available in this way rather than being refunded.
  7. Changing or ending. On Package A, PhysioTrio can change bundle for the next month, or stop with 30 days' written notice. Package B renews yearly, and either team can choose not to renew with 30 days' notice before the renewal date.
  8. Support. Code-ox monitors the platform, keeps it updated and supports the PhysioTrio team Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 to 18:00 Riyadh time. Anything that stops campaigns from sending is handled first.
  9. Data and privacy. PhysioTrio owns its data, contacts and conversations. Data is hosted in Saudi Arabia and handled under the Personal Data Protection Law. If the service ends, Code-ox exports PhysioTrio's data on request and then deletes it. The platform itself remains Code-ox's intellectual property.
  10. WhatsApp policies. Both teams follow Meta's WhatsApp Business policies. PhysioTrio messages only patients who have agreed to hear from it, and Code-ox applies pre-launch checks, auto-pause and opt-outs to protect the numbers' quality.
  11. Arranged by PhysioTrio. WhatsApp numbers and SIMs, Meta verification documents and any paid advertising. Access to Nixpend and eCare, with their documentation and test environments.
  12. Fair limits. Neither team is responsible for delays caused by events outside its control, such as a Meta service outage or policy change. Each team's liability is limited to the fees paid in the previous 12 months, excluding indirect losses.
  13. Confidentiality and law. Both teams keep each other's information confidential. Any disagreement is first settled amicably between the two managements. This quotation is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
