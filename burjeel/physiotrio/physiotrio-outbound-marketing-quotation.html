Overview
Executive Summary
Code-ox Technologies is pleased to submit this commercial quotation for the PhysioTrio Outbound Marketing Module. The module lets the PhysioTrio team run WhatsApp campaigns in Arabic and English to its patients across both branches, with an AI assistant that answers replies around the clock and passes interested patients to the booking coordinators with the full conversation.

The platform is the same on both packages. PhysioTrio can choose a monthly plan with a message bundle each month, or an annual licence that includes a year of messages, hosting and maintenance.
