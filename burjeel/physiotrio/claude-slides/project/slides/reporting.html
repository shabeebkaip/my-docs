PhysioTrio · Outbound Marketing Module

12

Every booking is credited to the campaign that produced it, including later bookings within 30 days. Results are live while a campaign runs, with a searchable contact list, filtered CSV and Excel exports, a printable management report and a campaigns dashboard with a "needs attention" view. Costs sit in separate wallets for marketing messages, call minutes and service messages, with an estimate before launch, a block if credits won't cover a campaign, and real cost per campaign and per booking. For calls, credits are reserved per call and charged by real minutes, unanswered calls are free, minutes can be kept back for priority calls, and results are shown day by day.
