PhysioTrio · Outbound Marketing Module

06

The AI voice agent places outbound calls in Arabic or English, with a separate agent per language, and works only from PhysioTrio's campaign brief, so it never invents offers. Each call's outcome is recorded: interested, call back requested, wants a WhatsApp message, not interested, or do not call. Calling hours, days off, calls at the same time and retries are all set by the team, and test calls can be made before and after launch. "Do not call me" is respected in future campaigns, and a call opt-out does not block WhatsApp, or the other way round.
