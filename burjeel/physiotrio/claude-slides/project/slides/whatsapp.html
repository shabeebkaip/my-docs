PhysioTrio · Outbound Marketing Module

03

Templates are built in the dashboard as text, text with buttons, or image with text, in Arabic or English, with a phone-style preview, and submitted to WhatsApp for approval from the same screen with the status tracked automatically. Editing is safe: the approved version keeps sending while the edited version is reviewed, and the team is alerted if WhatsApp pauses, rejects or disables a template. Each blank is filled per contact from name, phone, any uploaded field or fixed text; launch is blocked until every blank is set, and contacts missing a value are counted and skipped. The preview is illustrative.
