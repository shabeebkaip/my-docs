Contact lists are imported as CSV or Excel files of up to 50,000 rows (25 MB), including Arabic text, for example a patient list exported from Nixpend. One check screen matches columns automatically, keeps extra fields such as city, service or insurer, and lets fields be renamed. Phone numbers are cleaned from local formats, Arabic digits and international codes, Excel-damaged numbers are detected, and every unusable row comes with a reason and a download to fix it. Existing contacts are recognised, duplicates removed, and opted-out contacts are never contacted.
