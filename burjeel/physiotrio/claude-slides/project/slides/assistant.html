PhysioTrio · Outbound Marketing Module

04

The AI assistant replies 24/7, within seconds, to everyone who answers a campaign. It answers only from PhysioTrio's campaign brief, so it never invents prices, dates or promises, and replies in Arabic or English to match the contact. Interested leads are handed to the booking team by contact card or link; the hand-over wording is editable in both languages with a test send. When a handed-over lead writes to the main number, that assistant knows which campaign and offer they came from. The brief can be edited while the campaign runs, and if the AI has a problem the conversation passes to a person. The conversation shown is illustrative.
