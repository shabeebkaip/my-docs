Hidden charges

Costs that are easy to miss when pricing. The two marked high can move the margin by several points on their own.

Meta and WhatsApp

  1. Marketing rate rises HighSaudi marketing went from $0.0501 to $0.0576 on 1 Oct 2026, +15%. Meta revises rates quarterly.Keep the "prices follow Meta's rates" clause in every quotation.
  2. Service replies now billed HighReported from 1 Oct 2026 at about SAR 0.04 each. Up to SAR 2,000 a year on the annual licence.Confirm on our Meta billing in the first month.
  3. Provider fees if not direct Medium360dialog, Twilio and similar charge per number or add a markup per message. Package B allows 5 numbers.Connect to Meta's Cloud API directly.
  4. Tests and retries countMeta bills every delivered message, including test sends and failover retries that land.
  5. Blocked or low-quality numberPatient reports can restrict a number; replacing it costs a SIM, re-verification and time.

AI

  1. Arabic uses more tokensRoughly 1.5–2× the tokens of English. Long conversations add more history to every reply.Cache the campaign brief; cap history length.
  2. More than one call per replyIntent check, hand-over summary and opt-out detection each add a call.
  3. Model choiceSonnet 5.5 costs about 12× Haiku 5.5 per reply. Quality in Arabic decides which one we can use.

Hosting

  1. Saudi region costs moreDammam is about 30% dearer than Doha for the same machine. Each client gets a dedicated environment.
  2. Disk, backups, logs, monitoringSSD about $0.27 per GB a month, plus backup storage and log retention.
  3. Staging, SSL, domain, alertsSmall items that recur every month.

Tax, cash and people

  1. Withholding tax on foreign providers Medium5–15% on payments to Meta Ireland and Anthropic.Check with our accountant before pricing.
  2. Reverse-charge VAT15% on the same foreign services. Recoverable, but it ties up cash.
  3. Card fees on USD billingAbout 2–3% when paying by card in USD. Meta offers SAR billing since 1 Apr 2026.
  4. Rollover on Package AUnused messages carry over, so paid messages remain a liability.
  5. Meta bills before we invoiceIf a bundle is used early in the month, Meta charges us before the next invoice.
  6. Staff time and the build itselfSet-up, hypercare, support and maintenance, plus the one-time cost of building the platform.
