Opportunities between visits

Four hypotheses to test with your own data, not assumptions.

We have not seen PhysioTrio's appointment data. Each opportunity below is a question to check during discovery before anything is built around it.

Hypothesis

Missed appointments are not always rebooked

Some patients who miss an appointment may not reschedule on their own.

  • ?Check: share of missed appointments rebooked within 14 days
Hypothesis

Some active plans have gaps

Patients with a current clinician-approved plan may go longer between visits than intended.

  • ?Check: active plans with no upcoming appointment
Hypothesis

Callback requests wait during busy hours

People who ask for booking help may wait while reception handles walk-ins and calls.

  • ?Check: time from request to first contact
Hypothesis

Home-care routing is not always clear

Patients may not know that Makkah home care is PhysioTrio and Riyadh home care is Vitality.

  • ?Check: home-care enquiries by city and where they end up

One patient journey

A missed appointment, handled with care.

A patient on a current, clinician-approved plan misses a session. The patient has agreed to receive messages. Here is what happens, and where it stops.

Who is eligible is set with the clinical team. Discharged, paused, expired or opted-out patients are excluded before anything is sent.
1

Eligibility check

Missed appointment, active plan, consent on file, preferred language known.

2

One WhatsApp message

A neutral, approved template offering help to rearrange. No clinical details.

3

Patient replies "yes"

Preference captured, such as evenings, and a booking request is created for reception.

4

Reception confirms

Reception books the slot in the clinic system and the patient receives a confirmation.

↺

If there is no reply

Optionally, one clearly identified AI call after an agreed interval. No answer means no further contact in this cycle.

✓

Measured by attendance

Success is the attended appointment, not the message or the call.

05 / 14

AI calls · optional channel

A transparent, helpful call for patients who prefer the phone.

The call says it is an automated assistant from the first sentence and always offers a person. It handles administration only.

It does

  • ✓Ask if it is a good time
  • ✓Answer timings and location
  • ✓Capture booking interest or a callback
  • ✓Log the outcome

It never

  • ✕Diagnoses or advises on symptoms
  • ✕Prescribes exercises
  • ✕Decides treatment is needed
  • ✕Claims a slot or confirms a booking

06 / 14

Coordinated follow-up

Every reply has an owner.

The module starts the conversation. People at PhysioTrio decide and confirm. Each step has a named owner, agreed before the pilot starts.

The module

Sends approved messages and calls, captures interest and preferences, and creates booking or callback requests.

Reception

Works the request queue per branch, checks capacity, books in the clinic system and confirms with the patient.

Care team

Defines who is eligible and answers any clinical question under an approved protocol.

Operations & marketing

Approve message wording, audiences, contact limits and the campaign calendar.

Contact limits are set per patient across both channels. An unread message never triggers repeated calls.

08 / 14

Learning from results

Improve carefully, without over-contacting patients.

Each cycle is reviewed before the next one runs. Changes are kept small, so it is clear what made a difference.

Compare against a baseline

Results are compared with the period before, or with a similar group not contacted, before any expansion.

Frequency caps

A maximum number of contacts per patient per month, across WhatsApp and calls combined.

Clear stop rules

Opt-out, a booked appointment, discharge or no response ends the sequence for that patient.

Re-check eligibility

Audiences are rebuilt from current data each cycle, never reused from an old list.

One change at a time

Test one wording or timing change per cycle, on a small group first.

Patient feedback counts

Opt-outs and complaints are reviewed alongside bookings, with equal weight.

Nixpend & data approach

Use the minimum data, confirm access first, keep a fallback.

Nixpend as the source system is a premise to confirm with PhysioTrio and Nixpend. Nothing is assumed to be connected until permissions and fields are verified.

Proposed fields

Only what outreach needs:

First nameMobileLanguageBranchAppointment statusPlan statusConsent & opt-out

Even appointment and plan status can be sensitive, so access is restricted and logged.

Fallback: authorised export

Until an API is confirmed, PhysioTrio can export an approved patient list on a schedule and upload it to the dashboard. This is a working fallback, not a completed integration.

Bookings are always confirmed by reception. No automatic write-back to the clinic system is assumed.

To confirm

  • ●API permissions and terms from Nixpend
  • ●Which fields are available
  • ●Where marketing consent is recorded
  • ●Sync frequency
  • ●Read access to appointments
  • ●Whether write-back is possible later
11 / 14

Trust, privacy & clinical boundaries

Outreach that patients can trust.

These principles are proposed as design rules. Legal and platform requirements are verified with PhysioTrio before launch; no compliance certification is claimed.

Consent & choice

Only patients who have agreed to be contacted, with a simple way to stop at any time.

Honest about AI

Automated messages and calls identify themselves and always offer a person.

Clinical boundaries

Symptoms and clinical questions go to the care team. The AI does not diagnose, prescribe or decide treatment.

Privacy in every preview

No diagnosis, women's health or other sensitive details in message previews, or on calls before the recipient is verified.

Respectful contact

Agreed hours and contact limits, with no repeated calls driven by an unread message.

Data handling

Minimum fields, restricted access and hosting arrangements reviewed against Saudi PDPL with PhysioTrio.
