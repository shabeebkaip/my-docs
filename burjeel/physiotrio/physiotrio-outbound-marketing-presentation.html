Why Outbound, Why Now

Recovery happens over many sessions. Patients drift between them.

physiotrio.sa and its WhatsApp assistant already serve patients who come to PhysioTrio. Outbound reaches the patients PhysioTrio already knows, at the right moment in their care, without the front desk working the phones for days.

Plans left unfinished

A patient feels better after a few sessions and stops coming before the plan is complete.

Patients who go quiet

Past patients who could benefit from a review or maintenance session are never contacted again.

Phone time is expensive

Reminder and follow-up calls take hours of staff time that is better spent with patients in the clinic.

New services need an audience

Home visits, new specialties and new branches launch to the patients who already trust PhysioTrio.

WhatsApp Campaigns

Send on their own. Add a reply agent when you want one.

Campaign broadcasts

Reminders, seasonal offers, new services and health tips sent to thousands of patients with Meta-approved templates.

Patient segments

Target by branch, service line, language, last visit, or how a patient last engaged, so the right list gets the right message.

Personalised at scale

Each message carries the patient's name, branch and next step, while still going out as one campaign.

Optional AI reply agent

Turn it on and every reply is answered instantly. Leave it off and replies go to your team's inbox.

Automatic follow-ups

No reply after a set time triggers a gentle reminder, so interested patients are not lost in a busy inbox.

Rich media

Images, short exercise videos, documents and quick-reply buttons, in the formats WhatsApp supports.

05 / 14

AI Outbound Calling

A voice that calls, so your team does not have to.

Automated call campaigns

Select a segment and the voice agent dials through it, delivering the same clear message to every patient.

Speaks the patient's language

Natural Arabic and English voices, so the call sounds like a person, not a recording.

Real conversations

Patients can ask about timings, branches or home visits. The agent answers, confirms interest and captures the details.

Every outcome logged

Interested, call back later, wrong number or not now. Each result is recorded for the next step.

Warm handoff to WhatsApp

An interested patient receives a WhatsApp message straight after the call, with location and booking details.

Calling windows & retries

Calls are placed only within agreed hours, and unanswered numbers are retried on a set schedule.

Patient Trust & Compliance

Outreach that feels like care, not spam.

Healthcare outreach has to protect patient trust. These rules are built into every campaign.

Consent first

Only patients who have agreed to receive messages are contacted, in line with Saudi PDPL and WhatsApp's business policy.

One-tap opt-out

A patient who asks to stop, by message or on a call, is removed from every future campaign automatically.

No clinical details in messages

Messages and calls mention sessions and appointments, never diagnosis or treatment details.

Respectful timing

Calls and messages go out only within agreed hours, with limits on how often one patient is contacted.

Approved templates

Every WhatsApp campaign message uses a template approved by Meta and signed off by PhysioTrio.

Human handoff

Any patient who wants a person, or raises a clinical question, is passed to the PhysioTrio team.

Open Questions

What we need to confirm before the financial proposal.

Nixpend & patient data
  • ●Can Nixpend provide API access to patient contact, appointment and visit fields, and on what terms?
  • ●Is marketing consent recorded for existing patients today, and where?
  • ●Which branches are in scope at launch: Riyadh and Makkah, and Dammam when it opens?
Channels & volumes
  • ●Use the existing PhysioTrio WhatsApp number, or a dedicated number for campaigns?
  • ●Which phone number should calls show as the caller ID?
  • ●Expected monthly volume: patients messaged and patients called
  • ●Who handles hot leads and booking requests: reception, call centre or each branch?

WhatsApp message charges from Meta and telecom call charges are usage costs. They will be listed separately in the financial proposal.
