Page-by-Page Audit Findings
Homepage (pcabins.com)
Mixed — Critical Fixes Needed
Professional bilingual layout — clean grid-based homepage with clear bilingual navigation (EN/AR toggle in header). Professional typography and brand-consistent golden color scheme.
Strong hero tagline — "تجربة شاملة ومتفوقة في تصنيع الغرف الجاهزة والكبائن والكرفانات المتنقلة" clearly communicates the value proposition in Arabic above the fold.
6 product categories with real imagery — Steel Structures, Prefabricated Houses, Ready Rooms, Ready Cabins, Prefab Buildings, and Containers all shown with real photographs. Good visual product overview.
Social media presence — Instagram (@pcabinsfactory), Snapchat (@pcabinsfactory), TikTok (@portale.house.factory), and Facebook all linked. Phone +966 54 745 3225 and WhatsApp both present.
Mobile LCP of 19.9 seconds — catastrophically slow. While desktop scores 92/100, mobile performance is 65/100 with LCP 19.9s and FCP 3.6s. Render-blocking requests (saving 900ms possible) and unoptimized images (410 KiB potential saving) identified by PageSpeed.
No above-the-fold B2B CTA — no "Request a Quote" button, no product catalog download, no ISO certification badge bar. Primary call to action relies on visitors finding the "Contact Us" button in the nav.
Accessibility 87/100 — minor issues flagged. Some contrast ratios and ARIA attributes need attention to reach 95+.
Products Pages — 6 Categories
Photos Exist — Content Missing
Real product photography present — all 6 product categories (Steel Structures, Prefab Houses, Ready Rooms, Ready Cabins, Prefab Buildings, Containers) display real product photos. Good visual presentation of the product range.
Cross-sell section on each product page — every individual product page shows all 6 product categories below (e.g. Steel Structures page shows links to Prefab Houses, Ready Rooms, etc.). Good for internal navigation.
Values communicated — product pages show Excellence, Quality, Accuracy, Innovation pillars. Sets professional tone for B2B buyers.
Zero body copy on product pages — each product page has a title, a hero image, and a "Contact Us" button — that's it. No description of the product, use cases, specifications, customization options, or materials. Google cannot rank empty pages and buyers cannot self-qualify.
No pricing or price ranges — B2B buyers need at least indicative price ranges to qualify their budget before contacting. "Contact Us" for all products with no price guidance significantly reduces inquiry-to-lead conversion rates.
No technical specifications — dimensions, materials, weight capacity, delivery options, customization options not listed for any product. Procurement officers at Aramco, airports, or developers need specs before issuing RFQs.
CTA is only "Contact Us" — no per-product quote form, no downloadable spec sheet, no "See similar projects" link. Single CTA path with no alternatives.
Services Page
Partial — Missing Key Information
Services clearly listed — design, execution, transport, and maintenance are all mentioned as service pillars.
No service process diagram — how the order-to-delivery process works is unclear. B2B buyers need to understand: how long does it take? What are the steps?
No service area map or coverage — does PCabins deliver across all of KSA? Only Riyadh? This is critical information for corporate procurement.
Projects / Portfolio Page
Missed Opportunity — Clients Named, No Proof
Client names mentioned — Aramco, Riyadh Airports Company referenced as clients. This is excellent social proof — but not shown in detail.
No case study content — no "16 successful projects" with actual project photos, scope, timeline, or client testimonials. Just the number. This is a massive wasted opportunity.
No before/after photos — for a manufacturer, project photos are the primary sales tool. None exist on the site.
Contact Page
OK — But Missing Quote Form
Full contact information — address (7424 Al-Malaz, Adam Bin Rabea Street, Riyadh), phone (+966 54 745 3225), email (info@pcabins.com), WhatsApp all present.
Social media links — Instagram (@pcabinsfactory), Snapchat (@pcabinsfactory), TikTok (@portale.house.factory), Facebook all linked on contact page.
No online quote request form — B2B buyers at corporations cannot submit a formal quote request online. This is a significant barrier for corporate procurement which requires documented communication.
No Google Maps embed — physical factory location not shown on map. Visitors cannot easily navigate to the showroom.

⚠ Mobile LCP of 19.9 Seconds — Critical Issue
A Largest Contentful Paint of 19.9 seconds on mobile means users wait nearly 20 seconds before the main content appears. Saudi Arabia has high smartphone usage — most B2B initial browsing happens on mobile. PageSpeed identified render-blocking requests (estimated 900ms savings) and image delivery improvements (410 KiB potential savings) as the primary causes. Desktop loads in 1.7s LCP — so this is a mobile-specific optimization problem. Fix: optimize and lazy-load images, eliminate render-blocking CSS/JS, use a CDN.

Key Competitive Insight
PCabins has genuine competitive advantages no competitor can match: 20+ years experience, triple ISO certification, and Aramco/Airport client references — plus real product photography already in place. However, these advantages are under-leveraged online: no Google reviews, no specs on product pages, and a catastrophic 19.9s mobile LCP. Beit Al-Majd is winning on Google with 62 reviews despite weaker credentials. Fix the trust signals and performance and PCabins leads the market.
Top 10 Opportunities
Ranked by Lead Impact & Priority
#1 Critical
Fix Mobile LCP from 19.9s → Under 3s
Mobile LCP of 19.9s means most mobile visitors see a blank screen for 20 seconds — a near-total abandonment event. Fix render-blocking requests, convert product images to WebP with lazy-load, and enable server-side caching. Desktop is already excellent (1.7s).
Lead Impact: Very High · Effort: Medium · Timeline: 1–2 weeks
#2 Critical
Add Quote Request Form to Every Page
No online quote form means B2B procurement officers cannot submit formal inquiries. Add a form with fields: company name, project type, quantity, delivery location, timeline. This alone can 3x inbound leads.
Lead Impact: Very High · Effort: Low · Timeline: 1 week

Lead Impact: High · Effort: Medium · Timeline: 2–3 weeks
#5 High

Create a prominent trust strip showing ISO 9001, 14001, 45001 badges with downloadable PDF certificates. This is PCabins' single biggest differentiator vs. competitors — it's currently invisible.
