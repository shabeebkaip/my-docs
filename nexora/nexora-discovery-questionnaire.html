Market Research

Six marketplaces already solve pieces of this problem. None solve all of it.

Fiverr is the closest thing most people picture when they hear "marketplace" — but it's a generalist, gig-based product, not an AI-specific, RFP-driven B2B platform. The table below compares the platforms whose mechanics NEXORA is closest to.

The gap NEXORA sits in

No platform researched combines all of: an AI-specific taxonomy, a full RFP → shortlist → compare → contract flow (Upwork's core), escrow-style payment protection, tiered verification rigor (Clutch's), and — notably — nobody researched runs a double-blind mutual rating system. That mechanism, already scoped into NEXORA's MVP, is a genuine differentiator.
