Overview
Executive Summary
Code-ox Technologies is pleased to submit this financial proposal for Phase 1 of the NEXORA platform — the B2B marketplace connecting companies to verified AI solution providers. This document reflects the Core MVP scope confirmed in the NEXORA Platform Presentation: a scalable architecture and a launchable MVP that validates the NEXORA business model, ahead of every advanced feature.

To remove any ambiguity, every feature module included in Phase 1 is listed in full on the following pages, alongside the fixed price and a week-by-week delivery timeline. Optional Enhancements (online payments, analytics, self-serve browse) and Future Phase items (AI matching engine, subscriptions, escrow, native apps) are explicitly excluded from this price and available as a follow-on engagement.

05AI-Assisted Request ClassificationFull
For customers who'd rather describe their problem in their own words than fill in every field, free text is passed to an external AI service and turned into a structured brief automatically.
Free-text input — "I want to reduce time employees spend responding to customers" AI classification via external LLM API Suggested category & solution type Follow-up questions asked only when needed Customer confirms → structured request created
Simplified for the MVP — a proprietary AI model is explicitly not required.
06Admin Request ReviewFull
A quality gate before matching runs. NEXORA staff sanity-check every structured request — catching incomplete or miscategorised submissions before any provider ever sees them.
Structured request reviewed before release to matching Category management Request status set to Under Review → Matched
07Rule-Based Provider Matching EngineFull
Every eligible, verified provider is scored against the request using transparent weighted criteria, then NEXORA staff review the ranked shortlist before invitations go out — explainable by design, no black-box model at this stage.
Service match Industry fit Verification status Rating Budget alignment Technology match Experience Location Response time history Weights configurable per category by NEXORA admin
Explainable rule-based ranking. A future AI matching engine is a Future Phase item.
08Invitations & Proposal SubmissionFull
The matched shortlist is notified and responds individually — accepting, declining, or submitting a full proposal — so one provider's response never blocks another's.
"You have a new request that matches your services" notification Accept / Decline / Submit Proposal per invitation Proposal: solution, price, delivery timeline, scope of work Support terms, warranty, payment terms, attachments, notes

13Double-Blind Mutual RatingsFull
Four rating directions build the NEXORA Trust Layer. Neither side sees the other's rating until both have submitted, removing the incentive for retaliatory or courtesy scores.
Customer → Provider (delivery quality, timeliness, value for money) Provider → Customer (Client Reputation Score — clarity, cooperation, payment commitment) Both sides → NEXORA (platform experience & marketplace fairness) Ratings hidden until both parties submit, then unlock together Only completed-project participants can rate; fake/abusive ratings reportable
14Three Role DashboardsFull
Each role gets a purpose-built control centre showing only what it needs day to day — nothing shared, nothing cluttered with another role's workflow.
Customer — requests, proposals, compare, messages, projects, ratings Provider — new requests, proposals, active projects, revenue, ratings Admin — users, providers, requests, transactions, reviews, analytics
15NotificationsFull
Every state change in the marketplace reaches the right person on the right channel — a request received, a new proposal, a new message, or a provider selection.
Email SMS In-platform
Push notifications are a Future Phase item.
16Bilingual Platform & Security FoundationFull
Arabic and English are both first-class from day one — layout, navigation, and forms mirror correctly in RTL and LTR — backed by the baseline security controls any real marketplace needs before launch.
Arabic & English, RTL/LTR both first-class SSL/TLS, password encryption, OTP verification Role-based access control API security, scheduled backups, admin audit logging

What Happens Next
Beyond Phase 1 — The Product Roadmap
Phase 1 is the Core MVP — enough to launch NEXORA and prove the business model with real customers and providers. Once it's live, two further tiers of work extend the platform. Neither is priced into this proposal; each is scoped and quoted separately once NEXORA decides to move on it.
Phase 2 Optional Enhancements 6 items
  • Online Payment GatewayBrings payment on-platform instead of staying off-platform after a provider is selected.
  • Basic Analytics DashboardsUsage, conversion, and revenue reporting for the NEXORA admin team.
  • Self-Serve Provider Browse & SearchA full filterable directory customers can search directly, beyond guided requests.
  • Provider Apply-to-RequestCustomers can mark a request "Open to Applications" so providers apply, not just get invited.
  • Multi-Level Provider VerificationTiered badges — Bronze / Silver / Gold / Platinum — instead of one "Verified" flag.
  • Push NotificationsNative mobile-style push alerts alongside email, SMS, and in-platform notifications.
Phase 3+ Future Phases 8 items
  • AI Matching EngineReplaces rule-based scoring with a model trained on real match outcomes over time.
  • Provider Subscription TiersA recurring revenue layer for NEXORA — visibility only, never affects matching score.
  • Promoted / Sponsored PlacementPaid visibility boosts for providers, under the same visibility-only guardrail.
  • Escrow-Protected PaymentsFunds held in escrow until delivery milestones are confirmed by both sides.
  • Contract & SOW Template LibraryStandard agreements customers and providers can generate directly on-platform.
  • Milestone-Based Project TrackingContracted projects broken into trackable milestones with status per stage.
  • WhatsApp / CRM / Calendar IntegrationsConnects the platform to the tools NEXORA's team and providers already use daily.
  • iOS & Android Native AppsDedicated mobile apps beyond the responsive web platform delivered in Phase 1.
Ready When You Are

None of the above is a commitment today. Once Phase 1 is live, NEXORA will have real usage data to decide what's worth building next — we'll scope and price whichever items matter most at that point, starting with a short discovery call.

Terms & Conditions
General Terms (1 of 2)
Written to work both ways — clear commitments from Code-ox, clear commitments from NEXORA, with genuine notice periods and review windows on either side.
  • Scope of Work: This proposal covers the 16 Core MVP feature modules listed in Section 1. Optional Enhancements and Future Phase items shown in the NEXORA Platform Presentation are out of scope for this price and will be quoted separately.
  • Payment Terms: Invoices are due within 7 days of issuance against each milestone in the Payment Schedule. If a payment runs more than 14 days overdue, Code-ox may pause work after written notice; the 12-week timeline extends by the length of the pause, and resumes once payment clears.
  • Scope Changes: Small additions during the project are welcome and batched into a fortnightly review. Anything under 1 day is absorbed at no cost. Anything larger is estimated within 2 business days and either scheduled without slipping the 12-week deadline, or queued into a follow-on phase — NEXORA decides which.
  • Client Responsibilities: NEXORA agrees to provide brand assets, the initial category list, and timely feedback within agreed deadlines. Code-ox will flag any missing input in writing within 2 business days of it becoming a blocker, rather than letting the timeline slip silently.
  • Acceptance & Sign-off: Each milestone is demoed to NEXORA and open for written feedback for 5 business days. Issues raised in that window are fixed before moving on; if no feedback arrives, the milestone is deemed accepted and work proceeds to the next stage.
  • Revisions: The design phase (Weeks 1–4) includes up to two (2) rounds of revisions, each reviewed within the 5-business-day window above. Additional revision rounds are charged separately at an agreed rate.
  • Third-Party Costs: External LLM API usage fees, SMS gateway fees, hosting/cloud infrastructure, and domain registration are not included and remain NEXORA's responsibility and choice of provider.
  • Data Protection: Any personal data NEXORA provides, or that flows through the platform during development and testing, is handled in line with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and used solely to build and test the platform — never shared beyond the integrations NEXORA has approved.
