The Problem

AI supply is exploding. Buying it responsibly is not getting easier.

Companies know they need AI. What they lack is a safe, structured way to find the right provider — and providers lack a credible channel to reach demand that is actually ready to buy.

BuyerProvider

Buyers can't evaluate AI vendors

No shared vocabulary, no comparable pricing, no way to verify a provider's real track record before committing budget.

Providers chase unqualified leads

Time is spent on proposals for buyers who were never ready — no visibility into a customer's seriousness or intent.

No neutral trust layer exists

Reviews are scattered, self-reported, or absent. Neither side has a reliable signal of how the other behaves in a project.

This is a structural gap, not a temporary one — AI vendors are multiplying faster than any single buyer can vet them one by one.

Why Now

The timing lines up on both sides of the market.

Directional market context — not sourced statistics. These are the trends shaping the window NEXORA is building into.

AI adoption is a national priority

Saudi Vision 2030 explicitly pushes enterprise AI and digital transformation, creating sustained institutional demand for AI services across sectors.

Supply is fragmenting fast

AI agencies, freelancers, and specialist studios are multiplying across the region — with no shared way for buyers to compare or verify them.

No dedicated player owns this gap

General marketplaces (Fiverr, Upwork) and curated networks (Toptal, Catalant) each solve part of the problem — none are AI-specific, RFP-driven, and KSA-first. See the landscape next.

06 / 12

Business Model

A marketplace with a layered monetization path.

Core revenue is transaction-based, with two additional layers introduced once volume justifies them. Exact commission rates are being finalized against market benchmarks (Upwork: 0–15% provider / 3–10% customer; Fiverr: flat 20% seller-side).

Core · Launch

Transaction commission

A percentage of completed project value, charged to one or both sides of the marketplace. Rate under final review.

Phase 2

Provider subscriptions & promoted placement

Recurring revenue layer for visibility — explicitly never affects matching score, protecting trust in the core product.

Future

Escrow-protected payments

Platform-held funds for higher-value projects, unlocking a payments take-rate once transaction volume supports it.

Why NEXORA Wins

Defensibility comes from trust, not features.

Individual features are copyable. A two-sided reputation record built transaction by transaction is not — four pillars compound around a single trust core.

Double-Blind
Verified Supply
Explainable Match
Compounding Data
TRUSTCORE

Double-blind reputation

Customer and provider rate each other only after both submit — removing the retaliation and grade-inflation problems that make most marketplace reviews unreliable.

Verified-only supply

Every provider is reviewed by NEXORA before going live, publicly marked "Verified by NEXORA" — a trust signal buyers can act on immediately.

Explainable matching

Rule-based, weighted matching that both sides can understand — not a black box — building confidence in every shortlist NEXORA produces.

Compounding data advantage

Every request, match, proposal, and rating adds to a record that gets more valuable — and harder to replicate — with every transaction.
