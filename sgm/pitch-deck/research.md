## Conflicts between sources (flag in the deck)

1. **Size of Saudi OOH:** Mordor puts it at USD 0.96bn (2025). IMARC puts it at USD 279m. That is a 3.4x gap.
2. **Size of Saudi DOOH:**
   - Mordor's KSA report implies about USD 526m (54.83% of USD 0.96bn).
   - Mordor's own MEA report implies about USD 120m (33.17% of USD 361m).
   - IMARC says USD 220m, which would be 79% of IMARC's own OOH figure.
   - So Mordor's two reports disagree with each other.
   - Recommendation: quote one firm consistently, and show the range.
3. **DOOH share of OOH:** Mordor says 54.83%. The Campaign ME op-ed says "90%" (no source). Campaign ME's OOH briefing says UAE DOOH is under 10%.
4. **KSA digital ad spend:** DataReportal says USD 1.76bn (2024). The IAB MENA figure works out to about USD 3.77bn (2025). A Research & Markets databook says USD 4.01bn (2025). These likely use different definitions (some include platform spend booked offshore).
5. **Expo 2030 visits:** 42m (Argaam/BIE materials) vs 40m (Saudipedia). Use "40m+".
6. **Riyadh Season:** an Economy Middle East headline says "20m visitors, Riyadh Season 2025". SPA says the 6th edition (2025–26) closed at 17m. Wikipedia: 2023–24 = 20m, 2024–25 = 19m. The latest season came in lower. Quote 17m (SPA) or the multi-season trend.
7. **Market headwind:** Al Arabia's H1 2026 revenue fell 29% because ad spend dropped amid regional geopolitical tension. Address this risk; don't hide it.

## Headline numbers for the slides

**Market slide (pick 3–4)**
1. Saudi OOH+DOOH: **USD 0.96bn (2025) → USD 1.58bn (2031), 8.2% CAGR**. Mordor Intelligence, Feb 2026.
2. Digital is already **~55% of Saudi OOH** (54.83%, 2025). Mordor Intelligence, Feb 2026.
3. Saudi DOOH: **USD 220m (2025) → USD 511m (2034), 9.8% CAGR**. IMARC, Sep 2026 (the conservative case).
4. Saudi Arabia is the **#1 DOOH market in MEA (33% share)**. MEA DOOH is growing **12.8% CAGR to USD 748m by 2031**. Mordor Intelligence, Sep 2026.

**Why now slide (4)**
1. **Expo 2030 Riyadh**, 1 Oct 2030 – 31 Mar 2031, **40m+ visits** expected. Argaam/BIE, Oct 2025.
2. **FIFA World Cup 2034**, Saudi Arabia confirmed sole host on 11 Dec 2024. FIFA Congress, via Al Jazeera.
3. **122m visitors in 2025** (+5%), against a **150m target by 2030**. Ministry of Tourism, via Skift, Jan 2026.
4. **Riyadh Metro: 200m journeys in its first ~15 months** (Dec 2024 – Mar 2026). Riyadh is growing from **7.0m (2022) to a forecast 9.6m (2030)**. RCRC via List Magazine, Mar 2026; Knight Frank, Oct 2024.
