Risks & Flags for the Pre-Sale Call
Read before quoting
  • ⚠ Budget vs scope gap
    < 30K SAR budget against a Day-1 wish list of POS, Accounting, ZATCA Phase 2, CRM, tiered commissions (custom), discount governance (custom), Day-1 loyalty, digital receipts, plus near-term appointments + marketing automation. Realistic Day-1 scope at that price point would be POS + Accounting + ZATCA + CRM + a lightweight commission MVP. Required: reset expectations in the discovery call. Frame as a 3-phase rollout with Phase 1 in-budget.
  • ⚠ ZATCA phase ambiguity
    Owner answered "Not sure" on ZATCA Phase 1 vs Phase 2. Action: on the call, look up their CR/VAT and check the ZATCA wave list. Default the SoW to Phase 2 — cheaper to design for Phase 2 from day 1 than retrofit later.
  • ⚠ ASAP go-live with 20+ staff
    "ASAP" + 20+ staff + brand-new system + custom commission engine + Day-1 loyalty is an aggressive scope for a single big-bang go-live. Recommend a staged cutover: Week 0–3 POS + Accounting only, Week 4–6 add commissions + loyalty, Week 7+ bookings + marketing. Soft-launch commissions with parallel paper run for the first cycle to build stylist trust.
  • ⚠ "Comparing other providers"
    Competitive deal. We need to be specific and fast. Differentiation angle: (1) salon-specific custom modules out-of-the-gate (discount governance + tiered commissions), (2) AIWA WhatsApp connector for receipts/reminders/marketing, (3) Arabic-native team and discovery materials.
  • ℹ No legacy data to migrate
    No customer records, no commission history, no POS data, no retail inventory. Good news: zero migration scope. Clean configuration project. Saves ~1–2 weeks of discovery + import work compared to a typical Odoo project.
  • ℹ Single decision maker
    Mohammed is the sole decision maker. No board/spouse/finance gate. Decision cycle will be short — speed of proposal turn-around matters more than depth of stakeholder mapping.

Open Questions for the Discovery Call
30–45 min agenda
01
Staff breakdown: Of the 20+ total, how many are stylists (commission-earning) vs cashiers vs reception/admin? Drives commission-engine complexity + Odoo user licence count.
02
Commission tiers: What are the actual tiers today? (e.g. 0–5K sales = 10%, 5–10K = 12%, 10K+ = 15%.) Does the tier reset monthly or roll annually?
03
Multi-stylist services: When two stylists work on one customer (e.g. colour + cut), how is the commission split today?
04
Discount limits: What discount % do you want cashiers to give without owner approval? What's the over-limit approval flow — owner only, or also manager?
05
ZATCA wave: Have you received an email/letter from ZATCA about Phase 2 onboarding? Can you share your CR + VAT number so we can verify your wave?
06
Loyalty mechanics: Points-per-SAR? Points-per-visit? What rewards do you envision (free service, % discount, free product future)?
07
Online booking: Where does the booking link live — website, Instagram bio, WhatsApp? Do customers self-pick a stylist or auto-assign?
08
Budget reset: The wish list exceeds the 30K bracket. Walk through a phased plan and confirm Mohammed is open to a 3-phase rollout vs everything-at-once.
09
Internal accountant: Name, experience level with Odoo, comfort with English vs Arabic UI? Drives training scope.
10
Hardware: Existing POS terminals, receipt printers, cash drawers — keep or replace? Number of POS stations needed at peak?
11
Hosting: Odoo Online (Enterprise SaaS) vs Odoo.sh vs self-hosted on local VPS? Customer responses didn't cover this — propose Odoo.sh for the Phase-2 ZATCA requirement.
