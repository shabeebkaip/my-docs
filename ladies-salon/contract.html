01  ·  Preamble
Recitals & Parties

This Implementation Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of 25 June 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between:

Code-OX Technologies LLP, a limited liability partnership duly registered and incorporated under the laws of India, having its principal office at Building No. 3239, Al Zubair Bin Al Awwam Street, Al Rawabi District, Riyadh – 14215, Saudi Arabia (hereinafter referred to as the "Service Provider" or "Code-OX"); and

Angel Hands Beauty Centre, a ladies beauty salon business having its principal place of operation in Saudi Arabia, represented by its owner Mohammed (hereinafter referred to as the "Client" or "Angel Hands").

Code-OX and Angel Hands are each referred to herein individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

WHEREAS, Angel Hands operates an established ladies beauty salon with 20+ staff across one branch in Saudi Arabia, currently managing operations manually — including paper-based commissions, cash-register point of sale, manual discount tracking, no centralised customer database, and Excel-based reporting — and is desirous of implementing a comprehensive Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system to digitise and automate its operations; and

WHEREAS, Code-OX is engaged in the business of ERP implementation, software development and digital products, is an Odoo partner, and has presented the Financial Proposal (ref: COX-LS-FP-2026-001) and the Discovery Response (ref: COX-LS-ODOO-DQ-2026-001), each of which has been accepted by Angel Hands as the basis for this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out herein, the Parties agree as follows.

02  ·  Definitions
Defined Terms
2.1   Services

"Services" means the Odoo 19 Enterprise implementation services to be performed by Code-OX under this Agreement, comprising the six (6) phases described in Schedule A.

2.2   Deliverables

"Deliverables" means all configured Odoo modules, custom development artefacts, data-migration outputs, training materials, documentation, and any other tangible work product produced by Code-OX in performing the Services.

2.3   Go-Live Date

"Go-Live Date" means the date on which the configured Odoo 19 Enterprise system is deployed to the production server, accepted by Angel Hands in writing, and made live for daily business operations.

2.4   UAT · UAT Sign-Off

"User Acceptance Testing" or "UAT" means the structured testing period during which Angel Hands validates the configured system against agreed requirements. "UAT Sign-Off" means the written confirmation by Angel Hands that the system satisfactorily meets all agreed requirements, triggering the second payment milestone.

2.5   Implementation Fee

"Implementation Fee" means the fixed, one-time fee of SAR 18,000 payable by Angel Hands to Code-OX for the Services as set out in Schedule B.

2.6   Third-Party Costs

"Third-Party Costs" means all fees payable directly by Angel Hands to third-party vendors — including the Odoo Enterprise licence, LightNode server hosting, and WhatsApp Business API messaging fees — which are outside the Implementation Fee and payable separately by Angel Hands.

2.7   Support Period

"Support Period" means the period of six (6) calendar months immediately following the Go-Live Date during which Code-OX shall provide complimentary bug-fix and minor adjustment support as described in Section 7.

2.8   Confidential Information & Intellectual Property

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either Party to the other, including business data, customer records, financial information, source code, pricing, and any information reasonably understood to be confidential. "Intellectual Property" or "IP" means all configurations, custom code, development artefacts, written materials and original works of authorship produced under this Agreement.

03  ·  Schedule A
Scope of Work — Implementation Phases

Code-OX shall implement Odoo 19 Enterprise for Angel Hands Beauty Centre across six (6) phases. The following Odoo modules are in scope:

Point of Sale (POS)
CRM & Customer Database
Loyalty Program
Commission Management
Discount Approval Governance
Accounting & Invoicing
ZATCA Phase 2 e-Invoicing
Employees & HR
WhatsApp Marketing Integration
Online Booking (Appointments)
Phase 01 Project Kick-Off & Requirements Finalisation
  • Stakeholder alignment session with Mohammed and all department leads
  • Final requirements sign-off — module configuration decisions, commission tier structures, discount policy rules
  • System architecture design — server topology, user roles and access matrix (4 licensed users)
  • Data inventory — identify existing customer records, services catalogue, and financial data for migration
  • Project plan with agreed milestones, testing windows, and go-live target date
Phase 02 System Setup & Module Configuration
  • Odoo 19 Enterprise installation on LightNode Premium Server (Riyadh: 8 GB RAM, 4 vCPU, 50 GB NVMe)
  • Company settings, Saudi Arabia localisation, VAT (15%) configuration, and multi-currency setup
  • Point of Sale configuration — payment methods (cash, card, mada), receipt templates, service catalogue with pricing
  • CRM setup — customer profiles, visit history tracking, segmentation tags for the salon's target demographic
  • Loyalty program configuration — point earn/redemption rules, tier thresholds, reward catalogue
  • Employee profiles, job positions, and working-time calendars for 20+ staff
  • Accounting chart of accounts, VAT journal entries, and bank reconciliation settings
Phase 03 Customisation & Integration Development
  • Tiered commission engine — custom configuration or module build to calculate commissions per stylist per service tier, replacing paper-based tracking
  • Discount governance workflow — cashier-initiated discount requests routed to manager approval before application at POS, with audit trail
  • ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing — integration of certified ZATCA e-invoicing connector, QR code generation, and Fatoora portal compliance
  • WhatsApp Business API integration — automated booking confirmations, appointment reminders, promotional campaign messaging (via approved WhatsApp channel)
  • Online booking (Appointments module) — customer-facing booking link or widget, service-slot configuration, staff availability calendar

04  ·  Exclusions & Client Responsibilities
What's Outside Scope & What Angel Hands Provides
4.1   Out of Scope — Available Separately

The following sit outside the scope of this Agreement and may be picked up under a separate written engagement when Angel Hands is ready:

  • Additional Odoo modules not listed in Section 3 — e.g., multi-branch consolidation, advanced payroll, fleet management, or manufacturing — available as Change Requests
  • Third-party software licences — Odoo Enterprise subscription, LightNode server fees, and WhatsApp API messaging costs are paid directly by Angel Hands to the respective vendors (see Schedule B, Section 5.2)
  • ZATCA Phase 1 compliance remediation, if any existing invoicing is non-compliant prior to go-live — this Agreement covers Phase 2 e-invoicing for all new invoices from Go-Live forward
  • Payroll processing and WPS integration — salary disbursement, Wage Protection System filing, and GOSI contributions are outside scope unless added by Change Request
  • Custom mobile app development for customer-facing booking or loyalty — the implemented online booking is web-based via Odoo Appointments
  • Paid advertising campaigns — WhatsApp message content creation and campaign management beyond system configuration are billed separately
  • Ongoing system administration, hosting management — after the Support Period, ongoing server management, Odoo upgrades and AMC services are covered under a separate Annual Maintenance Contract
4.2   Code-OX Commitments

Code-OX commits to the following throughout the engagement, so Angel Hands can rely on a predictable, professional partnership:

  • Assign a dedicated Project Manager as the single point of contact for Angel Hands — all questions, updates and decisions go through one person
  • Deliver each phase within the mutually agreed project plan confirmed at kick-off; if Code-OX anticipates a delay on its side, Angel Hands will be notified at least five (5) business days in advance with a revised schedule
  • Conduct all UAT feedback cycles within five (5) business days of receiving Angel Hands' UAT issues list — no issue goes unresolved before sign-off is requested
  • Keep Angel Hands' business data and customer records strictly confidential and never use them for any purpose beyond performing the Services
  • Provide honest, plain-language status updates — no jargon, no surprises; if something is harder than expected, Angel Hands hears about it promptly
  • Remain available via WhatsApp or email during Saudi business hours (9 AM – 6 PM AST, Sunday – Thursday) throughout the implementation and Support Period
4.4   Client Responsibilities

To enable on-time delivery across all six phases, Angel Hands agrees to:

  • Appoint Mohammed (or a nominated deputy) as the single authorised point of contact for decisions and approvals throughout the project
  • Make key staff members available for requirements sessions, UAT, and training within the agreed phase schedule — non-availability shall shift the affected milestone by the equivalent number of working days
  • Provide existing data for migration in a clean, consolidated format (Excel or CSV) within five (5) business days of the Phase 4 kick-off date
  • Provide admin access to the LightNode server environment once provisioned, and to any existing systems (POS, accounting software) required for data extraction
  • Provide written UAT Sign-Off or a consolidated list of UAT issues within seven (7) business days of each test cycle; absence of written objection or a raised issue list by that date shall constitute sign-off on tested items
  • Procure the Odoo Enterprise licence (4 users, annual subscription) and the LightNode server subscription directly with the respective vendors before Phase 2 begins — Code-OX will guide procurement but the contracts and payments are the Client's
  • Register and activate the WhatsApp Business API account (or provide admin access to an existing account) before Phase 3 integration begins
  • Obtain a ZATCA certification certificate and ensure the business commercial registration and VAT number details are correct before ZATCA Phase 2 integration in Phase 3
  • Make timely payments as per the milestone schedule in Section 5.1 — project progression is contingent on payment of each preceding milestone
Decision velocity: Where any approval cycle or data delivery runs longer than the windows above, the affected deliverable shifts by the equivalent number of working days — no penalty, no fee adjustment. The intent is to keep both sides accountable to the cadence without punishing reasonable real-life delays.

06  ·  Post-Launch Support
Six-Month Complimentary Support
6.1   Support Period

Code-OX shall provide Angel Hands with six (6) calendar months of complimentary post-launch support commencing on the Go-Live Date. The 30-day Hypercare period in Phase 6 is included within and forms the first month of this Support Period.

6.2   Scope of Complimentary Support
  • Bug fixes — correction of any defect or non-conformity in the configured system attributable to Code-OX's implementation
  • Minor configuration adjustments — small changes to POS settings, commission rate adjustments, loyalty rule tweaks, user permissions
  • Functional clarifications — guidance on how to use configured features, via email or video call
  • Critical issue escalation — if the system goes down or a critical function fails, Code-OX prioritises resolution within one (1) business day of notification
6.3   Outside the Complimentary Support Scope
  • New modules, new customisations, or new integrations not in Schedule A
  • Issues arising from changes made to the system by Angel Hands or a third party after handover
  • Server infrastructure outages caused by LightNode or force majeure events
  • Issues arising from WhatsApp API policy changes or Meta platform updates
6.4   Extended Support

Upon expiry of the Support Period, continued support may be procured under a separate Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) at rates agreed in writing. Code-OX will present an AMC proposal to Angel Hands no later than one (1) month before the Support Period ends.

07  ·  Term & Termination
Duration & Termination Rights
7.1   Term

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until the later of (i) completion of all six (6) implementation phases; or (ii) expiry of the six-month Support Period — unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section.

7.2   Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and does not remedy it within fourteen (14) calendar days of written notification. The notifying Party will always attempt to resolve the matter in good faith before exercising this right.

If Angel Hands terminates for cause due to Code-OX's material breach, Code-OX shall refund the pro-rata portion of the Implementation Fee paid that corresponds to work not yet completed or not accepted by Angel Hands, within fifteen (15) business days of termination. If Code-OX terminates for cause due to Angel Hands' material breach, all Milestones triggered up to the termination date remain payable.

7.3   Termination for Convenience

Angel Hands may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' written notice. In such event, Angel Hands shall pay (a) all amounts owing for Milestones already triggered; and (b) a reasonable charge for work-in-progress completed since the last triggered Milestone, calculated in proportion to the phase progress achieved. Code-OX shall deliver all work product and Deliverables completed and paid for within fifteen (15) business days of the termination date.

7.4   Effect of Termination

Upon termination: (i) outstanding amounts are settled within fifteen (15) business days; (ii) Code-OX delivers all Deliverables produced and paid for; (iii) each Party returns or securely deletes the other's Confidential Information; and (iv) Sections 8 (IP), 9 (Confidentiality), 10 (Warranties) and 11 (General Provisions) survive termination.
