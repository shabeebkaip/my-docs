01
Business Overview
Foundation
Understanding the size, structure and maturity of the salon so we can right-size the deployment.
  1. How many branches will operate on this Odoo instance — now and in the next 12 months?
    If multi-branch, clarify whether each branch shares one inventory and one customer database, or operates independently.
  2. How many years has the salon been operating?
    Historic data depth shapes the migration plan — a 5-year-old salon has different reporting needs than a brand-new one.
  3. What is the total number of staff (cashiers, beauticians, support) currently employed?
    The brief mentions 4 system users — but please confirm total headcount. Commission and payroll exports depend on this.
  4. Is the salon owned by a single proprietor, partnership, or LLC? Are there parent/sister companies sharing the books?
    Affects chart-of-accounts setup and inter-company configuration in Odoo Accounting.
  5. Do you currently use any software for sales, accounting, customer management, or appointments?
    List by name and how long you've used it: e.g., "Mada terminal + Excel + WhatsApp", "Foloosi POS", "Fresha", "Zoho Books". Critical for data migration scoping.
02
Operations & Transaction Volume
Pricing-critical
Volume directly drives hosting tier, hardware sizing, training depth and post-launch support hours.
  1. Approximately how many customers does the salon serve per day across all chairs / stations? Price Driver
    Average + peak day estimate. e.g., "40 on a weekday, 90 on a Friday."
    < 20/day 20–50/day 50–100/day 100+/day
  2. What is your approximate monthly revenue range? Price Driver
    A confidential figure that helps us position the solution against your scale — not a tax disclosure. Even a band is fine.
    < SAR 50K SAR 50K–150K SAR 150K–400K SAR 400K+
  3. What is the typical average ticket size (per customer visit)?
    Helps right-size POS receipts and tip-handling workflows.
  4. How many distinct services are in your catalog?
    Approximate count is fine — e.g., "around 80 services across hair, nails, facial, threading, makeup."
    < 30 30–80 80–150 150+
  5. Do you sell retail products (shampoos, cosmetics, accessories) in addition to services?
    If yes, ballpark SKU count and whether inventory tracking is required. This activates Odoo Inventory.
  6. Do you offer service packages (e.g., bridal package, monthly-grooming bundle) or memberships / packs of sessions?
    Packages and pre-paid packs need careful POS configuration and revenue-recognition rules.
  7. Are appointments managed today? If yes — how? (paper diary, WhatsApp, Fresha, etc.)
    Note: Online appointments are NOT in the brief; let us know if you'd like them included.

03
Point of Sale (POS) & Hardware
Setup-critical
The brief calls for 4 POS terminals. We need to understand the existing hardware landscape and what you need us to supply versus reuse.
  1. Do you already own POS terminals (tablets / PCs / dedicated POS units)? If yes — what make, model, and operating system?
    e.g., "iPad 9th gen × 4", "Lenovo all-in-one × 2", or "we need to buy everything." Odoo POS runs on any modern browser.
  2. Do you have receipt printers already? Brand and connection type? (USB / LAN / Bluetooth)
    Most-used: EPSON TM-T20, TM-T82, Star TSP100. Knowing this avoids buying duplicates.
  3. Do you have cash drawers and barcode scanners?
    Barcode scanner is only needed if you sell retail products.
  4. What card-payment provider do you use? (Mada, STC Pay, HyperPay, Geidea, PayTabs, Foloosi, etc.)
    Affects whether we integrate POS with the terminal for one-click reconciliation or stay manual.
  5. Internet connection — is each branch on a stable broadband or fibre connection? Is there a backup connection (4G/5G dongle)?
    Determines whether to deploy Odoo Online (cloud) or Odoo.sh, and whether offline-POS mode is mandatory.
  6. Do you want digital receipts (SMS/email) by default, printed receipts, or both — customer's choice at checkout?
    This decides UI behaviour and whether SMS/email costs are recurring.
04
Users, Shifts & Access Control
Security
The brief specifies 4 users with shift-locked access. We need to confirm the shift structure and clarify exception-handling.
  1. Confirm the three shifts — what are the actual times for Morning, Evening, Night?
    e.g., Morning 09:00–17:00, Evening 17:00–01:00, Night 01:00–09:00. Used to harden login enforcement.
  2. Should a cashier be locked out entirely outside their shift, or allowed with a manager-PIN override?
    Hard lockout is simpler; manager override is more flexible for emergencies. Pick one.
  3. When a cashier opens a POS session, should the cash float (opening balance) be declared at session start and reconciled at close?
    Standard cash-control behaviour — recommended for shrinkage prevention.
  4. Is there any expected case where the Accountant needs to log into the POS to issue a refund, void a transaction, or correct an entry?
    Determines whether refund/void requires a manager-PIN at the cashier's screen or a full Accountant login.
  5. Will any of the 4 users need mobile access (smartphone / tablet) to view reports or dashboards?
    Affects whether mobile-friendly dashboards are part of scope.

05
Discount & Promotional Dashboard
Custom Module
The dedicated discount dashboard is a custom build. The complexity here drives a substantial portion of the development fee.
  1. How many promotional campaigns do you typically run in a year? Roughly how many active at once? Effort Driver
    e.g., "1 per month + 4 big seasonal (Ramadan, Eid, National Day, Founding Day)." Affects dashboard complexity and reporting depth.
  2. Will discounts apply to specific services, service groups, the entire bill, or all three?
    Tick all that apply. "Bridal package -20% but only on Thursdays" is a service-group + time-window combo.
  3. Do you ever need customer-segment-specific discounts? (e.g., VIP customers, customers with 5+ visits)
    If yes, this connects to the customer DB and adds logic complexity.
  4. Should cashiers ever be allowed to apply a manual discount at checkout — with or without a manager-PIN?
    The brief specifies fully-automatic discounts, but real-world salons often allow exceptional manual discounts. Confirm policy.
  5. Do you want discount stacking allowed, or strictly one discount per visit?
    e.g., "VIP -10%" AND "Friday -15%" — apply both, or pick the larger one?
  6. Should the dashboard support buy-one-get-one or free-service-with-X mechanics, or just percentage / fixed-amount discounts?
    BOGO needs different logic. Decide now or it becomes a change request later.
06
Employee Commission Engine
Custom Module
Commission is the second custom module. Salon commission structures vary wildly — we need yours mapped exactly.
  1. How many employee categories have different commission percentages? Effort Driver
    e.g., "Hair stylist 15%, Makeup 20%, Skincare 12%, Threading 8%" = 4 categories.
  2. Is commission based on revenue per employee, per-customer count, or a tiered combination?
    A tier example: "10% up to SAR 10K/month revenue, 15% above." Tiers add logic complexity.
  3. Do you have shared services where two employees split commission? (e.g., one washes, another colours)
    If yes, the POS must capture the split per line item — a non-trivial UI tweak.
  4. Does the discount applied to a service reduce the commission base, or does the employee still earn on the original (pre-discount) price?
    Common dispute area. Define clearly to avoid disputes after go-live.
  5. Are tips tracked through POS? Do they flow to commission, salary, or are they cash-in-hand?
    If digital tips, we need to design that flow. If cash, we just exclude.
  6. Should commission be paid monthly, mid-month + month-end, or weekly?
    Affects payroll-integration cadence and report timing.
  7. Do you want commissions to flow into Odoo Payroll automatically, or just exported as Excel for manual processing?
    Payroll integration is significantly more effort than export. Choose deliberately.

07
ZATCA Compliance & E-Invoicing
Mandatory
Phase 2 ZATCA integration requires credentials, certificates and approval steps — knowing your status saves weeks.
  1. Is the salon currently in ZATCA Phase 2 (Integration), or only Phase 1 (Generation)?
    If you've already been notified by ZATCA for Phase 2 onboarding, please share the wave/date.
  2. Do you have ZATCA Fatoora portal credentials and the production OTP / CSID ready?
    Without these, integration cannot complete. We'll guide you through generation if not.
  3. Are all invoices going to be simplified (B2C), or do you ever issue tax (B2B) invoices to corporate clients?
    e.g., weddings, hotel partnerships, embassy events. Determines templates and clearance flows.
  4. Will any services be VAT-exempt or zero-rated?
    Most salon services are 15% VAT, but certain medical/wellness procedures or exports can differ.
  5. Do you currently file VAT quarterly or monthly?
    Determines the cadence of automated VAT report exports.
08
Customer Database & Marketing Communications
Integrations
Migration volume and chosen SMS/email providers shape both upfront effort and ongoing running costs.
  1. Approximately how many existing customer records will need to be migrated into Odoo? Migration Effort
    Even a rough number — 500, 3,000, 15,000. Format also matters (Excel, another POS export, paper book).
  2. What format is your existing customer data in?
    Excel/CSV Old POS export WhatsApp/Phone book Paper No existing data
  3. Do you have a preferred SMS provider, or open to recommendations?
    Common KSA options: Unifonic, Resal, Mobily Business, STC Business. Sender-ID approval can take 2 weeks — let's start early.
  4. Estimated SMS volume per month — for receipts + marketing combined?
    Affects vendor selection (per-message vs bulk packages) and recurring cost in the proposal.
  5. Preferred email-marketing tool — Odoo native, Mailchimp, Sendinblue, other?
    Odoo has email-marketing built in; external tools add integration scope.
  6. Are marketing messages bilingual (Arabic + English) or Arabic only?
    Bilingual = two campaign sets per occasion. Affects content-design time.
  7. Are you planning a loyalty / points program immediately, or only in a future phase?
    The brief mentions it as future scope. Confirm Phase 1 vs Phase 2 so we don't over-quote now.

09
Accounting, Banking & Financial Setup
Accounting Module
Existing books need a clean cut-over date and a chart-of-accounts alignment.
  1. Do you have an existing chart of accounts we should replicate, or use Odoo's standard Saudi CoA?
    If you have one from a previous accountant/auditor, please attach it.
  2. What is the fiscal year start date? (typically 1 January in KSA, but confirm)
  3. Which banks do you operate with? Will bank statements be imported manually, or via direct integration (where supported)?
    Most KSA banks don't yet support direct integration. Manual MT940/CSV import is standard.
  4. Will there be multi-currency transactions? (e.g., dollar payments from non-resident customers)
    If "always SAR only," we skip multi-currency setup.
  5. How do you handle petty cash today? Should petty-cash transactions flow into Odoo or stay outside?
  6. Do you have opening balances (cash on hand, bank balances, payables, receivables) as of the planned go-live date?
    Required for a clean accounting start in Odoo.
  7. Do you require Zakat calculation and reporting separately from VAT?
    Salon entities often need both. Affects report templates.
10
Reports & Dashboards
Customisation
Standard Odoo reports cover ~80% of needs. The remaining 20% — formatted exactly the way your auditor or owner wants — is where custom reports live.
  1. Are there specific report layouts your auditor or owner currently uses (in Excel) that we must replicate exactly?
    Please attach 2–3 sample reports. Each replicated layout = ~half-day of dev.
  2. What does the owner want to see on the dashboard the moment they open Odoo each morning?
    e.g., "Yesterday's revenue, top 3 services, today's bookings, cash-on-hand." We design a tailored dashboard.
  3. Should reports be available in Arabic, English, or both?
  4. Do reports need to be auto-emailed (e.g., end-of-day summary to the owner at 11 PM)?
  5. Will an external auditor need read-only access to the system?
    If yes, that's a 5th user — affects Odoo licensing count.

11
Hosting, Deployment & Licensing
Infrastructure
Odoo can be deployed three ways — each with different price, control and maintenance trade-offs.
  1. Do you have a preference for hosting? Cost Driver
    Odoo Online — easiest, hosted by Odoo SA, no admin needed. Odoo.sh — managed cloud with full Studio + custom modules. Self-hosted — on a KSA-region cloud (AWS Riyadh, STC Cloud) or on-premise server.
    Odoo Online Odoo.sh Self-hosted Open to recommendation
  2. Have you already purchased Odoo Enterprise licences, or do they need to be part of our quote?
    Enterprise licences are billed per user per month directly by Odoo. We can act as your reseller.
  3. Is data residency in Saudi Arabia a mandatory requirement?
    Some regulators / Zakat queries may prefer local hosting. Affects deployment region selection.
  4. What is your preference for backups? Daily automatic, hourly, real-time replication?
12
Training, Support & Roll-Out
Adoption
A system is only as good as its adoption. We need to plan training and post-launch support correctly.
  1. How many people need hands-on training? (cashiers, accountant, owner, future hires)
  2. Should training be in Arabic, English, or both?
  3. Do you prefer on-site training at the salon, online sessions, or recorded videos?
  4. What level of post-launch support do you expect — basic email support, 8×5 hotline, or 24×7 critical-issue cover?
    Each tier carries a different Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) fee.
  5. Are you open to a phased go-live (e.g., POS first, then accounting, then ZATCA Phase 2), or do you need a single big-bang launch?
    Phased rollouts reduce risk; big-bang launches reduce timeline but increase risk.
13
Commercial Context & Timeline
Project Planning
Final calibration so the proposal hits the right shape on the first try.
  1. Do you have a budget range in mind for this implementation? Critical
    A confidential ballpark is fine. It helps us decide whether to propose a lean MVP or a full enterprise rollout.
  2. Are there hard deadlines we must hit? (e.g., Ramadan launch, lease renewal, ZATCA Phase 2 wave date)
  3. Who is the final decision-maker on this engagement? Owner / Spouse / Finance head?
    Important so we route the proposal correctly the first time.
  4. Have you compared other Odoo partners? If yes, what stood out as concerns or strengths?
    Optional — helps us articulate our differentiation clearly.
  5. Is there anything else — operational, cultural, or technical — that we should know before quoting?
    The "what we wish you'd asked" box. Use freely.
