Business Overview
Foundation
Five quick questions so we can right-size the deployment from day one.
How many branches will run on this Odoo instance — now and in the next 12 months?
Why this matters: Odoo lets multiple branches share one customer database and inventory, or run independently. We pick a model up front to avoid an expensive reconfiguration once you start adding shops.
How many years has the salon been operating?
Why this matters: A salon with 5 years of history brings 5 years of customer records and accounting we need to migrate. A new salon has lighter migration but heavier setup. Either way, no judgement — just shapes the plan.
Total staff headcount today (cashiers, beauticians, support, all included)?
Why this matters: The brief mentions 4 system users — but commission reports and payroll exports need to know everyone who earns from the salon, not just who logs in.
Legal structure — single proprietor, partnership, LLC?
Why this matters: Affects how we set up the chart of accounts and whether inter-company features in Odoo need to be enabled (e.g., if there's a parent company).
What software do you use today for sales, accounting, customer management or appointments?
Why this matters: Where your data lives today determines the migration path. "Excel + Mada terminal + WhatsApp" needs a different plan than "Foloosi POS" or "Zoho Books." No system is too small to mention.
02
Operations & Transaction Volume
★ Pricing-critical
The single biggest driver of fair pricing. Even rough estimates help — we'd rather under-quote and over-deliver than the reverse.
Price driver
Roughly how many customers does the salon serve per day, across all chairs?
Why this matters: Volume tells us how many concurrent POS sessions we need to design for, how chatty the SMS receipts will be, and how big the database grows year-on-year. There's no "right" number — we just need a real one.
Price driver
Your approximate monthly revenue band?
Why this matters: Strictly confidential — used only so we don't quote a SAR 50K/month salon the same way we'd quote a SAR 500K/month one. We'd rather propose a lean MVP that fits your scale than over-engineer your first phase. Even a band is enough.
Typical average ticket size (per customer visit)?
Why this matters: Tells us about your tip / commission flow and whether the receipt template needs to feature service breakdown prominently.
How many distinct services are in your catalog?
Why this matters: Catalog size dictates how much time we spend on initial data entry and whether grouping (categories) is needed for POS speed.
Do you sell retail products (shampoos, cosmetics, accessories) alongside services?
Why this matters: "Yes" activates Odoo Inventory and adds barcode scanning to POS. "No" keeps the setup simpler and quicker.
Do you offer service packages (e.g., bridal package) or pre-paid session packs?
Why this matters: Packages and pre-paid packs need careful POS rules and revenue-recognition logic — getting them right at launch prevents accounting headaches later.
How are appointments managed today? (paper diary, WhatsApp, Fresha, etc.)
Why this matters: Online appointments are not in the current brief — but if you'd like to add a booking website later, it's much cheaper to design it into the architecture now than retrofit.
03
Point of Sale & Hardware
Setup-critical
The brief calls for 4 POS terminals. We need to know what you already own vs what to source.
Do you already own POS terminals (tablets, PCs, or dedicated POS units)?
Why this matters: Odoo POS runs on any modern browser. Knowing what you have lets us avoid recommending a duplicate purchase.
Receipt printers — brand and connection type (USB/LAN/Bluetooth)?
Why this matters: Most-used: EPSON TM-T20 / T82 or Star TSP100. Knowing the brand confirms Odoo driver compatibility on Day 1.
Cash drawers and barcode scanners — already on hand?
Why this matters: Cash drawers are usually pulse-triggered from the printer. Barcode scanners are only needed if you sell retail products.
Which card-payment provider do you use? (Mada, STC Pay, HyperPay, Geidea, PayTabs, etc.)
Why this matters: Some providers integrate directly with Odoo POS so the bill amount auto-pushes to the terminal — one less manual step at checkout. Others stay manual.
Internet stability at the salon — main connection and backup?
Why this matters: Determines whether Odoo Online (cloud) is safe to recommend, or whether we should ensure offline POS mode is mandatory.
Default receipt delivery — printed, digital, or customer's choice?
Why this matters: Customer-choice at checkout is what we usually recommend. Default-digital reduces paper costs but adds SMS volume.
04
Users, Shifts & Access Control
Security
The brief specifies 4 users with shift-locked access. Five small clarifications fully define this.
What are the actual shift times for Morning / Evening / Night?
Why this matters: Used to enforce login windows. Even if shifts overlap during handover, we need the exact ranges.
If a cashier tries to log in outside their shift — hard lockout, or manager-PIN override?
Why this matters: Hard lockout is the strictest — simpler to build, no shrinkage risk. Override is more flexible for emergencies but needs a manager available.
Cash float declaration — should cashiers declare opening cash at the start of each session?
Why this matters: Standard cash-control behaviour. Strongly recommended for any cash-heavy salon. Costs no extra effort to enable.
For refunds, voids and corrections — accountant login or manager PIN on the cashier screen?
Why this matters: Manager-PIN-at-cashier is faster for the customer. Accountant-login is the strictest audit trail. Pick your preference.
Will any of the 4 users need mobile dashboard access (phone / tablet) outside the salon?
Why this matters: If yes, we polish the mobile views as part of the build. If no, we save you that effort.
05
Discount & Promotional Dashboard
★ Custom build
This dashboard is custom-built — its complexity is one of the biggest swings in the proposal. Six questions and we've defined it precisely.
Effort driver
How many promotional campaigns do you typically run in a year? Roughly how many active at once?
Why this matters: If you run one Friday discount + 4 big seasonal events (Ramadan, Eid, National Day, Founding Day), the dashboard logic is straightforward. If you run 50+ overlapping micro-promos per year, it needs different scaffolding.
Do discounts apply to specific services, service groups, the entire bill — or all of the above?
Why this matters: Each option needs its own dashboard field and POS rule. Tick everything you'll ever need.
Do you ever need customer-segment-specific discounts? (e.g., VIPs, 5+ visit customers)
Why this matters: Segment-aware discounts pull from the customer DB at checkout. Powerful, but adds development time.
Should cashiers ever apply a manual discount at checkout? With or without manager-PIN?
Why this matters: The brief says all discounts are automatic — but real-world salons usually want a "loyalty-recovery" manual override. Decide policy now to avoid awkward "we forgot to add that" requests later.
Discount stacking — apply multiple at once, or strictly one per visit?
Why this matters: "Both Friday -15% AND VIP -10%" stacks to ~24% off. Some salons love this; some hate it. Define your stance.
Do you need buy-one-get-one or "free service with X" mechanics — or just percentage / fixed-amount?
Why this matters: BOGO and combo logic is a separate development item. If you ever plan to run them, scoping now is much cheaper than as a change request.
06
Employee Commission Engine
★ Custom build
Salon commission structures are highly individual. Seven questions to map yours precisely so there's no commission dispute on Day 30.
Effort driver
How many employee categories have different commission percentages?
Why this matters: Each category becomes a configurable rule in the commission engine. Knowing the count up front shapes how the engine is architected.
Commission base — revenue per employee, per-customer count, or tiered combination?
Why this matters: Flat-percent on revenue is the simplest. Tiered ("10% up to SAR 10K, 15% above") adds complexity but boosts staff motivation.
Shared-service splits — two employees on one service (one washes, one colours)?
Why this matters: If yes, the POS line needs to capture a split per-line-item — a real UI design decision. If no, we save you that complexity.
When a service is discounted — does the employee still earn on the original price, or on the discounted price?
Why this matters: The single most common source of commission disputes after launch. Defining now means no awkward conversations later.
Tips — tracked through POS, or cash-in-hand only?
Why this matters: Digital tips need a dedicated POS workflow. Cash tips stay outside the system. Both are common — just tell us which.
Commission payment frequency — monthly, mid-month + month-end, or weekly?
Why this matters: Drives the cadence of automated commission reports and payroll integration.
Commissions — flow into Odoo Payroll, or just exported to Excel?
Why this matters: Payroll integration is significantly more effort. Excel export is cheaper to build and most salons start there.

ZATCA Compliance & E-Invoicing
Mandatory
Phase 2 needs credentials and certificates. Five questions to know where you are on the journey.
Are you currently on ZATCA Phase 2 (Integration), or just Phase 1 (Generation)?
Why this matters: Phase 2 needs Fatoora portal integration with OTP / CSID, while Phase 1 is just compliant invoice generation. Both are in scope; we just need to know your starting point.
Do you have ZATCA Fatoora portal credentials and the production OTP / CSID ready?
Why this matters: No CSID, no Phase 2 integration. If you don't have these yet, we'll walk you through the ZATCA onboarding flow as part of project Week 1.
Do you ever issue B2B tax invoices (to companies, hotels, embassies), or strictly B2C simplified invoices?
Why this matters: B2B and B2C have different ZATCA flows. Most salons are B2C only, but bridal contracts and corporate gifts are common exceptions.
Will any services be VAT-exempt or zero-rated?
Why this matters: Most salon services attract 15% VAT. Some wellness / medical procedures or exported services can differ — affects the chart-of-accounts setup.
Do you file VAT quarterly or monthly?
Why this matters: Drives how often the automated VAT return is generated.

Customer Database & Marketing
Integrations
Migration volume and SMS/email choices drive both initial effort and ongoing running costs.
Migration effort
Roughly how many existing customer records to migrate?
Why this matters: Migration is mostly the cleaning, not the loading. Even rough numbers help — 500, 3,000 or 15,000 makes a huge difference to scoping.
In what format does your existing customer data live?
Why this matters: Excel/CSV is fastest. Paper records mean we need a data-entry plan. Be honest — it's all manageable.
Preferred SMS provider — or open to recommendations?
Why this matters: Sender-ID approval can take 2 weeks in KSA. Starting this in Week 1 of the project avoids a launch delay. Common picks: Unifonic, Resal, Mobily Business, STC Business.
Estimated SMS volume per month — receipts + marketing combined?
Why this matters: Drives the SMS-package recommendation. A salon serving 50/day with digital receipts = ~1,500/month minimum, plus marketing on top.
Preferred email marketing tool — Odoo native, Mailchimp, Sendinblue, or other?
Why this matters: Odoo has solid built-in email marketing — usually the right call for salons. External tools add integration scope.
Marketing messages — bilingual (Arabic + English) or Arabic only?
Why this matters: Bilingual = two campaign sets per occasion. Most KSA salons go Arabic-only for SMS and bilingual for email.
Loyalty / points program — Phase 1 or future phase?
Why this matters: The brief mentions loyalty as future scope. Confirming "future" now keeps the proposal lean. If you'd like it included from Day 1, we add it deliberately.
09
Accounting, Banking & Financial Setup
Accounting
Books need a clean cut-over date and a chart-of-accounts that matches your accountant's habits.
Do you have an existing chart of accounts to replicate, or should we use Odoo's standard Saudi CoA?
Why this matters: Replicating your accountant's chart preserves continuity for tax history. Odoo's standard saves time but means your accountant adjusts to a new layout.
Fiscal year start date — usually 1 January in KSA. Confirm yours?
Why this matters: Drives fiscal periods, year-end closing rules and VAT-quarter alignment.
Which banks do you use? Will bank statements be imported manually or via integration?
Why this matters: Direct bank integration in KSA is limited. Most clients use MT940 or CSV imports — works perfectly fine, just sets the right expectation.
Multi-currency transactions ever needed?
Why this matters: If "always SAR," we skip multi-currency setup and save you that overhead. If you sometimes get dollar payments (tourists, corporate gifts), we enable it.
How is petty cash handled today? Should it flow into Odoo?
Why this matters: Most salons run a small petty cash for tea, supplies, tips. Whether it gets tracked in Odoo or stays offline is a deliberate choice.
Opening balances available as of the planned go-live date?
Why this matters: Cash, bank, payables, receivables — needed for a clean accounting start. Your accountant typically prepares these.
Do you need Zakat calculations and reporting separately from VAT?
Why this matters: Saudi salon entities typically file both. Affects report templates and year-end workflow.

Reports & Dashboards
Customisation
Odoo's standard reports cover ~80% of needs. The remaining 20% — your auditor's preferred layout or the owner's morning dashboard — is where custom reports live.
Are there specific report layouts (your auditor's Excel templates) we must replicate exactly?
Why this matters: Each replicated layout takes ~half a day. If you have them, please attach when emailing this form — it's the single biggest help to accurate quoting.
What does the owner want to see on her dashboard the moment she opens Odoo?
Why this matters: This is the only screen most owners ever look at. Getting it right makes the whole project feel valuable from Day 1.
Reports in Arabic, English, or both?
Why this matters: Reports going to external auditors often need English. Internal management reports often prefer Arabic. Both is fine — just sets template count.
Should reports be auto-emailed (e.g., daily summary to the owner at 11 PM)?
Why this matters: Owners love this feature. Costs almost nothing to enable if planned now.
Will an external auditor need read-only access?
Why this matters: If yes, that's a 5th Odoo user — counts towards Enterprise licensing.

Hosting & Licensing
★ Cost driver
Odoo can be deployed three ways. Each has different cost, control and maintenance trade-offs.
Cost driver
Hosting preference?
Why this matters: Odoo Online = simplest, hosted by Odoo SA, no admin. Odoo.sh = managed cloud, supports Studio + custom modules. Self-hosted = on a KSA-region cloud (AWS Riyadh / STC Cloud) or on-premise. Most salons start with Odoo.sh — gives custom modules plus managed hosting.
Have you already purchased Odoo Enterprise licences, or include them in our quote?
Why this matters: Licences are per-user-per-month, billed by Odoo SA directly. We can act as your reseller and include them in one consolidated invoice, or you buy them directly — your call.
Is KSA data residency a mandatory requirement?
Why this matters: Some regulators / auditors prefer Saudi-region hosting. Affects deployment region (forces Odoo.sh KSA or self-hosted KSA over Odoo Online which is EU-hosted).
Backup preference — daily, hourly, or real-time replication?
Why this matters: Daily is the default and covers 99% of cases. Hourly or real-time is more expensive but reduces data-loss exposure.

Training, Support & Roll-Out
Adoption
A system is only as good as its adoption. Five questions and we know how to plan training and post-launch support.
How many people need hands-on training?
Why this matters: Each cashier needs ~2 hours; accountant needs ~4; owner needs ~1. Total guides our training-time estimate.
Training language — Arabic, English, or both?
Why this matters: Arabic-only training is common for cashiers. Accountant training is often English. We adapt.
Training format — on-site at the salon, online, or recorded videos?
Why this matters: On-site is most effective but adds travel cost (we're remote). Online sessions are efficient. Recorded videos are great as a reference for new staff hired later.
Post-launch support — basic email, 8×5 hotline, or 24×7 cover?
Why this matters: Each tier is a different Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) fee. Most salons start with 8×5 and never upgrade.
Phased go-live (POS → Accounting → ZATCA → Marketing) or big-bang launch?
Why this matters: Phased reduces risk and lets you absorb change gradually. Big-bang is faster end-to-end. Both work — depends on your appetite for change.
