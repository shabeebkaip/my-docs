Overview
Executive Summary
Customers don't shop by menu. They write "a gift for my wife, rose gold, under 3,000" or send a photo of a design they like. Our WhatsApp agent — a chat-based AI agent — understands these messages in Arabic and English, answers in Adiba's tone, recommends pieces from your collections, books showroom visits and gathers custom-design requests — then hands warm, qualified customers to your team.

The WhatsApp agent also runs your WhatsApp marketing: campaigns go to opted-in customer segments, and when customers reply, the agent picks up the conversation immediately. It covers everything a menu bot does, plus real conversation.

Scope of Work · Part 1
WhatsApp Account, AI Agent & Sales Actions
#WorkstreamWhat's Included
01WhatsApp Business API SetupOfficial Meta platform
  • Support with Meta Business verification and WhatsApp Business account setup.
  • Registration of a dedicated business number (new, or your existing number moved to the API).
  • Business profile: Adiba logo, display name, description, address, working hours and website.
  • Opt-in wording and consent capture so every customer contact is compliant.
02AI ConversationsArabic & English
  • Understands free text in Saudi Arabic and English, and replies in the customer's language.
  • Handles follow-up questions naturally within the same conversation.
  • Quick-reply buttons where they help, without forcing menus.
03Knowledge BaseTrained on Adiba
  • Collections and pieces: metal, karat, stones, sizes, price ranges and photos.
  • Policies: warranty, exchange & returns, care, sizing, payment, delivery.
  • Store information, opening hours and seasonal announcements.
  • Your team can update content; changes reviewed with Code-ox during support.
04Product Discovery & Recommendations
  • Understands requests like occasion, recipient, budget, metal and style.
  • Recommends matching pieces from your collections with photos and details.
  • Where a customer sends a photo of a design, the agent suggests similar pieces where available or opens a custom-design request.
05Brand Voice & Guardrails
  • Persona and tone agreed with you; approved wording for prices and offers.
  • No discounts, price commitments or delivery promises outside your rules.
  • Complaints, high-value and sensitive requests handed to your team automatically.
06Visit Booking & Custom Design Briefs
  • Books showroom appointments, with confirmation and reminder.
  • Gathers custom-design details conversationally — piece, metal, stones, budget, occasion, deadline, reference photos — and sends your team a complete brief.
07Order & Repair Status
  • Answers status questions from a list your team updates (Odoo or a shared sheet).
