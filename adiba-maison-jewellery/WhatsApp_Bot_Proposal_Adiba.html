Overview

Executive summary

Adiba's customers already reach out on WhatsApp — to ask about a piece, check a price, book a visit or follow up on a custom order. This proposal sets up an official WhatsApp Business account with a menu-driven bot that answers these instantly, day and night, through buttons and lists, and hands the conversation to your team whenever a person is needed.

The same account powers WhatsApp marketing: approved broadcast campaigns for new collections and seasonal occasions, sent to opted-in customers by segment. Every enquiry is captured as a contact, so your customer list grows with every conversation.
How it works
Customer writesMessages Adiba on WhatsApp, any time.
Bot guidesMenus and buttons in Arabic or English.
Bot actsAnswers, shows pieces, books a visit.
Team closesTakes over whenever a person is needed.
What you get
Guided MenusArabic & English menus with buttons and lists — no typing needed.
Collection BrowsingWhatsApp catalogue of your pieces, with enquiry in one tap.
Visit BookingsShowroom appointments with confirmation and reminder.
Custom Design RequestsGuided form with reference photos, sent to your team.
WhatsApp CampaignsApproved broadcasts to opted-in customer segments.
Lead CaptureEvery enquiry saved as a contact in your Odoo.
Upgrade Path

The bot follows fixed menus, which keeps it simple, predictable and quick to launch. If you later want customers to type freely and get natural answers, the same WhatsApp account and content can move to our chat-based WhatsApp agent, without starting again.

Scope of work · part 1

WhatsApp Account & Customer Flows

01
WhatsApp Business API SetupOfficial Meta platform
  • Support with Meta Business verification and WhatsApp Business account setup.
  • Registration of a dedicated business number (new, or your existing number moved to the API).
  • Business profile: Adiba logo, display name, description, address, working hours and website.
  • Opt-in wording and consent capture so every customer contact is compliant.
02
Welcome & Main MenuArabic / English
  • Branded welcome message and language choice.
  • Main menu with buttons and lists: Collections · Book a visit · Custom design · Order / repair status · Store & hours · FAQs · Talk to our team.
03
Collection BrowsingWhatsApp catalogue
  • Your pieces set up in the WhatsApp catalogue with photos, descriptions and categories (initial load of up to 100 items).
  • Browse by category — rings, necklaces, earrings, bracelets, bridal sets.
  • One-tap "I'm interested" sends the piece and customer details to your team.
04
Showroom Visit Booking
  • Customer picks a date and time slot within your opening hours.
  • Instant confirmation, plus a reminder message before the visit.
  • Booking details sent to your team.
05
Custom Design Request
  • Guided steps: piece type, metal, stones, budget range, occasion and deadline.
  • Customer can attach reference photos.
  • Complete request delivered to your team for a personal follow-up.
06
Order & Repair Status
  • Customer enters an order or repair number and receives its current status.
  • Statuses read from a list your team updates (Odoo or a shared sheet).
07
FAQsFixed answers
  • Up to 20 answers: care & cleaning, warranty, exchange & returns, sizing, payment methods, delivery, store location.

Scope of work · part 2

Team, Marketing & Launch

08
Team Inbox & Human HandoverUp to 2 team members
  • Shared WhatsApp inbox for your team on web and mobile, with conversation history.
  • Customers can ask for a person at any point; the chat moves to your team instantly.

09
Customer & Lead CaptureInto your Odoo
  • Name, number, interest, budget range and occasion saved for every enquiry.
  • Saved to your Odoo as contacts (and as CRM leads once the Odoo CRM module is live), or to a shared sheet if preferred.
  • Tags applied automatically, ready for the next marketing campaign.
10
WhatsApp Marketing CampaignsBroadcasts
  • Approved message templates for new collections, Ramadan & Eid, Saudi National Day, Founding Day, White Friday and private-viewing invitations.
  • Customer segments by tag — e.g. interest (bridal, gold, diamonds), occasion, last visit, VIP.
  • Scheduled campaigns with images and buttons ("View collection", "Book a visit").
  • Automatic opt-out handling, so customers who unsubscribe are never messaged again.

  • Conversations, enquiries by type, bookings, leads captured and hand-overs.
  • Campaign results: sent, delivered, read and replied.

  • Training for your team on the inbox, campaigns and updating content.

Not included in this proposal

Free-text understanding and AI answers (available with our WhatsApp agent), website or app chat, payment collection inside WhatsApp, Odoo module implementation, product photography, and Meta's own message charges.
