Overview
Executive Summary
Code-ox Technologies is pleased to submit this commercial quotation for extending Adiba Maison Atelier's existing Odoo installation with CRM, Inventory, and WhatsApp Marketing modules. Adiba currently runs Odoo POS at the counter on a single user licence — this proposal covers the natural next step, switching on the rest of the platform Adiba already owns.

The result is a system where every customer, every piece of stock, and every WhatsApp conversation is captured on the same Odoo instance — configured specifically around how an atelier counter runs, from weight and purity tracking to custom order follow-up.

Why This Approach

Every item above installs directly into Adiba's existing Odoo instance — the same one already running POS. There is no new server, no new software, and no migration. Staff keep using the same login; CRM, Inventory, and WhatsApp simply appear alongside the till they already use every day.

Terms & Conditions
General Terms
  • Scope of Work: This proposal covers CRM, Inventory, and WhatsApp Marketing setup and configuration as described. Any features outside this scope will require a separate written agreement and may incur additional charges.
  • Client Responsibilities: Adiba agrees to provide Odoo instance access, stock category data, and timely feedback within agreed deadlines. Delays caused by the client may impact the project timeline.
  • Odoo Licence Costs: Odoo user licence fees are billed directly by Odoo per Adiba's subscription plan and are not included in this quotation.
  • WhatsApp API Costs: WhatsApp Business API usage — Meta's per-conversation fees and any Business Solution Provider (BSP) charges — is billed directly to Adiba by Meta/the BSP and is not included in this quotation.
  • Revisions: Configuration includes up to two (2) rounds of adjustment post-training. Additional rounds will be charged separately at an agreed rate.
  • Warranty: Code-ox Technologies provides 6 months of complimentary support post go-live, covering all configured modules — see scope and terms below. Extended support retainer packages are available upon request after this period.
  • Confidentiality: Both parties agree to keep the contents of this proposal confidential and not disclose them to any third party without prior written consent.
  • Governing Law: This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
Highlight — 6 Months Complimentary Support

Covered for 6 months from go-live, at no extra cost: bug fixes and configuration corrections on the CRM, Inventory, and WhatsApp modules as built, plus minor adjustments to fields, roles, and templates — target response 2 business days. Not covered: new features, changes from Odoo/Meta platform updates, or issues from Adiba's own post-launch configuration changes — these are scoped separately, keeping the period fair to both sides. An optional retainer covers support after 6 months.
