Overview
Executive Summary
Code-ox Technologies is pleased to submit this commercial quotation for extending Adiba Maison Jewellery's existing Odoo installation with CRM, Inventory, and WhatsApp Marketing modules. Adiba currently runs Odoo POS at the counter on a single user licence — this proposal covers the natural next step, switching on the rest of the platform Adiba already owns.

The result is a system where every customer, every piece of stock, and every WhatsApp conversation is captured on the same Odoo instance — configured specifically around how a jewellery counter runs, from weight and purity tracking to custom order follow-up.

Scope of Work — Modules & Configuration
# Module / Deliverable & Scope Description
01 CRM — Customer Profiles & Purchase History One record per customer — every purchase, preference, and occasion (weddings, Eid, anniversaries) in one place.
02 CRM — Custom Order & Bespoke Piece Tracking Custom orders and special requests tracked as CRM opportunities — status, deadline, and customer linked together.
03 CRM — Follow-Up Reminders Odoo reminds staff to follow up on quotes, repairs, and occasions — no customer forgotten.
04 Inventory — Weight, Purity & Stone Fields Stock tracked by weight, karat/purity, category, and stone details — not just a generic quantity count.
05 Inventory — Real-Time Stock Sync with POS POS sales auto-deduct stock. Know exactly what's on hand, without a manual recount at the counter.
06 Inventory — Low-Stock Alerts by Category Get notified before a fast-moving category runs out — reorder before you lose a sale.
07 WhatsApp — Direct Messaging from CRM Records Message customers directly from Odoo. Full conversation history logged on their CRM record — nothing on personal phones.
08 WhatsApp — Order & Repair Status Automation Pre-approved templates for automated order and repair status updates — no phone call needed.
09 WhatsApp — Broadcast Campaigns Announce new collections, Eid or wedding-season offers, and promotions to your customer list in one send.
10 User Roles & Access Configuration 2 users configured: Admin/Owner (full visibility, reporting, pricing control) and Daily Operations (POS, CRM entry, stock updates, WhatsApp updates).
11 Training & Go-Live Support Hands-on training for both users. CodeOx on standby support through go-live on the existing Odoo instance.
