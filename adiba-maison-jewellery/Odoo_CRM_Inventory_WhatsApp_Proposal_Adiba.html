02What POS-Only Is Costing You
Today — POS OnlyWith CRM + Inventory + WhatsApp
Customer purchase history lives only in scattered receipts — no single profile per customer Every customer has one record: purchases, preferences, and occasions (weddings, Eid, anniversaries) in one place
Stock is counted at the till, not tracked by weight, purity, or piece — hard to know real-time stock across categories Inventory tracked by weight, karat/purity, category, and item — accurate stock visible any time, not just at checkout
Custom order and bespoke piece requests are tracked on paper or memory — easy to lose or delay CRM opportunity per custom order — status, deadline, and customer tied together, nothing forgotten
New arrivals, offers, and repair/order updates go out by personal phone calls — inconsistent, no record WhatsApp Business messages sent from Odoo — order confirmations, repair status, and new collection broadcasts, all logged
Owner has no live view of stock or customer activity without asking staff or checking the counter Owner dashboard: stock levels, top customers, and sales activity visible any time, from any device

Full visibility across CRM, Inventory, and POS
Sales reports, stock valuation, and customer analytics
Approves pricing, discounts, and stock adjustments
Sends WhatsApp broadcast campaigns
🧑‍💼  Daily Operations
Runs POS sales — no change to current workflow
Logs walk-in customers and custom order requests into CRM
Updates stock on receiving and transfers
Sends order and repair status updates via WhatsApp

Splitting Admin and Daily Operations keeps pricing and reporting control with the owner, while daily staff work without needing owner-level access — standard practice for a single-outlet retail setup moving from 1 to 2 users.
