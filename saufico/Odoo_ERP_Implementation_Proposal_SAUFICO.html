Overview

One system for the whole factory

Code-Ox Technologies will implement a full Odoo 19 Enterprise ERP for Saudi Fiberglass Factory (SAUFICO) — one system covering the entire operation, from the first sales enquiry through purchasing, the warehouse, the GRP production floor, quality, finance and payroll. It is configured specifically for a fiberglass (GRP/FRP) manufacturer — your products, materials, process and Saudi compliance — and delivered as one fixed project in roughly 30–40 days for 4 users.

Across everything
  • Dashboards — live KPIs per department
  • Documents — COAs, test certs on the record
Most time-critical item — ZATCA e-Invoicing (Fatoora)

Saudi law requires every VAT-registered business to issue invoices electronically through ZATCA's Fatoora system, and Phase 2 is being rolled out in waves. This carries a legal deadline set by the tax authority — not by the project plan, so it is the most urgent piece. SAUFICO should confirm its wave from its ZATCA notification letter. Once configured, invoices are created exactly as before and the system handles the compliance part automatically.

  • Payment — 50% on kickoff, 50% on go-live, of the SAR 21,000 implementation charge.
