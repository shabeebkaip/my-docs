Overview
Executive Summary
Code-Ox Technologies is pleased to submit this commercial proposal for a full Odoo Enterprise ERP implementation for Saudi Fiberglass Factory (SAUFICO) — one system covering your entire operation, from the first sales enquiry through purchasing, the warehouse, the GRP production floor, quality control, finance and payroll.

The system is configured specifically for SAUFICO's business — your GRP/FRP products, your raw materials, your production process and your market. This proposal sets out the scope, timeline and terms to deliver it into daily use across every department, from the first sales enquiry through to a compliant invoice.
The most important point up front — one system for the whole operation

Standard bookkeeping software records what already happened — invoices, ledgers, VAT returns. It has no concept of a bill of materials, a work order, a production machine, a quality check or a sales pipeline. None of SAUFICO's factory floor — resin mixing, filament winding, curing time, machine output — can be planned or tracked there at all.

Odoo runs that entire operational half of the business — the sales pipeline, the raw-material and finished-goods stock, the GRP production floor, the quality process and your Saudi compliance — with full accounting built in as one connected piece of the same system. Instead of separate tools that don't talk to each other, SAUFICO gets a single source of truth from the first sales enquiry to the final invoice.

A note on jargon

This proposal uses a few unavoidable technical terms. In plain words: BoM = the recipe of materials for a product; work centre = a machine or station on the floor; lot traceability = being able to trace a finished tank back to the exact resin batch it was made from; WPS = the government system for paying salaries; Zakat = the Saudi statutory levy administered by ZATCA; ZATCA / Fatoora = the tax authority's electronic-invoicing system.

Priority Deliverable
ZATCA e-Invoicing (Fatoora) — the deadline item
Saudi law requires every VAT-registered business to issue invoices electronically through ZATCA's Fatoora system. Phase 1 requires structured electronic invoices with a QR code. Phase 2 requires the invoicing system itself to be connected to ZATCA — invoices are electronically stamped, given a unique ID, and either cleared by ZATCA in real time (business-to-business) or reported within 24 hours (cash/counter sales). ZATCA is bringing businesses into Phase 2 in waves.
Why we flag this first

Unlike every other item in this proposal, ZATCA e-invoicing carries a legal deadline set by the tax authority — not by the project plan. SAUFICO should confirm which wave applies to it (from its ZATCA notification letter or directly with ZATCA), as that fixes the go-live date for this piece. It is a distinct compliance integration, scoped and tracked as its own deliverable.

StepWhat we deliver
Confirm the deadlineEstablish SAUFICO's ZATCA Phase 2 wave and integration date, so this go-live is fixed rather than open-ended.
Activate the moduleConfigure Odoo's official ZATCA-compliant e-invoicing add-on — standard configuration, not a custom build.
Onboard with ZATCAGenerate SAUFICO's security credentials with ZATCA (test, then production).
Automatic compliant invoicesSet up invoice numbering, QR codes, invoice linking and the required electronic format so every invoice and credit note is compliant automatically — no manual step for staff.
ZATCA testingRun ZATCA's mandatory test batch (standard, simplified, credit and debit notes) before switching on live integration.
Go-live & trainingConfirm real-time clearance for B2B and 24-hour reporting for any counter sales; train finance on the (minimal) day-to-day change and how to handle a rejection.
In plain terms

Once configured, nothing changes for your team's daily routine — invoices are created exactly as before. The system quietly does the legal part in the background. The value is that SAUFICO stays fully compliant with no manual effort and no risk of penalties.
