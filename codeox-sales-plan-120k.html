Go-to-Market Channels — Ranked by ROI for KSA

1

WhatsApp BD Outreach

15–25% reply rate from a named, verified business number — 3–5× higher than cold email in KSA. Message must be bilingual (Arabic-first for contractors), under 60 words, and ask one specific question. Volume: 80–100 messages/week.

2

Referral Network Activation

55–70% close rate on referred leads vs. 25–30% cold. Every signed client triggers an immediate referral ask. WeCare (once Phase 1 is signed) is a referral channel into 1,200+ KSA contractors. One warm introduction from WeCare = 30 cold outreach messages.

3

LinkedIn Outreach

Best for GMs, Operations Directors, and IT Managers at 30–200 person companies. 30–40 connection requests/week. No pitch in the first message. Two-message sequence → 15-minute call request.

4

Partner & Reseller Channel (Month 2+)

Accounting firms, ERP consultants, and IT equipment resellers are natural referral partners for Odoo. Revenue-share at 10–15% for qualified referrals turns these into passive pipeline generators.

5

Events & Expos

Saudi Build, Saudi Health, Cityscape, GITEX Riyadh. Attend without a booth. WhatsApp follow-up within 24 hours of any card collected — the follow-up is where the value is.

6

Cold Email

Lowest ROI. Use only for gov-adjacent or large enterprise targets where WhatsApp is culturally inappropriate. Under 120 words, personalised subject line.

Objection Handling Scripts

"LinkedIn Marketing is too expensive for us."
Our minimum engagement is 3,500 SAR/month — that is one day of a marketing coordinator's salary in KSA. We handle 30 days of content, in Arabic and English, across your company and personal LinkedIn pages. The question is not whether 3,500 SAR is expensive. The question is whether your competitors are showing up on LinkedIn every week while you are not.
"We can hire someone internally for LinkedIn."
A capable in-house LinkedIn content person with Arabic + English writing skill costs 6,000–9,000 SAR/month in KSA, plus benefits, onboarding time, and 60–90 days to ramp. We are operational from day one at 5,500 SAR/month with a team that has already built content libraries for your sector. Most clients find both the price and the speed favour us.
"AIWA is too expensive at 18,000 SAR setup + 8,000 SAR/month."
A junior procurement coordinator in KSA costs 4,000–5,500 SAR/month — 48,000–66,000 SAR/year. That person takes Eid leave, works 8 hours a day, cannot handle 200 vendor WhatsApp messages simultaneously, and produces no audit trail. AIWA works 24/7, scales to any volume, and has never missed a message or an RFQ deadline. At 8,000 SAR/month you are replacing a full headcount cost while getting a system that scales. The ROI is positive from month one if your team is spending more than 2 hours a day on manual vendor communication.
"We need to think about it."
Do not send a follow-up email. Ask: "What specifically would you want to know before moving forward? Let's address it in a 15-minute call on [specific day]." If they will not book a call, the deal is cold — move to monthly nurture. A prospect who needs to "think about it" but will not book a follow-up call has a budget or authority problem, not an information problem.
"We already have an internal team for this."
What does your internal team currently not have time for? Our clients typically come to us when their internal team is stretched across operations and cannot maintain consistent output. We are not replacing your team — we are the execution arm that runs so your team can focus on strategy.
"Can you give us a discount like Vitality's pricing?"
Vitality was our pilot engagement at a launch price before we standardised our tiers. Our current pricing reflects what it actually costs to deliver a high-quality, supported deployment — and still gives you a system that costs less than one employee. The 18,000 SAR setup + 8,000 SAR/month is the standard, and it is not negotiable.
