Overview
Executive Summary
Following our discovery call, Platin Hotel is looking to elevate its online presence to match the calibre of its 4-star, 65-suite property — with Ritz-Carlton and Mövenpick named as the visual benchmark. We reviewed platinhotel.com directly and identified the specific gaps holding the current site back from that positioning: a broken, off-brand booking handoff, no working bilingual experience, and a generic template look that undersells the property.

This proposal covers a full redesign — an elegant, photography-led design system, true Arabic/English bilingual support with mirrored RTL layout, and a simple on-brand reservation inquiry flow. No payment gateway is included: guests settle payment directly at the hotel, exactly as requested.
"The destination you'll adore to reside in" — Platin's own words, finally given a website that looks like it.

Live Audit — platinhotel.com
Where the Current Site Falls Short
We reviewed the live site directly rather than assuming. The current build has good bones — real photography, a clear amenities list, a defined suite range — but several gaps are actively working against the premium positioning Platin Hotel wants. These are normal maturing steps for a site built on an earlier template, and every one of them is directly addressed in the redesign.
Most Urgent Finding

The "BOOK NOW" button does not lead to a Platin Hotel experience at all — it redirects to an unbranded third-party engine (booking.staygrid.com, "Powered by Hotelogix"): a generic blue template with no logo, no photography, and no visual connection to the hotel. For the single most important action on the site, guests land somewhere that looks nothing like a 4-star property.

AreaCurrent ExperienceRedesigned Experience
Booking "Book Now" exits the site to an unbranded, unstyled third-party booking engine — no trust continuity. On-page reservation inquiry widget in Platin's own design; guest never leaves the site.
Bilingual Nav items are hardcoded "Home (EN)", "Contact (EN)" — a language switcher that was never finished. No Arabic version exists. Full Arabic/English site with true mirrored RTL layout and a working language switcher.
Visual Identity Boxy sections, flat mauve/tan color blocks, generic system fonts — reads as a stock template. Full-bleed cinematic photography, refined typography, restrained gold/charcoal palette in the register of Ritz-Carlton and Mövenpick.
Photography One recycled room photo reused across multiple sections; no gallery. Curated photography plan per suite category, facility and dining space — with a shoot brief if new photos are needed.
Call to Action A single small "Book Now" button buried mid-page; no persistent reservation prompt. A floating, always-visible reservation widget on the hero, matching how every reference brand surfaces booking.
Suite Presentation Suite categories listed as plain paragraphs with no visual comparison. Structured suite cards with imagery, amenities and rate, side by side for easy comparison.
Why this matters

None of this reflects badly on the hotel itself — it reflects a site that was never finished to the standard the property now wants to compete at. Closing these six gaps is what turns platinhotel.com into a site guests trust enough to book directly, instead of bouncing to an OTA.

Direction
Design Direction — Referencing Ritz-Carlton & Mövenpick
We reviewed both reference brands live to extract the specific patterns that read as "premium" rather than describe them in the abstract. Both share one structural idea worth borrowing directly: a full-bleed photographic hero with the reservation widget floating on top of it — never a separate, disconnected booking page.
Ritz-Carlton pattern
Full-bleed dark hero imagery, a refined serif wordmark, a destination/date search bar floating directly on the hero, minimal top navigation, generous whitespace throughout.
Mövenpick pattern
Warm lifestyle photography as the hero, gold/cream accents on white, a "Find a hotel" search card overlapping the image, clean sans-serif navigation, visible language switcher.

Your Own Photography, Elevated
No reshoot required

These are Platin's existing photographs — the redesign doesn't need new imagery to look like a different hotel. Full-bleed crops, consistent color grading and generous whitespace do the work.

Direction Applied to Platin Hotel
  • Homepage hero: full-bleed photo or slow-motion video loop of the property, reservation widget floating on top — not a separate page.
  • Typography pairs a refined display serif for headings with a clean sans-serif for body text, mirroring the restraint both reference brands use.
  • Platin's existing gold logo mark is carried through as the accent color rather than replaced — the redesign elevates the brand, it doesn't reinvent it.
  • Language switcher placed top-right in the nav, exactly where both Ritz-Carlton and Mövenpick surface it.
  • Suite categories presented as an editorial gallery grid rather than plain text blocks, with a comparison-friendly layout.

Fast-Track Timeline

A 2-week delivery is achievable because the scope excludes a payment gateway and live PMS availability sync — the two items that typically extend a hotel website build. It requires Platin Hotel to hand over all content, rates and photography on Day 1, and to turn around design and content feedback within 24 hours at each checkpoint.
