1 · Overview
Executive Summary
Code-ox Technologies is pleased to submit this proposal for the implementation of a new Odoo 19 ERP system for your automotive dealership operations, covering vehicle sales, spare parts, workshop and after-sales service, finance, HR, marketing, call centre operations, and business intelligence reporting.

This proposal has been developed based on our review of your detailed requirements document covering 165 requirements across ten functional areas of your business. We understand that your current Odoo system is not meeting your operational needs, and that a decision has been made to proceed with a new implementation rather than continued development on the existing platform, for the reasons set out below.

Our recommended approach is to build a new Odoo 19 database from the ground up, engineered around your actual operational requirements, with historical data carried forward from your existing system. This gives you a clean, modern, fully-owned platform without dependency on your current implementation partner.

2 · Situation
Current Situation & Recommended Approach
2.1   Current Situation
Your dealership currently operates on Odoo 17, implemented and hosted by your existing service provider. Based on our discussions, the source code for the custom modules built on your existing system is retained by that provider and is not available to us, and the system itself is hosted on infrastructure controlled by them.

This means that a direct migration of your existing customisations to Odoo 19 is not practically achievable — we cannot migrate or extend code we do not have access to, and we cannot assume reliable access to the underlying server for a like-for-like upgrade.
2.2   Recommended Approach: Fresh Odoo 19 Implementation
Given the above, we recommend building your new system as a fresh Odoo 19 database, developed independently by Code-ox Technologies according to your operational requirements. This approach gives you full ownership of the new platform, a clean and modern architecture with no inherited technical debt, and complete independence from your current provider going forward.

Historical data — customers, vehicles, sales history, financial records and other operational data — will be extracted from your existing system and migrated into the new Odoo 19 database as part of this engagement.
Note — Inventory Data Migration

Certain inventory-related data (such as vehicle stock records, reservations, and VIN-level history) carry known limitations for a clean migration given the structure of the current system. The precise scope and method of inventory data migration will be discussed and finalised in detail once this proposal is approved, as part of the requirement gathering phase.
