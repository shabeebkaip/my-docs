Section 10 · Discovery — Business

Shape the story before we write a word.

Your answers shape website copy, target keywords, and LinkedIn voice. Short honest answers beat perfect long ones.

A structured questionnaire is coming

The questions on this slide and the next are here so you see the shape of what we need. We send the full questionnaire (Google Form / shared doc) within 24 hours — answer at your own pace, share with team members who know specific areas.

About Jethur
  • What does Jethur sell, in one sentence — for a stranger?
  • Who is the typical buyer? (role · company size · industry)
  • What problem are they hiring you to solve?
  • Average deal size and sales cycle length?
  • What part of the business is growing fastest right now?
Positioning
  • Why do customers pick Jethur over a competitor — in their words?
  • What's the one thing competitors say that's wrong about you?
  • What category should Google associate Jethur with first?
Geography & Verticals
  • Which countries / regions are primary targets for SEO?
  • Which verticals should we lead with? (banking, oil & gas, healthcare…)
  • Any geos where you do not want to be visible?
Goals · Next 6 Months
  • What does "the engagement worked" look like at Month 6?
  • Optimising for inbound leads, brand authority, or both?
  • Regulatory events / launches to time content around?
  • Topics off-limits (regulatory, legal, brand-safety)?

Audience & Competition
11
Section 11 · Discovery — Audience

Who we talk to · who we beat.

SEO and LinkedIn are zero-sum games against specific competitors. Naming them lets us reverse-engineer keywords, content gaps, and outreach segments.

Buyer Personas
  • Who is the economic buyer? (signs the cheque)
  • Who is the champion? (uses it, recommends it)
  • What job titles are your "ideal first reply" on LinkedIn?
  • What content do they read? (publications, podcasts, newsletters)
  • What objections come up most often in sales calls?
Content Authority
  • Who on the team is publishable as a thought leader?
  • Any white papers · decks · talks we can repurpose?
  • Are case studies cleared for public use, or under NDA?
Competitors
  • Top 3–5 competitors you want to outrank on Google.
  • Which of them are doing LinkedIn well — what should we learn?
  • Any competitor we should never look or sound like?
Sales Handoff
  • When a LinkedIn reply lands, who picks it up at Jethur?
  • What CRM / inbox should leads route to?
  • What SLA can sales commit to for first reply?
  • What qualifies as a "good lead" for you?
