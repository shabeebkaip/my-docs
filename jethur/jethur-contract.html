01  ·  Preamble
Recitals & Parties

This Website Development Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of 1 July 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between:

Code-OX Technologies LLP, a limited liability partnership duly registered and incorporated under the laws of India, having its registered office at UAQ Business Centre, Westhill, Kozhikode, Kerala — 673005, India (hereinafter referred to as the "Service Provider" or "Code-OX"); and

Jethur Innovations Private Limited, a private limited company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN: U62020KL2025PTC093959), having its registered office at Suite 544, 2nd Floor, Cochin Special Economic Zone, Ernakulam, Kerala — 682037, India (hereinafter referred to as the "Client" or "Jethur").

Code-OX and Jethur are each referred to herein individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

WHEREAS, Jethur operates a Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) platform and is desirous of revamping its corporate website jethur.com by migrating from its current WordPress infrastructure to a secure, enterprise-grade Next.js 16 web application with a custom content management system; and

WHEREAS, Code-OX is engaged in the business of software design, web application development and AI-driven digital products, and has presented a Website Revamp Proposal which has been accepted by Jethur as the basis for this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out herein, the Parties agree as follows.

02  ·  Definitions
Defined Terms

"Services" means the website design, development, custom CMS build, deployment and related professional services described in Schedule A of this Agreement.

"Deliverables" means the Figma design files, source code, custom CMS, configuration scripts, documentation and the final deployed website produced by Code-OX in the performance of the Services.

"Project Fee" means the fixed, one-time fee of USD 1,600 (United States Dollars One Thousand Six Hundred only) payable by Jethur to Code-OX as set out in Schedule B.

"Support Period" means the three (3) calendar months immediately following the Go-Live Date, during which Code-OX shall provide complimentary minor bug fix and minor change support as defined in Section 7.

2.5   Go-Live Date

"Go-Live Date" means the date on which the revamped jethur.com is deployed to production and made publicly accessible at the live domain following Jethur's final written acceptance.

2.6   Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either Party to the other, including business plans, source code, customer data, pricing, trade secrets, technical architecture and any information marked or reasonably understood to be confidential.

2.7   Intellectual Property

"Intellectual Property" or "IP" means all designs, source code, written content, graphic assets, configurations and any other original works of authorship produced under this Agreement.

03  ·  Schedule A
Scope of Services

Code-OX shall design, build and deliver the revamped jethur.com website, comprising the following workstreams:

3.1   Discovery & Strategy
  • Stakeholder alignment session to confirm sitemap, messaging hierarchy and content priorities
  • Audit of the existing WordPress site — content inventory and SEO baseline
  • Information architecture — revised sitemap and page hierarchy
  • Redirect plan for all existing URLs to preserve SEO equity
3.2   UX & Visual Design (Figma)
  • Design system — typography, colour palette, spacing and component library
  • Low-fidelity wireframes for all key page templates
  • High-fidelity, production-ready UI designs across Desktop, Tablet and Mobile breakpoints
  • Interactive Figma prototype for stakeholder review and sign-off
  • Accessibility review aligned with WCAG 2.1 AA standards
3.3   Frontend Development — Next.js 16
  • Build on Next.js 16 (App Router), TypeScript and Tailwind CSS
  • Reusable, component-driven architecture with Framer Motion page transitions
  • Full on-page SEO implementation — meta tags, structured data (JSON-LD), Open Graph, sitemap.xml, robots.txt
  • Security headers — CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy
  • Contact & demo-request forms with spam protection (reCAPTCHA v3 or Cloudflare Turnstile)
  • Analytics integration — GA4 or a privacy-first equivalent
  • Performance optimisation targeting Lighthouse 90+ across primary pages
3.4   Custom CMS (Built within Next.js)
  • Custom admin panel built directly within the Next.js application — no external CMS subscription
  • Blog/Insights module — rich-text editor, images, tags, publishing workflow
  • Custom block-based page builder for the Jethur marketing team
  • Management modules for team profiles, case studies, regulatory landing pages and newsroom
  • User roles & permissions (Editor, Publisher, Admin)
  • Media asset management with image upload and optimisation
  • RTL-ready architecture to support future Arabic language content
3.5   QA, Launch & Handover
  • Cross-browser QA — Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge
  • Responsive QA across Desktop, Tablet and Mobile breakpoints
  • Migration of existing approved content from WordPress into the custom CMS
  • One (1) recorded CMS training session of up to two (2) hours for the Jethur content team
  • DNS cutover and deployment to production at the Go-Live Date
  • Technical handover documentation covering codebase, CMS and deployment
Reference: The detailed scope reflects the Website Revamp Proposal previously shared by Code-OX (ref: jethur/website.html, April 2026), which is incorporated into this Agreement by reference. In the event of conflict between the proposal and this Agreement, this Agreement shall prevail.

04  ·  Schedule A (cont.)
Exclusions & Client Responsibilities
4.1   Adjacent Services (Available Separately)

To keep the project sharp and the launch tight, this Agreement focuses on the website build itself. The following adjacent services sit outside the current scope, but are areas Code-OX is happy to pick up under a separate engagement whenever Jethur is ready:

  • Content authoring — Jethur provides all written copy for each page, using the content template Code-OX shares at kick-off
  • Original photography, custom illustration and stock-asset licensing
  • Translation and Arabic content population — the site architecture is RTL-ready; population is handled as a separate phase
  • Backend product development for the Jethur GRC platform, customer portals or product dashboards
  • SEO services beyond the on-page implementation already included in Section 3.3
  • Paid-media campaigns (Google, LinkedIn, Meta) and email-marketing platform set-up
  • LinkedIn organic marketing
  • Ongoing content management and marketing retainers post-launch
  • Recurring infrastructure — hosting, domain renewal, CDN/WAF and third-party SaaS subscriptions — paid directly by Jethur. Code-OX provides recommendations and assists with initial set-up as part of deployment
4.2   Client Responsibilities

To keep the project moving and protect the launch date for both sides, Jethur agrees to:

  • Appoint a single Project Owner as the authorised point of contact for all decisions and approvals
  • Provide all brand assets — logo files, brand guidelines, fonts and colour codes — within five (5) business days of project kick-off
  • Provide all page content in final form before the corresponding development sprint begins, using the content template supplied by Code-OX
  • Provide written feedback on each Figma design milestone within five (5) business days of delivery
  • Provide written feedback on each staged development build within five (5) business days of delivery
  • Provide access to the existing WordPress site for the purpose of content review and migration
  • Provide DNS access, hosting credentials and any third-party platform access required for deployment
  • Attend the scheduled kick-off, design review and handover sessions
Working together on the timeline: This project moves at the speed of decisions. The response windows in Section 4.2 are designed to keep both sides accountable to the launch date. Where a review cycle runs longer than planned, the affected milestone simply shifts by an equivalent number of working days — no penalty, no fee adjustment, just a small calendar move.

06  ·  Term
Duration of the Engagement
6.1   Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date of 1 July 2026 and shall continue until the later of (i) the expiry of the Support Period defined in Section 7 or (ii) the completion of all obligations of the Parties under this Agreement.

6.2   No Automatic Renewal

This Agreement is a one-time, fixed-scope engagement and does not automatically renew. Any subsequent engagement — including extended support, maintenance retainers, SEO services or LinkedIn marketing — shall be the subject of a separate written agreement between the Parties.

07  ·  Post-Launch Support
Three-Month Complimentary Support
7.1   Support Period

Code-OX shall provide Jethur with three (3) calendar months of complimentary post-launch support commencing on the Go-Live Date (the "Support Period").

7.2   Scope of Complimentary Support

The Support Period covers, at no additional cost to Jethur:

  • Minor bug fixes — correction of any defect, error or non-conformity in the website or custom CMS attributable to Code-OX's build
  • Minor visual or copy adjustments — small text edits, image swaps, padding/spacing tweaks and similar non-structural changes
  • Minor functional refinements — small adjustments to existing components, form configurations and CMS settings
  • Browser and device compatibility fixes on the supported browser matrix set out in Section 3.5
  • Reasonable email support from a Code-OX representative during Indian Standard Time business hours
7.3   Outside the Scope of Complimentary Support

The Support Period does not include, and any of the following shall be quoted and billed separately upon mutual written agreement:

  • New pages, new templates or new CMS modules not part of the original Schedule A
  • New integrations with third-party systems, APIs or platforms
  • Major redesigns, restructuring of the information architecture or new branding
  • Performance issues caused by Jethur's hosting environment, third-party plugins or content uploaded by Jethur
  • Issues introduced by changes made to the codebase or CMS by Jethur or any third party after handover
  • Content authoring, ongoing content management, SEO services or marketing campaigns
7.4   Response Times

During the Support Period, Code-OX shall use commercially reasonable efforts to acknowledge in-scope support requests within one (1) business day and to resolve minor issues within five (5) business days, dependent on complexity. Critical defects that take the live site offline shall be prioritised.

7.5   Extended Support

Upon expiry of the Support Period, continued support may be procured under a separate Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) at rates to be agreed between the Parties in writing.

08  ·  Termination
Termination Rights & Effect
8.1   Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party: (i) commits a material breach of this Agreement and fails to remedy such breach within fourteen (14) days of receiving written notice; (ii) becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation or ceases to carry on business; or (iii) engages in fraudulent, unlawful or grossly negligent conduct.

8.2   Termination for Convenience

Jethur may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' written notice to Code-OX. In such event, Jethur shall pay (a) all amounts owing for Services rendered up to the date of termination, and (b) a reasonable charge for work in progress at the date of termination, in proportion to the milestone progress achieved.

8.3   Effect of Termination

Upon termination of this Agreement for any reason: (i) Jethur shall pay all outstanding undisputed amounts within fifteen (15) days; (ii) Code-OX shall deliver all Deliverables produced and paid for up to the termination date; (iii) each Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information of the other Party; and (iv) Sections 9, 10, 11 and 13 of this Agreement shall survive termination.

Ownership, Licensing & Changes

Jethur retains all ownership rights to its existing intellectual property, including its name, trademarks, logos, product information, brand assets and any content supplied to Code-OX for the project. Code-OX is granted a non-exclusive licence to use such assets solely for the purpose of performing the Services under this Agreement.

Upon Code-OX's receipt of the full Project Fee, all Deliverables produced specifically for Jethur under this Agreement — including the Figma design files, the Next.js source code, the custom CMS code and all written and graphic materials produced by Code-OX for the website — shall transfer to and become the exclusive property of Jethur. Code-OX retains ownership of all pre-existing tools, frameworks, libraries, internal templates and proprietary methodologies used in delivering the Services. Where such pre-existing materials are incorporated into the Deliverables, Jethur receives a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use those incorporated elements for the operation of the jethur.com website.

9.4   Portfolio Rights

Code-OX may reference Jethur as a client and display screenshots of the completed jethur.com website in its portfolio, case studies and marketing materials, provided that no Confidential Information of Jethur is disclosed and the brand is represented accurately. Code-OX will share any case-study or portfolio content with Jethur for review prior to public publication.

9.5   Evolving the Scope

Good projects evolve. If Jethur would like to add, expand or rethink something during the build, the Parties will document the change in a short Change Request that sets out the additional effort, fee and any timeline impact — so that nothing surprises either side. Work on the new item begins as soon as Jethur countersigns the Change Request, usually within the same working week.

12.1   Independent Contractor

Code-OX is and shall remain an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment relationship, agency, joint venture or partnership between the Parties. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. If a dispute ever arises, the Parties commit to thirty (30) days of good-faith discussion at senior level first. Anything that remains unresolved goes to a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated in Kochi (Ernakulam), Kerala, conducted in English. 12.3   Non-Solicitation

For the duration of the engagement and twelve (12) months afterwards, neither Party will directly solicit the other Party's team members who worked on this project, except by mutual written agreement. Ordinary public job postings are unaffected and do not breach this clause.

Neither Party shall be in breach of this Agreement or liable for any delay in performing, or failure to perform, any of its obligations if such delay or failure results from circumstances beyond its reasonable control — including acts of God, natural disaster, pandemic, war, civil unrest, government restrictions, cyber-attack or sustained failure of essential third-party services. 12.7   Assignment
