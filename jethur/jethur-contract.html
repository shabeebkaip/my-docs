03  ·  Schedule A
Website Services — Scope of Work

Code-OX shall design, build and deliver the revamped jethur.com website as a one-time, fixed-scope build, comprising the following workstreams:

3.1   Discovery & Strategy
  • Stakeholder alignment session to confirm sitemap, messaging hierarchy and content priorities
  • Audit of the existing WordPress site — content inventory and SEO baseline
  • Information architecture — revised sitemap and page hierarchy
  • Redirect plan for all existing URLs to preserve SEO equity
3.2   UX & Visual Design (Figma)
  • Design system — typography, colour palette, spacing and component library
  • Low-fidelity wireframes for all key page templates
  • High-fidelity, production-ready UI designs across Desktop, Tablet and Mobile breakpoints
  • Interactive Figma prototype for stakeholder review and sign-off
  • Accessibility review aligned with WCAG 2.1 AA standards
3.3   Frontend Development — Next.js 16
  • Build on Next.js 16 (App Router), TypeScript and Tailwind CSS
  • Reusable, component-driven architecture with Framer Motion page transitions
  • Full on-page SEO implementation — meta tags, structured data (JSON-LD), Open Graph, sitemap.xml, robots.txt
  • Security headers — CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy
  • Contact & demo-request forms with spam protection (reCAPTCHA v3 or Cloudflare Turnstile)
  • Analytics integration — GA4 or a privacy-first equivalent
  • Performance optimisation targeting Lighthouse 90+ across primary pages
3.4   Custom CMS (Built within Next.js)
  • Custom admin panel built directly within the Next.js application — no external CMS subscription
  • Blog/Insights module — rich-text editor, images, tags, publishing workflow
  • Custom block-based page builder for the Jethur marketing team
  • Management modules for team profiles, case studies, regulatory landing pages and newsroom
  • User roles & permissions (Editor, Publisher, Admin)
  • Media asset management with image upload and optimisation
  • RTL-ready architecture to support future Arabic language content
3.5   QA, Launch & Handover
  • Cross-browser QA — Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge
  • Responsive QA across Desktop, Tablet and Mobile breakpoints
  • Migration of existing approved content from WordPress into the custom CMS
  • One (1) recorded CMS training session of up to two (2) hours for the Jethur content team
  • DNS cutover and deployment to production at the Go-Live Date
  • Technical handover documentation covering codebase, CMS and deployment

04  ·  Schedule B
SEO Services — Scope of Work

Code-OX shall provide search-engine-optimisation services to grow Jethur's organic visibility and qualified-traffic share for GRC-related queries in Jethur's target geographies. The SEO Services consist of an integration phase delivered alongside the website revamp, and an ongoing monthly retainer thereafter.

4.1   Phase 01 — Revamp Integration (One-Time, Included in Retainer)

Delivered concurrently with Schedule A, included within the monthly retainer at no separate setup cost:

  • Keyword research and topic-cluster mapping — target intent, search volume, difficulty and commercial value
  • Information-architecture and URL-structure recommendations for SEO equity
  • On-page implementation across all new pages — page titles, meta descriptions, H-hierarchy, internal-link patterns
  • Structured-data and schema implementation (Organization, Product, Article, FAQ, BreadcrumbList JSON-LD)
  • Technical SEO setup — sitemap.xml, robots.txt, canonical tags, Open Graph and Twitter Card metadata
  • Redirect plan from the legacy WordPress URLs to preserve SEO equity and avoid 404s
  • Content briefs for cornerstone pages — About, Solutions, product/category pages, key blog pillars
  • Baseline analytics setup — Google Search Console, GA4 configuration, conversion event tracking
4.2   Phase 02 — Ongoing Monthly Retainer

Beginning the calendar month of the Effective Date and continuing each month thereafter, Code-OX shall deliver:

  • Blog publishing cadence — minimum two (2) optimised long-form articles per month targeting high-intent GRC keywords (compliance frameworks, regulatory updates, GRC tooling)
  • Content expansion and refresh — ongoing optimisation of existing pages (About, Solutions, product pages) for keyword relevance and conversion
  • Technical SEO health monitoring — crawl errors, schema validity, Core Web Vitals, broken links, redirect hygiene
  • Keyword rank tracking — weekly position monitoring across the target keyword set
  • Backlink monitoring and light outreach — quality of incoming links, disavow of toxic links, targeted outreach for relevant placements
  • Monthly performance report — rankings, organic traffic, impressions, click-through rate, key wins, issues, and the prioritised plan for the following month
4.3   SEO Service Levels
  • Blog publishing — 2 articles per month, drafts shared 5 business days before publication for Jethur approval
  • Monthly report — delivered by the 5th business day of the following calendar month
  • Critical technical issues (indexing loss, robots.txt misconfiguration, security flags) — acknowledged within 1 business day and remediated within 5 business days
  • Reasonable email support during Indian Standard Time business hours
Outcome expectations: SEO is a compounding asset, not a switch. Code-OX commits to the deliverables and cadence above. Ranking and traffic outcomes depend on content resonance, market conditions and Google's algorithm changes and are not guaranteed.

LinkedIn Services — Scope of Work

Code-OX shall provide ongoing LinkedIn marketing services to grow Jethur's brand presence and pipeline among GRC decision-makers (CISOs, compliance officers, GRC analysts) in Jethur's target markets. 5.1   Service C-01 — Page Management & Content (USD 300/month)

  • 12 posts per month across six content pillars — regulatory commentary, customer stories, product education, thought leadership, team culture, and industry news
  • Mixed formats — single-image posts, carousels, short video scripts (where Jethur provides recording resources), and text-only thought-leadership posts
  • Community engagement — daily comment responses, reactions to industry conversations, and engagement on Jethur's targeted ICP profiles
  • Monthly content calendar — pre-approved 30 days in advance, adjusted in-month for regulatory news and product milestones
  • Monthly performance report — follower growth, post engagement, profile views, top-performing content
5.2   Service C-02 — Outreach & Lead Generation (USD 350/month)

ICP-targeted outbound prospecting on LinkedIn:

  • Prospect identification — Sales-Navigator-style filters using Jethur's defined ICP (seniority, industry, geography, company size)
  • 150+ personalised connection requests per month with custom intro notes tailored to the recipient's role and industry
  • Multi-touch follow-up sequences for accepted connections — soft introduction, value share, qualification, and hand-off to Jethur's sales team where qualified
  • Qualified-lead tracking — leads tagged, scored and handed off to Jethur via shared tracker
  • Weekly outreach report — connection acceptance rate, reply rate, qualified-lead count and pipeline summary
5.3   LinkedIn Service Levels
  • Monthly content calendar shared with Jethur by the 25th of the preceding month for approval
  • Outreach sequences begin within 5 business days of the Effective Date once ICP definition is signed off
  • Monthly performance report delivered by the 5th business day of the following calendar month
  • Reasonable email support during Indian Standard Time business hours
Outcome expectations: 12 posts published and 150+ outreach actions per month are committed deliverables. Follower growth, lead volume and pipeline outcomes compound over time and depend on audience resonance, content quality, and market conditions — and are not guaranteed.

9.1   Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate this Agreement (or any individual Service stream) immediately upon written notice if the other Party: (i) commits a material breach and fails to remedy such breach within fourteen (14) days of written notice; (ii) becomes insolvent, enters liquidation or ceases to carry on business; or (iii) engages in fraudulent, unlawful or grossly negligent conduct.

9.4   Termination After Initial Term

After the Initial Term, either Party may terminate the SEO Services or LinkedIn Services upon thirty (30) days' written notice, with no exit settlement and no further obligation other than payment for Services rendered up to the termination date.

The Support Period covers, at no additional cost to Jethur:

  • Minor functional refinements — small adjustments to existing components, form configurations and CMS settings

10.3   Outside the Scope of Complimentary Support

The Support Period does not include, and any of the following shall be quoted and billed separately upon mutual written agreement:

Governing Law & Miscellaneous
14.1   Independent Contractor

Code-OX is and shall remain an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment relationship, agency, joint venture or partnership between the Parties. Code-OX's personnel are not employees of Jethur and shall not be entitled to any employee benefits from Jethur.

14.2   Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. If a dispute ever arises, the Parties commit to thirty (30) days of good-faith discussion at senior level first. Anything that remains unresolved goes to a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated in Kochi (Ernakulam), Kerala, conducted in English. The courts at Ernakulam retain supervisory jurisdiction.

14.3   Non-Solicitation

For the duration of the engagement and twelve (12) months afterwards, neither Party will directly solicit the other Party's team members who worked on this engagement, except by mutual written agreement. Ordinary public job postings are unaffected and do not breach this clause.

14.4   Force Majeure

Neither Party shall be in breach of this Agreement or liable for any delay in performing, or failure to perform, any of its obligations if such delay or failure results from circumstances beyond its reasonable control — including acts of God, natural disaster, pandemic, war, civil unrest, government restrictions, cyber-attack or sustained failure of essential third-party services. The affected Party shall notify the other promptly and the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to mitigate the impact.

14.5   Entire Agreement & Amendments

This Agreement, together with its Schedules and the Website Revamp Proposal, SEO Retainer Proposal and LinkedIn Marketing Proposal referenced herein, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, representations and understandings, whether written or oral — including any prior version of this Agreement that may have been circulated. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both Parties.

14.6   Notices

Formal notices may be sent by email (with read-receipt or acknowledgement) or by registered post to the addresses on the cover page. Either Party can update its notice address by simply writing to the other.

14.7   Assignment

Neither Party may assign or transfer its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign this Agreement to a successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of substantially all of its assets, upon written notice to the other Party.

14.8   Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is found by a court or arbitrator of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, such provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

14.9   Waiver

No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right or remedy under this Agreement shall constitute a waiver of that or any other right or remedy. A waiver of any breach shall not be construed as a waiver of any subsequent breach.

14.10   Counterparts & Electronic Signatures

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures and signed PDF counterparts exchanged by email shall be deemed valid and binding under the Information Technology Act, 2000.
