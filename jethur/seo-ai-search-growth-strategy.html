06 · Method
Our Strategy
The plan follows a proven semantic SEO method. Instead of chasing single keywords, we make Jethur the most complete, best-structured source on one subject: GRC software for Saudi Arabia. The work runs across six connected tracks.
1
Build Topical Authority — the Topical Map

Topical authority means being the site that covers a subject most completely. We treat the site like a library on one subject, with a strong front desk and deep, well-linked shelves. The central entity is GRC Software, with Jethur as the brand and the frameworks and modules as connected entities. The map has two halves:

  • Core pages (the money pages): the Saudi framework pages (NCA ECC, SAMA CSF, PDPL), the product module pages, comparison pages, and a local service page.
  • Outer pages (the teaching blogs): cluster articles that answer buyer questions and link back to the core pages, passing authority to them.
2
Content Strategy — Answer-First, Built for AI

Every important page is written so both a human and an AI can use it. The method is simple and repeatable:

  • Open with the direct answer in the first lines, then the context, then a short promise of what follows.
  • Under each question-style heading, place a 35–45 word answer block that makes sense on its own.
  • Use specific nouns and real numbers, not vague claims — AI quotes specifics, not adjectives.
  • Add a proof layer: a real Jethur result, a named client where possible, the RegTech100 recognition.
3
On-Page & Technical Fixes
  • Repair the 66 broken CSS and JS files and clear unused code to lift mobile speed.
  • Give every page one clear, solution-framed H1. Remove duplicate and missing H1s.
  • Add canonical tags where missing and prepare hreflang for the Arabic rollout.
  • Thicken thin pages with useful content and link orphaned pages back into the site.

Local, AI & Off-Page Tracks
4
Google Business Profile & Local SEO
  • Create and fully complete a Google Business Profile for the Riyadh office.
  • Publish a local service page and keep the business name, address and phone identical everywhere.
  • Build 15–20 local and industry citations to prove Jethur is a real, trusted Saudi business.
5
AI Search & SRO

Selection Rate Optimisation means making content so clear and specific that AI tools choose Jethur when they build an answer. We add labelled FAQ schema, direct-answer blocks, and comparison tables that AI can lift, and we publish an llm.txt file that tells AI systems who Jethur is and how to cite it.

6
Off-Page & Authority
  • Grow referring domains steadily from relevant, trusted sources, not volume for its own sake.
Why Six Tracks, Not One

Technical fixes without content don't rank. Content without authority doesn't get seen. Authority without AI-readiness doesn't get quoted. Each track removes one ceiling on the others — that is why they run in parallel, not in sequence.
