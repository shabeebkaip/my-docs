Confidential & Legally Binding Add-on Services Agreement
01  ·  Recitals
Background & relationship to the master agreement

This Add-on Services Agreement (this "Add-on" or "Schedule E") is entered into as of 5 September 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Code-OX Technologies LLP ("Code-OX") and Jethur ("Jethur" or the "Client").

Code-OX and Jethur are parties to a Digital Partnership Agreement covering website, SEO and LinkedIn services (the "Master Agreement"). This Add-on adds a further LinkedIn service stream to that engagement and is incorporated into, and governed by, the Master Agreement. All defined terms, confidentiality, intellectual-property, payment, liability and governing-law provisions of the Master Agreement apply to this Add-on. Where this Add-on is silent, the Master Agreement controls; where it conflicts on the specific scope and fee below, this Add-on controls.

Jethur has requested that Code-OX manage the personal LinkedIn profile of Hazem Galal, VP of Sales (the "Managed Profile") as a dedicated sales-generation engine, operating in parallel with the company page and the authority profile already managed under the Master Agreement. Jethur is the contracting and paying party; Hazem Galal is the individual whose profile is managed and a designated point of contact.

02  ·  Services
Scope of the LinkedIn Sales Engine
2.1   Content management

Code-OX shall plan, produce and publish three to four (3–4) posts per week on the Managed Profile, in Hazem's voice, across a mix of: industry / thought-leadership content (GRC and cybersecurity), deal wins and success stories, expertise-driven posts, and personal storytelling.

2.2   Outbound prospecting

Code-OX shall conduct outbound activity directly from the Managed Profile — connection requests and follow-up messaging to the agreed target audience — at a target of no fewer than ten (10) connection requests per day. Outbound and content run in parallel from the Effective Date, with no separate phase-in.

2.3   Inbound & response handling

Code-OX shall handle initial responses to inbound messages and engagement arising from the Managed Profile's content and outreach (and inbound routed from the company page or authority profile). Once a conversation progresses into a substantive sales discussion, Code-OX shall hand it off to Hazem / Jethur's sales team, who retain sole responsibility for qualification beyond the initial exchange, negotiation and closing.

2.4   Targeting (ICP)

Code-OX shall reuse the Ideal Customer Profile and targeting criteria already established for the authority profile under the Master Agreement. No additional audience research is included in this Add-on.

2.5   Reporting

Code-OX shall provide a monthly performance report covering messages sent, qualified conversations and content engagement. Reporting is on activity and engagement volume. The parties acknowledge that this Add-on carries no committed numeric outcome targets — Code-OX does not guarantee any specific number of meetings, opportunities or closed deals.

03  ·  Access & Client Responsibilities
What Jethur provides
3.1   Account credentials

Jethur shall procure and provide Code-OX with direct login access to the Managed Profile (Hazem Galal's LinkedIn account), on the same access model already used for the authority profile under the Master Agreement. Code-OX shall treat such credentials as Confidential Information under the Master Agreement, use them solely to perform the Services, and restrict access to personnel who need it.

3.2   LinkedIn Sales Navigator

Effective outbound requires LinkedIn Sales Navigator on the Managed Profile. Its status is to be confirmed with Hazem at onboarding. If required and not already active, the Sales Navigator licence is a Client-side cost of approximately USD 80 per month, paid by Jethur directly, and is not included in the fee in Section 4.

3.3   Cooperation

Jethur shall ensure Hazem and Jethur's sales team are reasonably available to receive handed-off conversations and to provide input on content and deal context where needed.

05  ·  Term & Termination
Duration

This Add-on commences on the Effective Date and runs on a month-to-month rolling basis, co-terminous with the LinkedIn Services of the Master Agreement. Either party may discontinue this Add-on, without affecting the remainder of the Master Agreement, on thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Termination of the Master Agreement automatically terminates this Add-on. All other termination rights and effects in the Master Agreement apply.
