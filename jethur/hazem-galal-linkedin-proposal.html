01  ·  Context
Two profiles, two jobs

Code-OX already manages Jethur's company LinkedIn page and the personal profile of Hatem, which we run as an authority and thought-leadership builder — it earns credibility and reach, but it is deliberately not used for direct selling.

Jethur has now asked Code-OX to also manage the personal LinkedIn profile of Hazem Galal, VP of Sales. The intent is clear and, in our experience, exactly right: give the sales motion its own dedicated engine so the authority profile stays clean while a second profile does the direct, measurable outreach. Both run in parallel and reinforce each other.

The split in one line. Hatem's profile builds the reputation. Hazem's profile turns that reputation into conversations — and routes every inbound enquiry, from either presence, to the person who can close it.

03  ·  What Code-OX Delivers
Scope of work
3.1   Content — 3 to 4 posts per week

We plan, write and publish a consistent content mix on Hazem's profile, in his voice, designed to build authority and open sales conversations:

  • Industry & thought-leadership — GRC and cybersecurity topics that position Hazem as a knowledgeable voice
  • Deal wins & success stories — credibility through outcomes
  • Expertise-driven posts — practical, informed takes that a buyer respects
  • Personal storytelling — the human layer that makes a sales profile approachable
3.2   Outbound prospecting — from Hazem's account

Operating on the same model as Hatem's account, Code-OX runs outbound directly from Hazem's LinkedIn profile: connection requests and follow-up messaging to the agreed ICPs, building a steady, compounding pipeline of relevant contacts.

3.3   Inbound & response handling

Code-OX handles the initial responses to inbound messages and engagement — from both content and outreach — keeping every conversation warm and moving. Once a conversation moves into a genuine sales discussion, we hand it off to Hazem / Jethur's sales team to close. We generate and qualify the conversation; your team owns the deal.

3.4   Monthly reporting

A performance report each month covering messages sent and qualified conversations, alongside content engagement. Reporting is on activity and engagement volume — this is a consistent, compounding outreach engine, not a guaranteed-outcome promise, so we do not commit to a fixed number of meetings or deals.

Why activity-based reporting is the honest model. On LinkedIn, deals are closed by your sales team, not by a post. What we can control and be measured on is the volume and quality of qualified conversations we put in front of you — consistently, every week.
