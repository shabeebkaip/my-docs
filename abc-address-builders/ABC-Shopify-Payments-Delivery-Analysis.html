Delivery — the clean part

RedBox on Shopify, end to end

Unlike payments, RedBox is a straightforward fit — its own free, official Shopify app does the two things that matter for ABC's Shop track.

Integrated delivery partner
At checkout

Locker picker

Customers pick from 2,000+ RedBox lockers across Saudi Arabia inside Shopify checkout — no redirect, no separate portal.

In the backend

Shipment sync

Shipments are created, edited and cancelled straight from Shopify as orders arrive — no manual re-entry into a RedBox dashboard.

For the customer

Speed & tracking

Same/next-day within-city, 2–3 days elsewhere, 24/7 self-service pickup and live tracking.

Worth flagging for a furniture catalogue

Lockers are sized for parcels — an olive-wood bowl fits, a dining table does not. So the realistic setup is two tiers, mirroring the Shop / Custom Works split: RedBox for small, parcel-sized items, plus a separate freight / white-glove arrangement for large furniture (own fleet or a logistics partner), priced outside the automated checkout. Not a build gap — a real constraint of parcel-locker delivery on any platform.

Filling Shopify's KSA gaps

The add-ons that complete the store

The real cost of the Shopify decision: three areas a local platform handles natively need a deliberate add-on here — each scoped by CodeOx.

Localisation

Bilingual / RTL (AR + EN)

Shopify Markets + the free Translate & Adapt app handle regions and copy. Full right-to-left mirroring for Arabic needs custom theme CSS (themes are LTR-first) — front-end work CodeOx builds into the theme.

Compliance

ZATCA e-invoicing (Phase 2)

Not native to Shopify. Needs a compliance app or custom build issuing a UBL 2.1 XML invoice with QR + cryptographic stamp per order, cleared via ZATCA's API. Set aside a distinct budget line (~2–4 weeks).

Optional

BNPL — Tabby / Tamara

Buy-now-pay-later added if ABC wants it — a proven sales lift for higher-value furniture.

Logistics

Large-furniture delivery

RedBox covers parcels; big furniture needs a manually-coordinated freight / white-glove process layered alongside the automated RedBox flow.

How we'd sequence it

Implementation phases

  1. Discovery & accounts — confirm Shopify plan tier, set up the payment merchant account (Mada needs bank-level KYC, with its own lead time), open a RedBox business account.
  2. Store & theme setup — Online Store 2.0 theme or custom build, with the bilingual/RTL work scoped as its own task.
  3. Catalogue build — Shop collections & products with images, descriptions and parameter metafields; Custom Works gallery pages.
  4. App integrations — payment gateway, RedBox Parcel Locker, Translate & Adapt, and the ZATCA e-invoicing solution.
  5. Custom Works flow — enquiry form, WhatsApp button, lead routing to ABC's team.
  6. Compliance & testing — test payment transactions, a real RedBox test shipment, a sample ZATCA invoice end to end.
  7. Training & handover — ABC's team walked through orders, shipments and invoice records.
  8. Launch & hypercare — go-live, then close support before standard maintenance.
