Overview

The engagement, in plain terms

Code-Ox Technologies will act as SkillVera's software support and development partner for its mobile application. Following our discussion, SkillVera has selected the Essential plan — 30 hours per month: a fixed monthly retainer that keeps the app stable, current and steadily improving, handled by a dedicated team rather than ad-hoc freelancers.

This proposal confirms exactly what those 30 hours cover, the monthly commercials, and the terms — so SkillVera knows precisely what it is committing to before signing.
How the retainer works

The 30 hours are a monthly capacity block your team draws on for maintenance and small development work. You get a dedicated point of contact and a priority channel; we work through a shared list, ship in reviewable releases, and report at month-end on hours used and work delivered. It is a rolling arrangement — scale hours up or down as the product's needs change.

Scope

What the 30 hours cover

The Essential plan keeps the app running well and delivers a steady stream of small improvements each month. Here is exactly what is in scope — and what sits outside it.

Maintenance & stability

  • Bug fixes — reported issues triaged and resolved
  • App-store updates — iOS & Android releases kept current
  • Monitoring — the app watched for crashes and errors
  • Content & data cleanup as needed

Improvements & response

  • A few small improvements shipped each month
  • The quickest priority (P1) fixes handled first
  • Fast issue response on a priority channel
  • Monthly report — hours used, work shipped, what's queued
Included in the retainer

Team & ways of working

  • One team for both maintenance and development
  • A dedicated point of contact + priority channel
  • Proactive suggestions — we flag improvements, not just await tickets
  • Work in version control, staged reviewable releases

Quality & flexibility

  • QA and documentation included in delivered work
  • Up to 20% of unused hours roll over one month
  • Extra hours beyond the block billed at the tier rate ($25/hr)
  • Scale up to a higher plan any time as needs grow
What's not included

Essential is light-upkeep capacity — it is not enough runway for large new features (e.g. a full analytics dashboard) at pace, and it is "keep-the-lights-on" rather than active roadmap delivery. Third-party costs (hosting/servers, app-store fees, SMS/email/API usage, paid plugins) are billed at cost or paid directly. Large one-off builds are scoped and quoted separately and can run alongside the retainer.

Terms

Commitment, billing & assumptions

The simple ground rules that keep the engagement clear for both sides.
  • Billing — monthly in advance, in USD; SAR shown at an indicative 3.75. Invoices issued at the start of each month.
  • Commitment — a minimum 3-month term, then continues month-to-month with 30 days' notice to change, pause or stop.
  • Capacity, not fixed scope — the 30 hours are a monthly capacity retainer, not a guaranteed fixed deliverable. Work is prioritised jointly each month.
  • Roll-over — up to 20% of unused hours carry into the following month; they do not accumulate indefinitely.
  • Extra hours — work beyond the monthly block is billed at the tier's hourly rate ($25/hr on Essential), agreed in advance.
  • Handover sprint — a short codebase takeover at the start ensures a clean, documented transfer of the existing app code.
  • Third-party costs — hosting/servers, app-store fees, SMS/email/API usage and paid plugins are billed at cost or paid by SkillVera directly.
  • Large builds — major one-off features or a full redesign to production are scoped and quoted separately, and can run alongside the retainer.
  • Taxes — all prices are exclusive of applicable taxes.
  • Validity — this proposal is valid for 30 days from the date of issuance.
Scaling with you

Essential is the right entry point to keep the app healthy and moving. As the product grows toward its milestones, SkillVera can step up to Momentum (60 hrs) or Scale (120 hrs) at a lower hourly rate — same team, same standard, just more monthly capacity.
