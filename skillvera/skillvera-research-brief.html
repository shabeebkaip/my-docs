Executive Summary

What SkillVera is, in three parts

Read this before the detail sections below.

Company

Saudi ed-tech / sports-tech company in Riyadh building smart training, talent-development, and facility-management solutions aligned to Vision 2030. B2B/B2G first — sells to schools, sports facilities, government, healthcare and corporate clients, not directly to consumers.

Product

Five-product suite (Talent Development, XR/AI Training, Event App, Training Centers, SkillVera Rent). Only one is a live mobile app today — "Skv Activities" — an activity discovery & booking app tied to the Event App / SkillVera Rent line.

Stage

Early / pre-launch. App has 10+ Android installs, zero reviews on either store, empty demo data, a dead product-demo link on the marketing site, and unencrypted data collection flagged by Google Play. This reads as an MVP that shipped but never got a maintenance/QA pass.

Findings

Issues observed during this pass

Not a formal QA audit — surface-level findings from public site/store review only. Useful as talking points, not a committed bug list.

Security / Compliance

Collected user data is unencrypted

Google Play's Data Safety section explicitly states data isn't encrypted, while the app collects financial and health data linked to identity. Worth a real security review given PDPL obligations in KSA.

Broken flow

"SkillVera Rent" demo link is dead

The Rent product's "Demo" CTA on skillvera.sa opens a Hostinger-hosted subdomain that returns an error page — a live, linked-to broken experience on the public marketing site.

Store metadata

App Store language mismatch

App Store lists "English" as the only supported language, but the actual app UI is Arabic RTL throughout. Store listing likely needs correcting or localizing.

Store metadata

Category likely wrong

Listed under "Entertainment" on both stores; content (sports/education activity booking) fits Health & Fitness or Lifestyle better for discoverability.

Content

Site polish gaps

CEO headshot is a placeholder icon on the About page; footer copyright reads "© Skillbera 2025" (misspelled + stale year); no visible privacy policy content was reachable during this pass.

Adoption

Pre-launch traction

10+ installs on Android, no App Store install count shown, zero reviews anywhere — this is very early. Frame the engagement as "stabilize & grow" rather than "fix a mature product."

Next Step

Discovery questionnaire (draft, for client)

Send ahead of or use live in the discovery call. Goal: turn our outside-in guesses into their actual product truth before we scope anything.

Product & roadmap

  1. Of the five products on your site (Talent Development, XR/AI Training, Event App, Training Centers, SkillVera Rent) — which are live software today vs. service offerings vs. planned?Confirms what actually needs dev support.
  2. Is "Skv Activities" the same product as "Event App" / "SkillVera Rent" on the site, or separate apps?Site copy suggests overlap; store listing name doesn't match site branding.
  3. What's the current install/user base and is there a target for the next 3–6 months?
  4. What features are already planned or in progress that we should know about before suggesting anything new?

Technical & access

  1. What's the app built with (native Swift/Kotlin, Flutter, React Native, other)?Same UI/UX across both stores suggests one cross-platform codebase — confirm.
  2. Backend stack, hosting provider, and database — is the "SkillVera Rent" demo (currently hosted on Hostinger) representative of production infra?
  3. Do you have an existing in-house or outsourced dev team, and what would our role be alongside them?
  4. Do you have CI/CD, staging environment, and crash/analytics monitoring in place today?
  5. Who currently owns the App Store Connect / Google Play Console accounts, and can we get collaborator access?
  6. Is payment/financial data actually being processed today, and if so through which PSP?Play Store declares financial info collection but we saw no payment UI in the screenshots reviewed.

Support scope & commercials

  1. Is this ongoing retainer-style maintenance, a fixed feature backlog, or both?
  2. Expected SLA — bug response time, release cadence?
  3. Budget range and whether it's monthly retainer vs. per-feature quoting.
  4. Any compliance requirements we should design around (PDPL, sector-specific rules for education/healthcare clients)?
Bottom line: SkillVera is a credible, Vision-2030-positioned Saudi ed/sports-tech company with real institutional ambitions, but the one shipped product is an early-stage MVP with visible security, content, and store-hygiene gaps.
