09How it fits Abjad

The system, drawn.

Abjad owns the subscription. Moyasar owns the bank rails. Clean separation.

Note 01

No raw card data in Abjad

Card is tokenized in Moyasar's iframe. PCI scope stays at SAQ-A — the lightest tier.

Note 02

Webhooks drive state

Subscription status set by webhooks. Reconciliation cron catches any missed deliveries.

Note 03

Entitlements live in Abjad, not Moyasar. The gateway stays replaceable.

12Risks · held

No integration is risk-free. These are managed.

Each risk has a known shape and a known mitigation.

R.01Low

PCI compliance scope

Abjad never touches raw cards. Moyasar's iframe keeps us at SAQ-A — lightest tier.

R.02Medium

Webhook delivery failure

Idempotent handlers + 15-min reconciliation cron polling Moyasar for missed events.

R.03Low

Gateway downtime

Existing access continues (entitlements in Abjad DB). Failed renewals retry on schedule.

R.04Medium

Chargebacks & disputes

Clear ToS refund policy. Automated suspension on chargeback. One-click finance flow.

R.05Low

Vendor lock-in

Subscription state in Abjad DB. Migration = re-tokenize cards once. Bounded effort.

R.06Low

Fraud & abuse

Moyasar 3-D Secure handles most cases. Abjad adds velocity limits per account.
