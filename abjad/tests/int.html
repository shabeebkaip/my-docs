Interview Management (Teacher)

Route /interviews. Invitation shows date, type, link, interviewer, duration. Actions: Accept / Reschedule / Decline. Google Calendar export.

IDPriTest case · steps · expectedStatusNotes
INT-01Invitation appears. After a school schedules an interview, open /interviews. Expect: invite card with date/time, type, meeting link, interviewer name, duration.
INT-02Accept invitation. Click Accept. Expect: status → Accepted; school notified; card reflects confirmed state.
INT-03Reschedule with reason. Choose Reschedule. Expect: a reason/notes input appears; submitting sends the request; status reflects "reschedule requested".
INT-04Decline with reason. Choose Decline. Expect: reason captured; status → Declined; school notified.
INT-05Google Calendar export. Click add-to-calendar. Expect: opens Google Calendar prefilled with correct title, time, and link.
INT-06Join link. Open the meeting link. Expect: navigates to the correct video URL provided by the school.
INT-07Past vs upcoming. View list with mixed dates. Expect: upcoming and past interviews are visually separated / correctly ordered.
INT-08Empty state. Teacher with no interviews. Expect: clean empty state, no errors.
