Job Application & Tracker

Apply from /jobs (ApplyJobModal). Tracker at /applications with 7 tabs: All, Submitted, Shortlisted, Interview, Offer, Hired, Rejected.

IDPriTest case · steps · expectedStatusNotes
APP-01Apply confirmation modal. Click Apply on a job. Expect: a summary modal opens with an optional cover-letter field (max 500 chars) and a Confirm button — it does NOT apply instantly.
APP-02Cover letter char limit. Type past 500 chars. Expect: counter blocks/warns at 500; over-limit cannot submit.
APP-03Confirm apply. Submit the modal. Expect: success confirmation; job shows "Applied"; application appears under Submitted.
APP-04Duplicate apply blocked. Try to apply again to the same job. Expect: disabled / "already applied"; no duplicate created.
APP-05Apply gated by approval. As a non-approved teacher, attempt to apply. Expect: prompted to complete/await profile approval (or blocked appropriately).
APP-06Tracker — 7 tabs present. Open /applications. Expect: exactly All, Submitted, Shortlisted, Interview, Offer, Hired, Rejected; counts per tab.
APP-07Tab filtering. Click each tab. Expect: only applications in that status show; All shows everything.
APP-08Status reflects school action. After a school shortlists/rejects the application, reload. Expect: it moves to the matching tab; status label + timestamp update.
APP-09Withdraw application. Withdraw a Submitted application. Expect: confirmation; status becomes Withdrawn/removed from active; cannot re-withdraw.
APP-10Application detail. Open an application. Expect: job summary, submitted cover letter, reference number, match score visible.
APP-11Empty state. New teacher with no applications. Expect: friendly empty state with a CTA to browse jobs; tab counts all zero.
