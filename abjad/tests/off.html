Offer Management (Teacher)

Offers surface on the applications view. Lifecycle: Sent → Viewed → Accepted / Declined / Negotiation Requested / Expired.

IDPriTest case · steps · expectedStatusNotes
OFF-01Offer notification + view (Sent→Viewed). After a school extends an offer, open it as teacher. Expect: offer details (position, salary, contract, start date, deadline, benefits); status auto-marks Viewed on open.
OFF-02Accept offer. Click Accept. Expect: confirmation; status → Accepted; hiring confirmation shown; school notified.
OFF-03Decline offer. Click Decline (with reason if prompted). Expect: status → Declined; school notified; cannot then accept.
OFF-04Request negotiation / counter. Submit a counter (e.g. different salary). Expect: status → Negotiation Requested; entry added to negotiation history.
OFF-05Expired offer. View an offer past its deadline. Expect: status shows Expired; accept/decline disabled.
OFF-06Negotiation round-trip. After school responds to a counter, view updated offer. Expect: revised terms + full negotiation history visible; teacher can accept/decline the new terms.
OFF-07Contract document (if provided). Where a contract/offer letter is attached, download it. Expect: PDF opens with correct details.
OFF-08Status sync with tracker. After accept, check /applications. Expect: application moves to Hired/Offer tab consistently.
