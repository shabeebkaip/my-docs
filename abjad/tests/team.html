Team & Account Management

Route /school/team. Four roles: Admin, Recruiter, Interviewer, Viewer. Invite/manage members and enforce per-role permissions.

IDPriTest case · steps · expectedStatusNotes
TEAM-01Invite member. As school Admin, invite a member (email, name, role). Expect: invite created; member appears with chosen role.
TEAM-02Change role. Update a member's role. Expect: role changes; permissions update on their next action.
TEAM-03Cannot remove last Admin. Try to demote/remove the only Admin. Expect: blocked with a clear message (at least one Admin required).
TEAM-04Recruiter permissions. Login as Recruiter. Expect: can post jobs / manage candidates & applications; cannot manage team/billing (verify restricted areas).
TEAM-05Interviewer permissions. Login as Interviewer. Expect: can view assigned interviews / submit feedback; cannot post jobs or extend offers.
TEAM-06Viewer permissions. Login as Viewer. Expect: read-only; action buttons hidden/disabled; API rejects write attempts.
TEAM-07Remove member. Remove a non-admin member. Expect: member loses access; their session can no longer act for the school.
