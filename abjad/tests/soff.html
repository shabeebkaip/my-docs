Offer & Hiring (School)

Route /school/offers (and from applications). Extend offer, track status, handle counter-offers, confirm hire.

IDPriTest case · steps · expectedStatusNotes
SOFF-01Extend offer. Open OfferModal; set position, salary, contract, start date, deadline, benefits; send. Expect: offer created (Sent); teacher notified.
SOFF-02Track offer status. View /school/offers. Expect: 6 statuses reflect correctly (Sent/Viewed/Accepted/Declined/Negotiation/Expired).
SOFF-03Receive counter-offer. After teacher requests negotiation, view it. Expect: counter terms + negotiation history log visible.
SOFF-04Respond to counter. Send revised terms or reject the counter. Expect: new offer version recorded; teacher notified; history appended.
SOFF-05Confirm hire. After teacher accepts, confirm hire. Expect: celebration/confirmation modal; application → Hired; candidate marked hired.
SOFF-06Declined offer handling. Teacher declines. Expect: status → Declined visible to school; can extend a new offer to another candidate.
SOFF-07Offer deadline / expiry. Let an offer pass its deadline. Expect: status → Expired; teacher can no longer accept.
SOFF-08Cross-role consistency. After each transition, verify teacher's offer view (OFF cases) matches school's view. Expect: both sides agree on status + terms.
