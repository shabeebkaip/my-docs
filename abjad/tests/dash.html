Teacher Dashboard

Route /dashboard. Overview stats, profile strength meter, activity feed, and the "Recommended for You" top-5 jobs with match badges.

IDPriTest case · steps · expectedStatusNotes
DASH-01Dashboard loads. Login as teacher, open /dashboard. Expect: overview cards (applications, interviews, offers counts), no console errors.
DASH-02Profile strength meter. View strength indicator; complete a missing section. Expect: percentage rises after saving new info.
DASH-03Recommended for You — top 5. On an Approved profile with matching jobs, view the Recommended section. Expect: at most 5 job cards, ordered by match; empty-state copy when no matches.
DASH-04Match badge on recommendations. Inspect each recommended card. Expect: a MatchBadge shows a percentage; "Why this match" chips (subjects/grade/location…) render.
DASH-05Recommendation CTA. Click a recommended job. Expect: opens the job detail / can apply; save toggles work from the card.
DASH-06Activity feed. Perform an action (apply/save), return to dashboard. Expect: recent activity reflects it; timestamps sensible.
