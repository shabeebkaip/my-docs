01 · The Brief

Project overview & our understanding

Benchy has asked us to revamp its existing website, benchy.in, with a proper bespoke design. Today the site presents a broad and capable furniture range — workstations, office tables, chairs, reception tables, height-adjustable desks, storage solutions, conference tables, pantry tables and workpods — but on a design that no longer does that range justice or makes it easy for a buyer to explore.

This is a full, custom website redesign: we design a cohesive, brand-appropriate website tailored to Benchy's products and customers. The value is practical — a visitor can find the right furniture faster, see it presented well, move around the site with confidence on any device, and reach out to enquire without friction. Rather than reskinning the current site, we redesign the key experiences it is built from — the home page, the way products are browsed and viewed, the story and credentials of the business, the content, and the ways a customer gets in touch.

02 · Objectives

What the redesign sets out to achieve

Clearer product discovery
Help visitors move from a broad range to the exact category and product they need, quickly.
Stronger presentation
Show Benchy's furniture with the photography, layout and detail it deserves.
Easier navigation
A consistent, predictable structure across categories, products and content.
Mobile usability
Layouts that read and work well on phones, where much browsing now happens.
Accessible enquiry paths
Make it obvious and simple to get in touch and request information.
A cohesive brand feel
One consistent visual language across every page, from home to contact.

We are focused on design quality and usability. This proposal does not promise increased leads, search rankings or revenue — those depend on many factors beyond design alone.

03 · Approach

A cohesive, bespoke design approach

Rather than fitting Benchy into a generic template, we design a considered system tailored to your brand and your range — so every page feels part of the same whole, and each product is easy to understand and act on.

  • Brand-appropriate visual direction — a look and tone developed around Benchy's furniture, customers and market, agreed with you during discovery.
  • Clear information hierarchy and navigation — a structure that guides a visitor from range, to category, to product, to enquiry.
  • Consistent typography, colours, spacing and components — a single design language applied across every page.
  • Strong furniture photography and product presentation — layouts built to show products, features and specifications clearly.
  • Responsive desktop and mobile layouts — each design considered for large screens and phones.
  • Accessible contrast, readable text and usable form states — designs that are comfortable to read and simple to use.
  • Clear enquiry calls to action — obvious, well-placed ways to get in touch on every relevant page.
  • Shared header and footer, reusable components and templates — consistent global framing, so the whole site stays on-brand and is efficient to extend.
  • Design handoff — final designs delivered with agreed assets and specifications, ready for build.
Decided together, during discovery

We deliberately do not lock in a specific font, colour palette, CMS, framework or development platform before discovery. Those choices are made with you, informed by your brand assets and priorities — not assumed up front.

04 · Scope

What we'll design

The redesign covers the core pages and reusable templates that make up Benchy's website. Several are reusable templates — one bespoke design that every category, product, portfolio item or article then shares — so the whole site stays consistent and easy to extend.

DesignReference / examplesPurpose
1 · Homebenchy.inIntroduce the brand, communicate its value, showcase key furniture categories, and guide visitors toward products and enquiries.
2 · About/about/Present the brand story, values, design approach, craftsmanship and ergonomic focus.
3 · Company Profile/company-profile/Present company capabilities and business credentials using client-approved information — distinct from About. No certifications, capacity, customer names or achievements are invented.
4 · Products Overview/products/A clear gateway into the full product range.
5 · Product Category template/office-tables/ · /chairs/ · /work-station/ · /reception-tables/ · /height-adjustable-table/One reusable category design. Content and imagery vary per category; substantially different layouts would need a separate scope agreement.
6 · Product Detail template/chairs/silla/ · /giro/ · /mode/ · /eco/A reusable layout for imagery, descriptions, features, available specifications and enquiries. Does not assume e-commerce, online payments, live stock or product configuration.
7 · Portfolio Detail template/portfolio/item/workstations/ · /office-tables/ · /workpods/A reusable visual showcase layout. Existing entries appear to represent furniture categories; they are not described as completed customer projects without confirmation.
8 · Blog Listing/blog/A clear, readable article-discovery experience.
9 · Blog Article template/blog/types-of-office-chairs/A reusable article layout with strong reading hierarchy, imagery and relevant next steps.
10 · Contact/contact/Make it easy to enquire, using client-approved contact details and an enquiry-form design.
Optional additional design — Portfolio Listing / Gallery

A portfolio gallery archive exists at /portfolio/category/gallery/, but no dedicated main portfolio page was confirmed. A dedicated portfolio listing that leads visitors to the portfolio detail pages can be added as an optional add-on design, priced separately so the core package stays clear.

07 · Process

How we deliver

  1. Discovery — understand Benchy's brand, products, customers and priorities; agree visual-direction inputs, and gather brand assets and product information.
  2. Structure — agree the site's information architecture and how the designs map across the range, so navigation and hierarchy are settled before visuals.
  3. Visual direction — establish the design language: typography, colour, spacing and components, agreed with you before full page design begins.
  4. Page design — design the core pages and the reusable templates, plus the shared header and footer, for desktop and mobile.
  5. Review — present designs, gather your consolidated feedback, and refine to an agreed point.
  6. Handoff — deliver final designs with agreed assets and specifications, ready for build.

What we'll need from Benchy

A bespoke design is only as strong as the material behind it. To keep the work moving and true to your brand, we'll need brand assets (logo, guidelines, references), good-quality product imagery, accurate specifications, approved copy, client-approved contact details, and a single consolidated set of feedback at each review point.

10 · Exclusions & Assumptions

Separately confirmable or separately quotable

This engagement is the design scope. The following are not included in ₹75,000 and are confirmed or quoted separately:

  • Frontend and backend development
  • CMS setup or changes
  • Population or migration of existing content
  • Mapping existing URLs into the new structure
  • Redirects and technical SEO work
  • Form integrations and enquiry delivery
  • Analytics setup
  • Hosting, domain, maintenance, deployment
  • Copywriting, photography, paid stock, translations

Their treatment is open: they may be retained, adapted to an agreed standard layout, or quoted separately if bespoke designs or extra functionality are needed.
