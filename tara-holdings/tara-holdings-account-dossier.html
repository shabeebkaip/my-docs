Their own words — "forward-thinking design and cutting-edge technologies for communities" — are the hook. One modern platform running the whole group is that ambition, made real.

Opening hook — their web presence is unbuilt. Urban Eats' site (yumurbaneats.com) is down and the group site (taraholding.com) is a "Coming Soon" placeholder. Two fast, visible quick wins: (1) rebuild Urban Eats as a branded online-ordering channel on Odoo eCommerce — own-channel orders carry zero aggregator commission (vs ~20–30% to Jahez/HungerStation/Keeta), so it pays for itself and plugs into the same POS + Aggregator Hub; (2) the Tara Holding corporate site. Lead with something concrete we can fix immediately, then widen to POS, the hub, and the group backbone.
Deck tip: Tara's brand is dark teal-green + gold (elegant, heritage). Co-brand the pitch deck in their colours — instant rapport and shows we already "get" them.

Existing Odoo footprint & the upgrade (immediate project)

They already run Odoo 18 and asked to upgrade to the latest. Two implications:

  • They already trust Odoo — the single strongest argument to move Urban Eats onto the same Odoo stack instead of a separate Foodics silo. One platform, one team, one consolidated group.
  • The upgrade is billable work now — a warm, low-risk first engagement that earns the relationship before the bigger POS/hub build.

Upgrade guidance: Odoo 20 is the next LTS (even-numbered), unveiled 24 Sep 2026, GA in weeks, first stable patch ~Oct–Nov. Recommend planning 18 → 20 (LTS, 3–5-yr horizon) — not 18 → 19 now. 19 is odd-numbered / shorter-support, so 18→19→20 = double the work and cost. Stay on fully-supported 18 until 20's first patch is stable, then jump. Build any new POS / aggregator / website work directly on the target version to avoid re-porting (Odoo 20 breaks OWL-based POS customisations).
