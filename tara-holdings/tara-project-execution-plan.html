Introduction

Purpose, Principles & Key Dates

What this document is

  • The execution plan for Financial Proposal CX-TARA-2026-02 Rev. 1: Part 1 (Odoo 18 → 19 upgrade) and Part 2 (Urban Eats / MYLK implementation).
  • It expands the weekly plan on page 8 of the proposal into every task, owner, target week, input and sign-off from kick-off to the end of support.
  • Every task ID here is loaded into Odoo Project on your own Odoo by end of Week 1, so your team sees status, owner and due date at any time.

How the plan is run

  • Standard Odoo first: standard › configuration › Studio › supported module › custom development, each custom item justified in writing and approved by you.
  • Nothing reaches production untested: upgrade validated on staging and signed off by every department before production is touched.
  • Continuity of data: your POs, journal entries, invoices and history continue in Odoo 19 in the same database. Nothing is re-keyed into a separate system.
  • No surprises: we meet once or twice a week (Sunday steering and a Thursday demo / review) and send a written status report every week.

Environments

  • Production (Odoo.sh): your live six companies. Upgraded only after sign-off; Urban Eats deployed to it in cutover week.
  • Staging, upgrade validation: copy of production upgraded to Odoo 19 for the before / after checks.
  • Staging, build & UAT: where Urban Eats is built, demonstrated each Thursday and accepted in UAT.
  • Repository: owned by Tara Holding; all custom modules, migration scripts and deployment notes.
  • The UAT environment on 1 Dec is built from a fresh copy of production using the same deployment package used at go-live. The method is proven on 1 Dec and again at the Week 10 rehearsal before production.

Change control & defects

  • Change request: logged in the tracker, then Code-ox gives impact on scope, cost and timeline before any work starts. Work starts only on your written approval.
  • Code freeze from Week 10: Critical fixes only until go-live.
  • Critical: blocks selling, payment, orders, ZATCA or a sign-off; handled first.
  • High: key function impaired, workaround exists; handled next.
  • Normal: minor or cosmetic; planned into the next UAT cycle.
  • Every defect has an ID, owner, severity and status visible in the tracker.
