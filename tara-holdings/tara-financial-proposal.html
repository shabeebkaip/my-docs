02Standard Odoo FirstApplies to all workStandard > Configuration > Studio > Supported module > Custom development only when necessary. 03Urban Eats StructureArchitecture document for approvalExisting companies kept as they are. Urban Eats as one company (one CR, one VAT number, one set of accounts). Brand, Branch and Channel reporting dimensions — each brand with its own POS categories, menus, pricelists and website; one warehouse and one POS per outlet. P&L by brand, branch and channel. Includes the one small, documented module that tags POS sales by brand and branch.
04Shared OperationsMain Warehouse receiving all supplier deliveries; Central Kitchen as its own warehouse; outlets resupplied automatically through standard routes and minimum stock rules. Shared finance with "Urban Eats Shared" cost tagging and optional brand allocation. B2B orders fulfilled from Main Warehouse or Central Kitchen. Access rules: central team sees everything, outlet staff see only their own POS and warehouse.
05Product Master, Procurement & InventoryOne generic material per ingredient used in all recipes; supplier products as vendor lines with code, pack size and price, converted automatically on receipt; supplier barcodes on purchased packs only. Product types and an agreed coding scheme. Minimum stock rules at Main Warehouse, Central Kitchen and outlets, with lot and expiry tracking where needed. Production cost from materials (labour and overhead optional); waste recorded with a reason.
06Central Kitchen ManufacturingDemand plus minimum stock > daily production plan > manufacturing orders > automatic ingredient consumption > stock > transfers to outlets. Make-to-stock, make-to-order and no-carry-over items (expiry-dated, leftovers listed for daily disposal). One simple "what to make today" screen for kitchen staff. Outlets use recipe kits, so each POS sale deducts its ingredients from that outlet's stock.

Code-ox Recommendation — Option A, Code-ox Aggregator Hub

Lower total cost of ownership. The SAR 10,000 difference equals roughly one year of UrbanPiper fees for 5–6 branches. After that, Option A carries no recurring fee, while UrbanPiper's cost grows with every new branch and brand.

Full coverage. All seven required platforms — HungerStation, Jahez (incl. Noon), Keeta, The Chefz, ToYou, Ninja and Mrsool — connected through each platform's own API, which Code-ox requests on Tara Holding's behalf.

Ownership and no third-party dependency. The hub's source code is yours, in your repository, with no middleware subscription in the order path.

Upgrade-safe by design. Built as a separate module on Odoo's standard POS, documented and re-validated by Code-ox at each upgrade. UrbanPiper remains a valid standard alternative if minimising custom code matters more to you than recurring cost.
