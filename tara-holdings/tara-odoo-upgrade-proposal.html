Overview
Executive Summary
Code-ox Technologies is pleased to submit this commercial quotation to Tara Holding for two services on your existing Odoo environment — the multi-company instance your internal team already runs for Abdullah Alkadi Company and Urban Eats.

Phase 1 upgrades that environment from Odoo 18 to the current stable release, carrying every customization across safely. Phase 2 moves Urban Eats' point of sale off Foodics and onto Odoo, and consolidates every delivery platform — HungerStation, Keeta, Noon, Jahez and Mrsool — into a single aggregator hub. Both build directly on the Odoo you already own; nothing is rebuilt from scratch.

How the Two Options Work

Option A — Studio-only. Tara Holding purchases the latest compatible versions of all required third-party modules and shares the module details and source code with Code-Ox. On receipt, Code-Ox undertakes the complete Odoo 18 → 19 upgrade, including the necessary module migration and compatibility adjustments.

Option B — Studio + third-party. Code-Ox evaluates all installed third-party modules and apps, discusses the approach with your team, and handles them on Odoo 19 — priced on evaluation.

Recommendation — MCP Integration

MCP currently runs on the MuK IT community modules (muk_mcp, muk_mcp_access). For Odoo 19 we recommend sourcing MCP through Much Consulting, a top-tier Odoo Gold Partner — a partner-supported, maintained integration rather than a community dependency. Licence billed by the vendor.
