Thirteen weeks from 15 October to 14 January. The upgrade runs first: staging in the first two weeks, production on 6–7 November. Urban Eats builds through November, with delivery platforms and settlement running longest because they depend on platform approvals. Foodics data runs alongside the whole project until the archive is complete. Testing starts in November, UAT and training run through December, and cutover leads into go-live on 1 January.
