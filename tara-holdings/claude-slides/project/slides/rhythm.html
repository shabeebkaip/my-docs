Our rhythm: a status report every Thursday from the Project Manager to the Project Owner, a live tracker in Odoo Project that everyone can see, and Helpdesk for UAT issues and support tickets. Steering reviews with the Co-founder, Engagement Lead, Project Manager, Owner and Sponsor are on 29 October, 12 and 26 November, and 10 and 24 December. The signed SRS is the agreed scope: any change is approved in writing with its effect on cost and dates, and code freeze starts on 24 December.
