Support runs from go-live until 30 June 2027 through Helpdesk. Critical issues, meaning POS, payments, delivery platforms, order sync or ZATCA down, get a response within one hour and a fix or workaround within four hours, seven days a week. High: four-hour response, fix within one business day. Normal: response within one business day, fix within three. Standard hours are Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 to 18:00 Riyadh time. New features, branches and integrations are quoted separately.
