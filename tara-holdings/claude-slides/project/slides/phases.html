Phase 1, 15 October to 12 November: agreement and access, requirement workshops, the staging upgrade, finance sign-off on 29 October and the production upgrade on 6–7 November. Phase 2, November: foundation, central kitchen, POS, delivery platforms, payments, ZATCA simulation, data loads and internal testing. Phase 3, 1–24 December: two UAT cycles, role training, cutover rehearsal and UAT acceptance. Phase 4: production deployment, Go / No-Go on 29 December, cutover night on 31 December and go-live on 1 January. Phase 5: weekend cover, two weeks of hypercare, handover on 14 January, then support until 30 June 2027.
