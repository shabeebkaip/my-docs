The plan at a glance

Work starts on Thursday 15 October 2026. The UAT environment opens on Tuesday 1 December and every outlet goes live on Odoo POS on Friday 1 January 2027, with six months of support after that.

Part 1: Odoo 18 to 19 upgrade

All six existing companies move to Odoo 19 in the same database, with history intact. Everything is validated on staging first, and finance signs off each company before production is touched.

Part 2: Urban Eats on Odoo 19

Structure and finance, central kitchen, POS and Kitchen Display, delivery platforms, payments, ZATCA, WhatsApp, loyalty, and the exit from Foodics.

Scope, prices and terms are as in proposal CX-TARA-2026-02 Rev. 1. This plan does not change them.

Standard Odoo first

Standard, then configuration, Studio, a supported module, and custom development last. Each custom item is justified in writing and approved before it is built.

Staging first, production last

Every department validates on staging, and finance signs off each company before production is touched.

Dated and linked

Every task has a start, an end, an owner and the task or input it depends on, tracked live in Odoo Project.

The 1 December and 1 January dates hold if the agreement is signed and kick-off is held by 15 October, inputs arrive on the dates below, UAT feedback is timely, and delivery-platform approvals come through.
