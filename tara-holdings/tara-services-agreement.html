1Background

Tara Holding issued Purchase Order TH P00008 on 14 October 2026, accepting Code-ox Technologies' Financial Proposal CX-TARA-2026-02 Rev. 1 for the Odoo 18 to 19 upgrade and the Urban Eats / MYLK implementation with Option B (Odoo's standard UrbanPiper connector). This Agreement records how the Parties will work together. It is read with the Purchase Order, the signed Software Requirements Specification (SRS), the Proposal and the Expected Project Plan of 13 October 2026. If they differ, this Agreement applies first, then the Purchase Order.

2Scope

Code-ox Technologies will upgrade Tara Holding's six companies to Odoo 19 and implement Urban Eats / MYLK on Odoo, as described in the Proposal and the signed SRS. Two weeks of hypercare, the final Foodics archive, the source code and documentation handover, and six months of support until 30 June 2027 are included in the price.

The price is fixed. Invoices reference the Purchase Order and are paid within 15 days. Odoo and UrbanPiper subscriptions, WhatsApp, payment-provider and terminal fees are paid by Tara Holding directly to each provider. Any third-party module licence is arranged by Code-ox Technologies with Tara Holding's prior approval and invoiced at cost.

5Working together

Both Parties will work in good faith and share information openly. Code-ox Technologies delivers to the Project Plan and reports progress weekly. Tara Holding names a Project Owner and provides access, data and decisions within 5 business days of a request.

6Requirements, changes and acceptance

The SRS signed by Tara Holding is the agreed scope. Any change is agreed in writing, with its effect on cost and dates, before work starts, and is recorded as a new SRS revision. Each milestone is accepted against the agreed criteria once its Critical and High issues are resolved or accepted by Tara Holding.

7Support and future work

Support runs from go-live until 30 June 2027 under the SLA in the Proposal. Future work is charged at the agreed rates, fixed for 24 months: new outlet SAR 2,000, new brand SAR 600, new delivery platform SAR 1,000, and change requests SAR 135 per hour. After the support period, an optional annual maintenance contract is available at 20% of the project value per year, with 10% off the first year.

8Ownership and confidentiality

Tara Holding owns all custom code, its data and the project documentation, kept in its own repository. Both Parties keep each other's information confidential, and Tara Holding's data is used only for this project, in line with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law.

9General

Each Party's liability is limited to the fees paid under this Agreement, excluding indirect losses. Neither Party is responsible for delays caused by events beyond its control. Either Party may end this Agreement with 30 days' written notice, and completed work is then paid for and handed over. Questions are first settled amicably between the Parties' management. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and is signed in two originals, one for each Party.
