Background

  1. Tara Holding runs Odoo 18 for six companies and has chosen to upgrade to Odoo 19 and to bring Urban Eats / MYLK onto Odoo in place of Foodics.
  2. Code-ox Technologies, an Odoo Silver Partner, submitted Financial Proposal CX-TARA-2026-02 Rev. 1 dated 10 October 2026 (the "Proposal").
  3. Tara Holding issued Purchase Order TH P00008 on 14 October 2026 for the full Odoo 18 to 19 upgrade and the Urban Eats / MYLK implementation using Option B, Odoo's standard UrbanPiper connector, and both Parties now record their agreement in this document.

1Scope of the services

Code-ox Technologies will deliver the following, as described in detail in the Proposal and, once signed, in the SRS.

Odoo 18 to 19 upgrade

  • Written upgrade assessment and a module-by-module register.
  • Full backup, complete upgrade on a staging copy, data checks and functional testing.
  • Studio customisations and the third-party modules migrated; a module without an Odoo 19 version is migrated or a replacement is proposed. Nothing is removed without Tara Holding's written approval.
  • Financial and operational validation for each of the six companies, signed off by Tara Holding before production is upgraded outside working hours.

Urban Eats / MYLK on Odoo (Option B)

  • Urban Eats as one company with Brand, Branch and Channel reporting from standard Odoo.
  • Shared operations, product master, procurement, inventory and central kitchen manufacturing.
  • Restaurant POS and Kitchen Display for each branch and brand.
  • Delivery platforms through Odoo's standard UrbanPiper connector, configured by Code-ox.
  • Settlement reporting, payments (Paymob, Tabby, Tamara and POS terminals), ZATCA Phase 2, WhatsApp, loyalty and promotions.
  • Foodics migration, UAT, training and go-live.

Included after go-live, within the contract price

Two weeks of hypercare, the final Foodics archive with index and data dictionary, a go-live report, the complete source code and documentation handed over to Tara Holding's repository (deployment, migration, integration and configuration documents), a walkthrough with Tara Holding's developer, and six months of support under the SLA until 30 June 2027.

All custom code is kept in separate, upgrade-safe, documented modules, and standard Odoo features are used first wherever they meet the need.

4Working together

Both Parties will work together in good faith, share information openly and raise anything that could affect the plan as early as possible.

Code-ox Technologies will:

  • Deliver the services with qualified, Odoo-certified staff, following the Project Plan.
  • Keep Tara Holding informed through a weekly status report and the live project tracker.
  • Share design documents and the SRS for review and approval before building.
  • Request delivery-platform access and onboarding on Tara Holding's behalf and configure each platform once it is available.
  • Hand over the source code, scripts and documentation to Tara Holding's repository.

Tara Holding will:

  • Name a Project Owner and key users for each area.
  • Provide access, data and decisions within 5 business days of a request, as listed in the Project Plan.
  • Review and sign the SRS, and give timely feedback during UAT.
  • Hold the merchant accounts and subscriptions needed for the delivery platforms, UrbanPiper, payments and WhatsApp.
  • Make the outlet devices and network available for testing and go-live.

5Requirements and changes

All requirements are brought together in one Software Requirements Specification (SRS), issued by Code-ox Technologies and signed by Tara Holding's Project Owner before the build starts. The signed SRS is the shared reference for what will be delivered.

Either Party may propose a change at any time. Each change is described in writing and measured against the signed SRS, with its effect on scope, cost and dates, and work on it begins once Tara Holding approves it in writing. Each approved change is recorded in the SRS as a new revision, so the SRS always shows the agreed scope.

6Acceptance

  • Upgrade: The before and after validation is signed off by Tara Holding for each of the six companies, and the agreed business flows pass on staging, before production is upgraded.
  • UAT: The agreed UAT scenarios pass on staging with no open Critical or High issues.
  • Go-live: Production is live on Odoo 19, POS is selling, ZATCA invoices are accepted and orders are flowing from the connected platforms, with any remaining Normal issues listed and scheduled.

Each milestone is accepted in line with the Project Plan and these criteria. Critical and High issues that apply to a milestone are resolved, or formally accepted by Tara Holding, before that milestone is accepted. Tara Holding shares its feedback within 5 business days of each milestone, and production go-live confirms acceptance of that milestone.

10Confidentiality and data

Both Parties keep each other's business information confidential, during this Agreement and afterwards, and use it only for this project. Code-ox Technologies handles Tara Holding's data in line with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, uses it only to deliver the services, and Tara Holding can review or withdraw access at any time.

11Liability

Each Party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid under it, and neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential losses.

12Duration and ending the Agreement

This Agreement starts on the date it is signed and continues until the end of the included support period on 30 June 2027. Either Party may end it with 30 days' written notice. In that case, work completed up to that date is paid for and handed over to Tara Holding, together with its source code and documentation.

13Events beyond control

Neither Party is responsible for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control. The affected Party informs the other promptly, and both agree revised dates together.

14Resolving questions together

If a question or difference of view arises, the two project contacts first discuss it and agree a way forward. If they need support, it is referred to the senior management of both Parties, who aim to settle it amicably within 15 business days. If it still cannot be settled, it is referred to the competent courts of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
