Urban Eats today

Three frictions holding Urban Eats back.

Foodics is a silo

It does POS well, but it stops there — no connection to a group ERP, hardware locked to iPad, and cost that climbs with every branch and every connector you add.

Delivery is chaos

A separate tablet or portal for every aggregator — HungerStation, Keeta, Noon, Jahez, Marsool, ToYou — with manual re-keying, menu and price drift, and items sold that are already out of stock.

The storefront is dark

yumurbaneats.com is currently offline, so every order still routes through an aggregator — paying 20–30% commission, with no owned channel to fall back on.
